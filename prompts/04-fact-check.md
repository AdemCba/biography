# Step 4: fact check

Check every factual claim in the article against the cited source, by opening the source.

For each claim output one line: `OK`, `WRONG` (with the correct value and source), `UNSUPPORTED`
(source does not say it) or `OUTDATED` (source is older than the claim needs). Then list claims with no
citation at all. Pay special attention to: dates and ages, club/company names and years, ownership and
money, awards (name, year, category), anything about private life.

Do not rewrite the article. The editor or step 5 fixes it.
