People searched for Diego Moreira around the AC Milan–Inter derby, after an interview in which the winger was asked whether he would celebrate a goal against Inter with a breakdance. His answer was no. He said he did not want to risk an injury, and he had not danced for a long time [s:goal-moreira-interview]. The interview is the reason his name is trending, but his move to Milan is the bigger story.

## How he joined Milan

AC Milan announced the signing on 19 August 2026. The club said Moreira would wear the number 22 shirt and that his contract runs until 30 June 2031 [s:acmilan-moreira-statement]. The club's statement welcomed him with a short message: "AC Milan warmly welcomes Diego and wishes him every success, both individually and with the team, in the Rossoneri shirt" [s:acmilan-moreira-statement].

The reported fee was €50m, with another €16m in add-ons, and the deal was a five-year contract [s:bein-moreira-transfer]. beIN Sports also reported that Moreira had been in advanced talks with RB Leipzig before choosing Milan [s:bein-moreira-transfer].

## His career before Milan

Moreira is a 22-year-old Belgian winger. He moved to Strasbourg in 2024 from Chelsea, which is a partner club of the French side [s:bein-moreira-transfer]. In 76 appearances for Strasbourg he scored 7 goals and provided 15 assists. Over the last two Ligue 1 seasons he had 13 assists, joint fourth in the league, and he led Strasbourg in chances created (66) and dribbles completed (93) [s:bein-moreira-transfer].

He has also been on the international stage. beIN Sports says he has one World Cup appearance, coming off the bench in Belgium's round-of-32 win over Senegal [s:bein-moreira-transfer].

## The name and the number

In the Goal interview, Moreira explained his first name. His mother chose it because she loved it, and Diego Maradona was part of the reason. "It was her favourite name, also because of Diego Maradona," he said [s:goal-moreira-interview]. He also said that he left home at 15 to pursue football, a decision his family found hard [s:goal-moreira-interview].

He chose number 22 over number 7. He hopes to honour the legacy of Kaká, who wore the number at Milan [s:goal-moreira-interview]. On his idols, he named Cristiano Ronaldo: "To me, he is football; he's my favourite player" [s:goal-moreira-interview]. Goal reports that he had recovered from injury in time for the derby, which he described as one of the most important matches in Europe [s:goal-moreira-interview].

## Why it matters now

Moreira is a new name in Milan's attack, and the derby he is asked about in the interview is, in his words, one of the most important matches in Europe [s:goal-moreira-interview]. His early impact on the team will show whether the reported €50m fee looks like good business.

## What to watch next

Watch how often Moreira starts for Milan in the weeks after the derby, and whether he keeps the scoring and assist numbers that he had at Strasbourg. Watch also whether he stays fit, because Goal reports he had only just recovered from an injury [s:goal-moreira-interview].
