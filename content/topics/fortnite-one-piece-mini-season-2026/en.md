People are searching for Fortnite and One Piece today because the crossover goes live on November 1, 2026, the day Chapter 7 Season 4 ends [s:frvr-treasure-hunters]. Epic has packaged the collaboration as a pirate-themed mini-season called Treasure Hunters, and the name was announced in a short video captioned "The greatest adventure of all! 11.1." [s:frvr-treasure-hunters]

## What Epic has confirmed

Epic revealed the collaboration on Saturday, October 24, 2026. A cinematic teaser was played inside the game at 2 p.m. ET. It showed the Going Merry sailing out of fog and ended on the Fortnite and One Piece logos. No skins appeared in it [s:frvr-treasure-hunters]. The official Fortnite account posted three straw hat emojis that same day [s:games-gg-one-piece].

A larger reveal was scheduled for October 25 at 12 p.m. PT, and FRVR reported that it was expected to cover the Battle Pass and the full collaboration lineup [s:frvr-treasure-hunters]. The Battle Bus gets a special One Piece design [s:frvr-treasure-hunters].

## What is still unknown

Epic has not said whether One Piece content arrives on the first day or later in the mini-season [s:games-gg-one-piece] [s:frvr-treasure-hunters]. The mini-season runs until December 1, 2026, when Chapter 8 begins [s:games-gg-one-piece].

No official character list exists. The reports rank the leaks by confidence. Monkey D. Luffy comes from a datamined file that one outlet rates as high confidence. Roronoa Zoro, Nami, Usopp and Sanji come from a leaker report rated medium [s:games-gg-one-piece]. Nico Robin appeared only as a skin suggestion in a player survey [s:games-gg-one-piece]. A leaker, NotPalo, described two waves of skins starting with Luffy, Zoro, Nami, Usopp and Sanji. FRVR presents that claim as unverified [s:frvr-treasure-hunters].

No price has been published for any One Piece item [s:games-gg-one-piece]. The full item shop lineup has not been published either [s:frvr-treasure-hunters].

## Why it matters now

The timing is tight. The mini-season opens on the first day of November and closes when Chapter 8 starts on December 1, so most players will have roughly a month to collect the items [s:games-gg-one-piece] [s:frvr-treasure-hunters].
## What to watch next

Watch for three things: the item shop listing on November 1, the Battle Pass contents, and whether a second group of One Piece characters is scheduled for later in the season. Each of these would settle the open questions above [s:games-gg-one-piece] [s:frvr-treasure-hunters].
