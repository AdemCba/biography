Elena Rybakina is trending because she has moved closer to the year-end No. 1 ranking. On Sunday 25 October she won the Ningbo Open final 6-2, 6-2 against Leylah Fernandez, defending a title she had also won last year [s:star-rybakina-ningbo] [s:qatar-ningbo-title]. The match lasted one hour and 16 minutes, and Rybakina broke her opponent's serve twice in each set without losing her own [s:star-rybakina-ningbo].

## What happened in Ningbo

Rybakina was the top seed. Her route to the final was a 6-3, 7-5 win over sixth seed Diana Shnaider in the semi-final, while Fernandez beat Marie Bouzkova in the other semi-final, also 6-3, 7-5 [s:star-rybakina-ningbo]. Fernandez was unseeded and ranked 30th, and it was her 10th career final. It was also her second final of the season, a month after she won the title in Singapore [s:star-rybakina-ningbo].

The result gave Rybakina her fourth title of 2026. The others came at the Australian Open, in Stuttgart and at the US Open [s:star-rybakina-ningbo]. That makes her the tour leader for titles this year, with 53 wins [s:wta-rybakina-week]. Ningbo was also her second straight title at the event, a feat the WTA highlighted in its weekly review [s:wta-rybakina-week].

Head-to-head, Rybakina now leads Fernandez 3-2 [s:star-rybakina-ningbo]. Her run started with a first-round win by retirement, as Polina Kudermetova stopped while trailing 5-0 in the first set, and Rybakina then beat qualifier Katarzyna Kawa on the way to the final [s:wta-rybakina-week]. The Ningbo event was part of a busy Asian swing that also included Osaka that same week [s:wta-rybakina-week], and the WTA noted that Rybakina is the first player to win the same tournament in consecutive years [s:wta-rybakina-week].

## What it means for the ranking

Rybakina is already world No. 1 [s:wta-rybakina-week]. Reuters reported that the Ningbo win was a big step towards securing the year-end No. 1 ranking [s:star-rybakina-ningbo]. The WTA said a win in Tokyo this week would seal that year-end spot [s:wta-rybakina-week]. Al Jazeera's preview of the final also framed the Ningbo event as a title defence in a busy stretch of the Asian swing [s:aljazeera-ningbo-preview].

The WTA's review frames her current form as strong ahead of her WTA Finals title defence, which makes the end of the season the next big target [s:wta-rybakina-week].

## What to watch next

The Tokyo tournament is the next test. A win there would secure the year-end No. 1 ranking, which is the question fans are searching for [s:wta-rybakina-week]. Watch her early-round matches, because a run of wins would settle the ranking within a week. A loss would leave the race open for the rest of the swing.
