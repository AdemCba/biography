Fans are searching for Esteban Ocon and Ryo Hirakawa because Haas changed its driver line-up for the Mexico City Grand Prix. The team announced on October 31, 2026 that Hirakawa would take over Ocon's car for the rest of the weekend [s:racefans-ocon-out-2026] [s:motorsportweek-hirakawa-replaces-ocon-2026].

## What happened

Hirakawa, 32, took part in Haas's opening practice session on October 30 [s:racefans-ocon-out-2026]. He is a two-time World Endurance Championship winner and a Le Mans 24 Hours winner with Toyota. After the weekend he goes back to his regular Toyota seat in the WEC [s:racefans-ocon-out-2026].

The team has not given a reason. It confirmed that Ocon is not ill. Team principal Ayao Komatsu declined to explain the decision when he spoke to Sky during the third practice session [s:racefans-ocon-out-2026]. Haas's statement was that "no further statements will be made at this time" [s:racefans-ocon-out-2026].

## The background

Ocon had already been due to leave Haas at the end of the season. Rafael Camara will replace him next year [s:racefans-ocon-out-2026].
Before the change, Ocon had questioned the team's tyre strategy over the radio to his race engineer, Laura Mueller, after the second practice session [s:racefans-ocon-out-2026].
The Ocon-for-Hirakawa move was reported by several outlets. The Race described Ocon as suddenly replaced for the rest of the Mexico weekend [s:the-race-ocon-replaced-2026].

## Why it matters

Hirakawa now drives Haas's car for the rest of the Mexico City weekend, and he returns to his Toyota WEC seat afterwards [s:racefans-ocon-out-2026]. It also leaves open whether Ocon returns for the rest of the season. The sources do not say [s:racefans-ocon-out-2026].

## What to watch next

Watch the Haas statements after the race, the grid and the result from Hirakawa's car, and whether the team names a driver for the next round. Four more rounds follow after Mexico [s:racefans-ocon-out-2026]. Any explanation from Haas would settle the question of whether the move was about performance, contract timing or a dispute over strategy.
