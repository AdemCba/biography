## Why people are searching today

Amazon reports its third-quarter 2026 results on Thursday, October 29, and it will host a conference call at 2:00 p.m. PT, which is 5:00 p.m. ET [s:amazon-q3-2026-release-date]. Two numbers draw most of the attention: the growth rate of Amazon Web Services and the amount Amazon plans to spend on infrastructure.

## What Amazon guided for the quarter

Amazon guided third-quarter net sales to between $197.0 billion and $202.0 billion, growth of 9% to 12% on a year earlier [s:amazon-q2-2026-report]. The company expects a currency headwind of about 80 basis points on that figure [s:pepperstone-q3-2026-preview]. Operating income is guided to between $22.5 billion and $26.5 billion [s:amazon-q2-2026-report]. Consensus sits at about $1.96 in earnings per share, on revenue near $202 billion [s:pepperstone-q3-2026-preview].

The net sales range runs $5.0 billion from bottom to top, and the operating income range runs $4.0 billion. Both ranges are set by Amazon's own guidance, so the reported figures will be measured against them.

## The second quarter as the baseline

In the second quarter, Amazon reported net sales of $200.6 billion, up 20% from a year earlier, and operating income of $27.5 billion, up 43% [s:amazon-q2-2026-report]. That operating income figure was already above the top of the third-quarter guidance range of $26.5 billion [s:amazon-q2-2026-report].

AWS sales reached $42.2 billion in the second quarter, up 37% [s:amazon-q2-2026-report]. AWS segment operating income reached $16.6 billion in the same quarter, compared with $10.2 billion a year earlier [s:amazon-q2-2026-report]. Analysts expect AWS to grow around 35% in the third quarter, a step down from that 37% pace [s:pepperstone-q3-2026-preview].

## Capital spending

Amazon's net capital expenditure in the second quarter was $53.1 billion [s:pepperstone-q3-2026-preview]. The company's 2026 plan is about $220 billion, up from $200 billion [s:pepperstone-q3-2026-preview]. That plan implies average spending of about $61.8 billion a quarter in the second half of the year [s:pepperstone-q3-2026-preview]. Second-half capital spending is therefore set to run well above the second-quarter level of $53.1 billion [s:pepperstone-q3-2026-preview].

## What the options market expects

Options markets imply a move of about 7.4% in either direction around the results [s:pepperstone-q3-2026-preview]. That is the size of reaction the market is pricing in before the call, and it gives a benchmark for how the stock moves once the numbers are out.

## What to watch next

Three things matter most. The first is whether AWS growth holds near 35% or slows further. The second is whether operating income lands inside the $22.5 billion to $26.5 billion range. The third is whether Amazon keeps the 2026 capital spending plan near $220 billion or raises it again.

After the call, the fourth-quarter outlook will be the next figure to check against the numbers in this report.
