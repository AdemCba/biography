## Why his name is in the news

John Goodman's remarks about his weight were reported in October 2026. TV Insider reported that he gave an interview to The New Yorker, published on 18 October 2026, in which he talked about losing a large amount of weight and about quitting alcohol [s:tvinsider-goodman]. The Daily Beast followed with its own report on 19 October 2026, under a headline about a surprise downside to his 200-pound weight loss [s:dailybeast-goodman].

Goodman is 74 [s:dailybeast-goodman], and his name is tied to Roseanne, the sitcom on which he played Dan Conner [s:tvinsider-goodman]. A second reason for interest is Digger, a new film in which Goodman appears alongside Tom Cruise, and which reached UK cinemas on 2 October 2026 [s:wbd-digger]. The two threads have been covered separately, so this piece keeps them apart.

## What Goodman said about his weight

Goodman described a pattern of losing and regaining weight long before his most recent change. He said: "I'd lose sixty pounds in a summer, gain it all back. Seventy pounds, eighty pounds. And that got tiring." [s:tvinsider-goodman] He said he quit drinking at 55 [s:tvinsider-goodman].

He also gave a sense of how heavy he had become. "I was almost four hundred pounds," he said [s:tvinsider-goodman]. The Daily Beast reported that at his heaviest he approached 400 pounds, and that the change "saw him lose roughly half his former body weight." It adds: "By 2023, he had lost 200 pounds" [s:dailybeast-goodman].

On the method, Goodman said: "I had an operation on my stomach." He did not name the procedure or give a date for it, and the Daily Beast report does not add either detail [s:dailybeast-goodman]. Any account that names the operation or its timing goes beyond what he said publicly.

## The downside he described

The headline in both outlets centres on what Goodman called an unexpected cost of the change. He said: "But I also turned anemic and lost a layer of fat under my skin, and I'm cold all the time." [s:tvinsider-goodman] [s:dailybeast-goodman] These are his own words about the effects of his weight change, and neither outlet reported a diagnosis or a treatment plan in addition to them [s:dailybeast-goodman].

This article does not add medical context to those remarks. The reporting presents them as Goodman's experience of losing weight, and that is the scope used here.

## Roseanne and the revival

Goodman played Dan Conner on Roseanne, which aired from 1988 to 1997 [s:tvinsider-goodman]. TV Insider reported that the show returned in 2018 as a revival, was cancelled the following May after Roseanne Barr was fired, and carried on through the spin-off The Conners [s:tvinsider-goodman]. The Daily Beast called him the "Roseanne" star and said he battled alcoholism and managing his weight while starring on the show [s:dailybeast-goodman].

The sources do not connect Goodman's recent weight remarks to his time on the show beyond noting the struggles with alcohol and weight that the Daily Beast described [s:dailybeast-goodman].
