Fans searched for Raphinha after Carlo Ancelotti announced Brazil's squad for the November international break on Thursday 29 October 2026. The Barcelona winger is in the 26-man group, and the call-up has raised one obvious question: is he fit enough to play, given the hamstring problems that have interrupted his season [s:si-brazil-barcelona].

## The squad and the fixtures

Ancelotti named three goalkeepers, nine defenders, seven midfielders and seven forwards [s:si-brazil-barcelona]. Raphinha is one of the forwards, alongside Vinícius Júnior, Estêvão and Endrick [s:si-brazil-barcelona]. Brazil play two friendlies in the November window, both at the National Stadium in Singapore. The first is against Japan on 14 November at 7:15 Brasília time, and the second is against Singapore on 17 November at 7:00 Brasília time [s:oliberal-brasil-japao-singapura] [s:correio-brasil-japao-singapura]. The stadium holds about 55,000 people [s:correio-brasil-japao-singapura].

Ancelotti is using the window for a rebuild. He said: "Right now, it's time to observe, refresh the squad and help young players reach their full potential" [s:si-brazil-barcelona]. Brazil are coming out of a World Cup that did not go to plan, and the coach has left some established names out. Alisson was not picked, and Ancelotti said: "We don't need to test Alisson. We know him very well" [s:si-brazil-barcelona].

## Raphinha's injury history

The main concern is Raphinha's fitness. He had a hamstring injury in September and has started only two Barcelona games since 19 September, according to Sports Illustrated [s:si-brazil-barcelona]. His problems go back further. A hamstring injury in March kept him out for more than a month, including both Champions League quarter-final legs against Atlético Madrid. A hamstring problem at the 2026 World Cup meant he missed Brazil's last three matches there. Another hamstring injury during the September-October break cost him nearly three weeks [s:si-brazil-barcelona].

That record explains why his call-up is a worry for Barcelona. He has come back from three recent Brazil call-ups with an injury, so the club will want to see how he feels after the trip to Asia rather than only whether he is selected.

## The contract and his role

Raphinha is one of the main attackers in Hansi Flick's team, and Barcelona agreed to renew his contract to June 2028 [s:fcb-raphinha-renewal-2028]. beIN Sports has reported that a further extension through 2030 is near [s:beinsports-raphinha-2030], but the club had not confirmed it in the sources checked for this story. The current question, though, is simpler: whether he will be fit for the next league and Champions League matches.

Barcelona are only three points clear at the top of La Liga after losing El Clásico to Real Madrid, and their Champions League game against Manchester City is on 8 December [s:si-brazil-barcelona]. Flick needs Raphinha fit for that run.

## What to watch next

Watch Raphinha's minutes against Japan on 14 November. If he plays and comes back without setbacks, the call-up will look like a sensible return to the national team. If he is eased in or pulled out early, Barcelona will need to plan without him. Watch also whether Ancelotti uses the games to test younger players, as he has said he wants to do.
