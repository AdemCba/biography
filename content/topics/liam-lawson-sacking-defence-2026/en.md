## What Chandhok said

RacingNews365 reports that Chandhok spoke on Sky Sports F1 during first practice in Mexico [s:racingnews365-chandhok-lawson-2026]. His argument depended on a condition. If Lawson loses the seat, Chandhok said, it would be "one of the most unjust sackings in F1" [s:racingnews365-chandhok-lawson-2026].

He went on to question the case for replacing him with Nikola Tsolov: "I just don't see how they can have any data that suggests that Tsolov is a better option than Liam Lawson. But we'll see." [s:racingnews365-chandhok-lawson-2026]

Chandhok also described the weight Lawson carries back from Red Bull, saying he came back "carrying the mental baggage of being sacked from the Red Bull top team" [s:racingnews365-chandhok-lawson-2026]. He said Lawson had done "a fantastic job this season" [s:racingnews365-chandhok-lawson-2026]. The article adds that Lawson "performed admirably for three rounds at Red Bull when Isack Hadjar was injured" [s:racingnews365-chandhok-lawson-2026]. Chandhok said Racing Bulls exists to prepare drivers for the top team, but has its own identity and sponsors [s:racingnews365-chandhok-lawson-2026].

## Background: Red Bull, then Racing Bulls

Lawson was demoted from Red Bull's top team in early 2025 after just two races [s:1news-lawson-red-bull-return-2026]. He then went back to Racing Bulls, where Chandhok says he has done "a fantastic job this season" [s:racingnews365-chandhok-lawson-2026].

In August 2026, 1News reported that Lawson would return to Red Bull Racing for the Dutch Grand Prix at Zandvoort as injury cover for Isack Hadjar, who was almost certain to miss the race with a wrist injury [s:1news-lawson-red-bull-return-2026]. RacingNews365 describes the stint as three rounds [s:racingnews365-chandhok-lawson-2026]. The two reports differ on how long the cover lasted.

## The 2027 seat

Lawson has been asked about his future for weeks. On 2 October he said the decisions "I'm sure will be made in the next few weeks", and added: "I don't feel like there's much more I could have done this year" [s:1news-lawson-decision-2026]. He also said that, for him, the focus was "very much" on the racing [s:1news-lawson-decision-2026].

By 12 October, asked whether his Singapore result would help him keep the seat, he answered "You tell me!" and said that was "obviously normal at this point of the year" [s:formula1-lawson-odds-2026]. As of the 1 November report, Lawson was "yet to be offered a new contract for next year" [s:racingnews365-chandhok-lawson-2026].
