Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27 [s:awarenessdays-black-friday-2026]. SSBCrack gives the same date and says Cyber Monday is November 30 [s:ssbcrack-black-friday-2026]. Best Buy has published a dated calendar for the run-up, and it is the main retailer schedule this article can confirm.

## Best Buy's holiday calendar

Best Buy published its 2026 holiday calendar on its corporate site on October 27, 2026 [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026]. The holiday season officially starts on Friday, October 30, with the return of DoorBOOsters doorbuster deals [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026]. Starting November 1, Best Buy says it is "making it even easier to discover the latest tech" [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026].

Early Black Friday deals and doorbusters start November 6, and new deals drop every Friday [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026]. The main Black Friday doorbusters and deals start November 19 and run for 10 days [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026]. My Best Buy Plus and Total members earn double rewards on purchases made from November 19 to December 6 [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026].

Cyber Week Deals run from December 1 to 6, and the Last-Minute Savings Event runs from December 18 to 24 [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026].

SSBCrack puts Cyber Monday on November 30 [s:ssbcrack-black-friday-2026]. Best Buy's page does not mention Cyber Monday or November 27 at all [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026], so readers should check which calendar a deal belongs to. Eight days separate November 19, when Best Buy's doorbusters start, from Black Friday itself on November 27.

Best Buy's Holiday Price Match Guarantee covers price drops on products Best Buy sells during the holiday season. Limitations apply, and Marketplace products are excluded [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026]. Most purchases made October 30 through December 31 can be returned through January 15, 2027, or through January 31, 2027 for Plus and Total members [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026]. Stores will be closed on Thanksgiving Day [s:bestbuy-holiday-calendar-2026].

## Early clothing deals

The sources in this article do not list specific early clothing discounts for 2026.

SSBCrack, last updated October 18, 2026, says many brands have already started their sales with early promotions beginning in November, and that some launch as early as October. It does not name the brands behind those offers [s:ssbcrack-black-friday-2026]. The same article says official Black Friday discounts will be set on the designated date [s:ssbcrack-black-friday-2026]. It names Net-a-Porter, Boden and MyTheresa as retailers that frequently keep year-round sales sections on their websites. That is not the same as confirming their Black Friday participation [s:ssbcrack-black-friday-2026].

## What is still unconfirmed

The sources we opened give no 2026 percentage discounts. None of them confirms which clothing retailers will join Black Friday in 2026, or when their sales will start. The Black Friday date and the Best Buy windows are the only firm facts in this article.
