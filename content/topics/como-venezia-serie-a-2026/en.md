Como and Venezia meet at the Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia on Sunday 1 November in Serie A matchday 10. The
kick-off is at 12:30 CET, which is 11:30 GMT [s:football-italia-lineups]. Most people searching for this game
want three things: the time, the channel and the team sheet. The answers follow, starting with the broadcast,
because the rights change from one country to the next.

## Kick-off and where to watch

In Italy the match is on DAZN, which needs a subscription [s:corriere-sport-tv]. Sky Italia says it runs in
the DAZN app on Sky Q, Sky Glass and Sky Stream, and on the DAZN 1 channel (channel 214) for Sky satellite
customers who hold a DAZN Full or Family subscription [s:sky-italia-tv].

Outside Italy the picture is less tidy. Goal's US guide lists Paramount+ as the broadcaster and gives a start
time of 06:30, without naming a time zone [s:goal-us-tv]. Goal's Spanish guide says the match is live on DAZN
in Spain, and it lists Disney+ for Mexico and Argentina [s:goal-es-tv]. Goal's US guide also lists DAZN as the
UK broadcaster [s:goal-us-tv]. Check the broadcaster's own schedule before you plan the morning, because the
guides do not agree on every market. The referee is Maria Sole Ferrieri Caputi [s:football-italia-lineups].

## Como: Fàbregas, Paz and the absences

Como are coached by Francesc Fàbregas, with Jean Butez in goal [s:football-italia-lineups]
[s:anecdotarios-venezia-preview]. Nico Paz plays in the middle of the three behind the striker Douvikas, with Da
Cunha and Baturina either side of him [s:football-italia-lineups]. Reading the line-up, that is the player to watch first.

The absentees are a longer list. Virgilio names Trevoh Chalobah, Addai, Mattia Liberali, Assane Diao and Jesús
Rodríguez as unavailable [s:virgilio-preview]. Corriere dello Sport reports Fàbregas on Moïse Kean, who is out
for five weeks, but the coach says Como have not signed a player who is "broken" [s:corriere-sport-tv].

Como's form is good on paper. They have six wins, two draws and one defeat in nine league games
[s:anecdotarios-venezia-preview], and they come off a 3-1 win at Torino [s:lanacion-como-venezia]. Sky Italia
notes that their last three Serie A wins were each by at least two goals, which it describes as a club
record streak, and that they have conceded only one goal in their last five home league games
[s:sky-italia-tv].

Virgilio also gives the type of each injury: Chalobah has a leg problem, Addai a muscle injury, Liberali a calf injury, Diao a thigh injury and Rodríguez a muscle problem [s:virgilio-preview].

## Venezia at the bottom

Venezia are bottom of the table with three points, 17 behind Como [s:virgilio-preview]. Sky Italia puts the
record at one win and eight defeats this season, and says Venezia have not won any of their last 30 away
league games [s:sky-italia-tv]. Their most recent result before this week was a 2-0 defeat to Internazionale
[s:lanacion-como-venezia]. Sky also notes that Venezia have three own goals this season, the joint most among
the five major European leagues. A ninth defeat in their first ten games would make them only the second Serie A
side to suffer that, Sky reports [s:sky-italia-tv].

Since Nicola took over, Venezia have one win and two defeats [s:corriere-sport-tv]. Coach Davide Nicola has
chosen a 3-5-2, with Filip Stanković in goal and Lauberbach and Adams up front
[s:football-italia-lineups] [s:anecdotarios-venezia-preview]. Virgilio lists Šverko, Correia, Franjić and
Adorante as unavailable, with Helgason doubtful [s:virgilio-preview]. Helgason is nevertheless named in the
official XI, which is one of the points where the reports differ [s:football-italia-lineups].

Serie A has 20 clubs and 38 rounds, and the bottom three go down to Serie B [s:lanacion-como-venezia]. Corriere dello Sport describes Venezia as still alone in last place [s:corriere-sport-tv].

## Head to head and what to watch

Como and Venezia have met four times in Serie A. Como won once and drew three, so Venezia have never won a
league meeting with them [s:virgilio-preview] [s:sky-italia-tv]. In 16 home meetings across Serie A and Serie B,
Como won 8, drew 6 and lost 2 [s:virgilio-preview].

Three things will show more than the scoreline. The first is how Paz is used between the lines, because the
Como attack runs through him. The second is whether Venezia can get a result away from home after 30 games
without a win on the road. The third is whether Como keep the one-goal home record that Sky Italia describes,
against a side that has scored three own goals this season.

The official line-ups are already published, so the lineup questions are settled. What remains open is how the
two coaches react when the first goal goes in.
