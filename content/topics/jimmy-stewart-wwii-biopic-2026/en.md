Jimmy opens nationwide on November 6, one week from today. [s:variety-jimmy-kj-apa-november] [s:fathom-jimmy-nationwide-nov6] The film is a biopic about Jimmy Stewart that centres on the years he put his career on hold to serve in the Second World War. [s:fathom-jimmy-early-release-oct27] [s:variety-jimmy-kj-apa-november] KJ Apa, known for the series *Riverdale*, plays Stewart. [s:aol-jimmy-nov6] [s:fathom-jimmy-nationwide-nov6]

## A wide release through Fathom

Burns & Co. Entertainment is partnering with Fathom Entertainment for U.S. theatrical distribution. [s:variety-jimmy-kj-apa-november] [s:fathom-jimmy-nationwide-nov6] Fathom says the film will open nationwide with a traditional theatrical release starting November 6. [s:fathom-jimmy-nationwide-nov6] [s:aol-jimmy-nov6]

Limited theatres across the U.S. will host early screenings from Sunday, November 1, and tickets are on sale now. [s:fathom-jimmy-early-release-oct27] Vet Tix also gives the date as November 6, 2026, in its own notice. [s:vettix-jimmy-give-back] [s:aol-jimmy-nov6]

Fathom will also bring *It's a Wonderful Life* back to theatres starting December 18, for its 80th anniversary. [s:variety-jimmy-kj-apa-november] [s:fathom-jimmy-nationwide-nov6]

## Festival run before the national opening

Fathom says the film had a strong festival run, and critics praised it. [s:fathom-jimmy-early-release-oct27] The Washington West Film Festival billed it as an East Coast premiere, with a screening on October 10 at LOOK Dine-In Cinemas in Reston Town Center, Virginia. [s:wwfilmfest-jimmy-east-coast-premiere] The same festival listed *Jimmy* as its closing night film. [s:wwfilmfest-jimmy-east-coast-premiere]

## What the film covers

The film covers the period from Stewart's Academy Award win for *The Philadelphia Story* to his World War II service as a combat pilot and commander. [s:variety-jimmy-kj-apa-november] [s:aol-jimmy-nov6] Stewart flew 20 missions over Europe, reached the rank of colonel, and returned home emotionally exhausted. [s:variety-jimmy-kj-apa-november] [s:aol-jimmy-nov6] Shortly after, he was offered the role of George Bailey in *It's a Wonderful Life*. [s:variety-jimmy-kj-apa-november]

Fathom describes the story as Stewart putting his career on hold to serve his country. [s:fathom-jimmy-early-release-oct27] Vet Tix's notice is headlined around his World War II service. [s:vettix-jimmy-give-back]
