## Carrick's job and his answer

Carrick took over in January 2026, after Ruben Amorim was sacked, and he was given the full-time job in the summer [s:sky-carrick-support]. Last season he guided United to a top-three finish and back into the Champions League [s:aljazeera-carrick-blame]. That is the record his critics now set against this start.

His response has been direct. He said: "So I totally accept what we're going through at the moment." He also called the Como performance "really unacceptable" and said: "We will get back to it. I fully believe that" [s:aljazeera-carrick-blame]. He pointed to five months of relative success last season as the standard the team needs to return to, and he said the side had drifted from its identity in recent games [s:aljazeera-carrick-blame].

He also described his own position in a way that put the group first. In his words, the job was "holding the umbrella for me to get wet and everyone else to stay dry" [s:aljazeera-carrick-blame]. He rejected reports of a split dressing room, and he sidestepped questions about captain Bruno Fernandes's future [s:aljazeera-carrick-blame].
