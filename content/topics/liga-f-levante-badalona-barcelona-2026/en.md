Levante Badalona and Barça Femení meet on Sunday 1 November in matchday 9 of the Liga F. Three things are driving the searches: the kick-off time, the venue and the name. LaLiga's match page lists the club as FC Badalona Women [s:laliga-jornada-9], and so does the Liga F results page [s:ligaf-jornada-8], but the press still uses Levante Badalona [s:vavel-jornada-9-preview]. It is the same club. Levante UD, the Valencia side, does not appear in the Liga F table for matchday 8 [s:ligaf-jornada-8], so this is not a Levante UD fixture.

## Date, kick-off and venue

The match is on Sunday 1 November at the Nou Municipal de Palamós on the Costa Brava, not in Badalona [s:laliga-jornada-9] [s:fcb-preview-palamos]. The fixture was first expected in Badalona, but the venue was not secured, so it moved to Palamós [s:fcb-preview-palamos]. Most previews give 12:30 as the start, which is Spanish peninsular time [s:fcb-preview-palamos] [s:lasfutbolistas-derbi-preview] [s:vavel-jornada-9-preview]. LaLiga's match page shows 11:30 [s:laliga-jornada-9]. Check the official listing before the game, because the published times differ.

In Spain the broadcasters are DAZN, Teledeporte, 3CAT, RTVE Play and TV3, and the referee is Arantza Gallastegui [s:vavel-jornada-9-preview]. No UK broadcaster is named in the match coverage, so British viewers should check their own listings.

Las Futbolistas calls it a Catalan derby [s:lasfutbolistas-derbi-preview].

## Barcelona top with one defeat

Barcelona are first with 21 points [s:vavel-jornada-9-preview] [s:lasfutbolistas-derbi-preview], and their own preview says the aim is to stay at the top of the table [s:fcb-preview-palamos]. A Liga F table page, labelled "Clasificación Jornada 1", shows 24 points after nine matches [s:ligaf-jornada-8]. The two figures do not agree, and the page does not give a cut-off date, so this piece uses the 21 from the previews.

On 24 October Barça beat Granada 3-0 in matchday 8 [s:ligaf-jornada-8]. VAVEL says their only defeat of the season was a 2-1 loss at Real Sociedad in Zubieta [s:vavel-jornada-9-preview]. In the Women's Champions League they beat HB Køge 8-0 [s:lasfutbolistas-derbi-preview].

Romeu named 21 players. Kika Nazareth is out with a muscle injury to her right thigh, with an estimated four to five weeks of recovery [s:fcb-preview-palamos]. Irene Paredes, Laia Aleixandri and Vicky López are also listed as absent [s:lasfutbolistas-derbi-preview].

## Badalona looking for a first win since matchday 3

Badalona are 14th with 8 points, according to Barça's preview [s:fcb-preview-palamos] and the Liga F table [s:ligaf-jornada-8]. They have not won since matchday 3 and lost their last two games [s:fcb-preview-palamos]. Las Futbolistas counts five matches without a win, with two draws and three defeats [s:lasfutbolistas-derbi-preview].

On 24 October they lost 1-0 at Logroño [s:ligaf-jornada-8]. VAVEL's preview, which gives no dates, lists a 0-0 draw with Real Sociedad, a 0-1 defeat to Granada, a 3-3 draw with Sevilla and a 0-4 defeat to Tenerife [s:vavel-jornada-9-preview]. Las Futbolistas lists Antonia Canales, Berta Pujadas, Izarne Sarasola, Vicky Losada and Lauri Requena as unavailable [s:lasfutbolistas-derbi-preview]. Their coach is Marc Ballester [s:vavel-jornada-9-preview], and Barça's preview says they need a win to move away from the drop zone [s:fcb-preview-palamos].

Romeu was direct about the opposition: "Badalona should have more points than they have, given the quality of their players" [s:fcb-preview-palamos].

## The wider table after matchday 8

Matchday 8 gave these Liga F results: Atlético de Madrid 4-0 VCF Femenino, Real Madrid 3-2 Costa Adeje Tenerife, Athletic Club 0-0 Sevilla, Real Sociedad 1-1 Espanyol, Deportivo Abanca 2-1 Deportivo Alavés and SD Eibar 2-2 Madrid CFF [s:ligaf-jornada-8].
