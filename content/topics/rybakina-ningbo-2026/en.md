## Why the final is being searched

Her name is in the headlines after the Ningbo final on Sunday 25 October 2026 [s:the-star-ningbo-final]. The match was short. [Elena Rybakina](person:elena-rybakina) beat unseeded Canadian Leylah Fernandez 6-2, 6-2 in 1 hour and 16 minutes [s:qatar-tribune-ningbo]. Fernandez went into the final ranked 30th in the world [s:the-star-ningbo-final]. The top seed therefore faced an unseeded opponent in the final [s:the-star-ningbo-final] [s:wta-ningbo-preview].

## The final and the road to it

Rybakina was the world No. 1 and the top seed in Ningbo [s:wta-ningbo-preview]. In the semifinal on Saturday she beat sixth-seeded Diana Shnaider 6-3, 7-5 [s:wta-ningbo-preview]. Fernandez reached the final with the same score against Marie Bouzkova [s:wta-ningbo-preview].

The win was her fourth title of the 2026 season, the most by any player this year, and her 15th overall [s:qatar-tribune-ningbo] [s:the-star-ningbo-final]. Reuters, carried by The Star, described it as her first trophy as world No. 1 [s:the-star-ningbo-final]. The champion takes 500 ranking points and $185,500 [s:wta-ningbo-preview]. She was defending those points as the reigning champion [s:wta-ningbo-preview] [s:wta-ningbo-2025].

Rybakina linked the result to last year's event. "Last year, I started to play really well from this tournament," she said after the match [s:qatar-tribune-ningbo]. The Star's headline describes the result as a retention of the Ningbo crown [s:the-star-ningbo-final].

## Defending a title from 2025

This was a defence. Rybakina first won Ningbo on 19 October 2025, beating Ekaterina Alexandrova 3-6, 6-0, 6-2 [s:wta-ningbo-2025]. That was her 10th career WTA singles title and her second of the 2025 season [s:wta-ningbo-2025]. After that final she thanked her team "for always pushing me to be better" [s:wta-ningbo-2025].

Her 2026 season already had two majors in it. She won the Australian Open on 31 January, beating Aryna Sabalenka 6-4, 4-6, 6-4 and hitting 47 aces across the tournament [s:wta-australian-open-2026]. In September she beat Sabalenka 6-4, 5-7, 6-2 in the US Open final, her first US Open title and her third Grand Slam singles title [s:us-open-2026-ap]. Sabalenka was the two-time defending champion in New York [s:us-open-2026-ap]. The WTA reported that she earned AUD 4.1 million (USD 2.75 million) during that Australian Open run [s:wta-australian-open-2026].

## What comes next

The next question is the year-end No. 1 ranking. A Ningbo title would go a long way toward securing it [s:wta-ningbo-preview], and the win moved her closer to that goal [s:the-star-ningbo-final]. The US Open result was also tied to the rankings. Rybakina was set to replace Sabalenka as world No. 1 when the next rankings were released [s:us-open-2026-ap], and at Ningbo she was already the top seed [s:wta-ningbo-preview].

Her next event is the season finale, which the Star's report places next month, ahead of the WTA Finals [s:the-star-ningbo-final]. Watch whether she carries the top ranking into Indian Wells, where the season finale starts on 8 November [s:the-star-ningbo-final].
