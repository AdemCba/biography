## From TNA to NXT under a new name

Cruz Montana is the ring name that Mike Santana uses in WWE [s:postwrestling-cruz-montana-name]. WWE's profile describes "a globally respected catalogue of tag team and singles stardom" and closes with the line "He's here to take it." [s:wwe-cruz-montana-profile]

Before WWE, Santana was one of TNA's leading singles wrestlers. He left AEW in March 2024 [s:wrestlinginc-cruz-montana-ajstyles] and won the TNA World Championship twice [s:slamwrestling-cruz-montana-tna-exit]. He lost that title to Nic Nemeth at TNA Slammiversary 2026, a few days before his NXT debut [s:slamwrestling-cruz-montana-tna-exit]. Slam Wrestling says he became one of TNA's top singles stars following his departure from AEW in 2024 [s:slamwrestling-cruz-montana-tna-exit].

He explained the move himself. "It wasn't an easy decision. They've done so much for me in my career, and loyalty is a big thing for me." He also said that TNA "was somewhere where I felt comfortable. I didn't want to feel comfortable anymore." [s:slamwrestling-cruz-montana-tna-exit]

The name has a family reason. Santana called it "a name I put together as a tribute to honor my late father" [s:postwrestling-cruz-montana-name]. He was a free agent earlier in July 2026 [s:postwrestling-cruz-montana-name]. His NXT debut came in the week of 21 July 2026, when he appeared at the WWE Performance Center after a title match [s:postwrestling-cruz-montana-name]. The new name was confirmed on a Monday night later that month, a week after that debut [s:postwrestling-cruz-montana-name].

## What happened on Raw on 2 November 2026

WWE's results page says that "The OTC was seemingly in control, but NXT's Cruz Montana arrived and took out the World Heavyweight Champion" before seemingly aligning with the dangerous group, The 946 [s:wwe-raw-2026-11-02-results].

Earlier in the show, Reigns dismissed The 946 as a threat during a confrontation with Sami Zayn, who refused to back down [s:wwe-raw-2026-11-02-results]. The match was Royce Keys against Solo Sikoa. It ended in a no-contest, and WWE says it "ended with The 946 destroying The Bloodline" [s:wwe-raw-2026-11-02-results].

Bronco Nima, Lucien Price and Carmelo Hayes then entered the ring and attacked Sikoa. The page says that prompted Reigns and The Usos to come to his aid [s:wwe-raw-2026-11-02-results].

## The 946 and the Bloodline feud

The results page does not give a full roster for The 946. The page places Reigns and The Usos on the other side, coming to Sikoa's aid [s:wwe-raw-2026-11-02-results].
