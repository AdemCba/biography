## Why people search it today

The Golden State Warriors won their first game of the season on Monday, 26 October, beating the Denver Nuggets 134-114 [s:nba-starting-5-warriors-nuggets-2026] [s:cbs-warriors-nuggets-2026]. Stephen Curry was eight for eight from the field, three of those shots from deep, and finished with 24 points [s:nba-starting-5-warriors-nuggets-2026] [s:cbs-warriors-nuggets-2026]. It was the first time in his career that he scored 20 or more on 100 percent shooting [s:nba-starting-5-warriors-nuggets-2026]. The game's top scorer, though, was Yaxel Lendeborg, who led everyone with 31 points, 12 rebounds and six assists [s:nba-starting-5-warriors-nuggets-2026].

## Lendeborg's night

Lendeborg made nine of 19 shots from the field and 12 of 13 free throws, and he went 1-of-8 from three-point range [s:cbs-warriors-nuggets-2026]. Brandin Podziemski added 18 points for Golden State, with a plus-minus of +33 [s:cbs-warriors-nuggets-2026]. Before Monday, Lendeborg had been averaging 18.7 points and 8.3 rebounds, and he had scored a team-high 26 in the loss at Phoenix [s:cbs-winless-warriors-preview-2026].

After the game Curry talked about his teammate. He said Lendeborg has composure and is "never really in a hurry," and that he is "starting to see the pictures really well." Curry called him "kind of the full package" [s:nba-starting-5-warriors-nuggets-2026]. NBA.com wrote that after "a strong opening week," Lendeborg provided his biggest spark yet [s:nba-starting-5-warriors-nuggets-2026].
