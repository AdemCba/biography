Netflix is showing a live darts special called Beat Luke Littler on Friday 30 October 2026 [s:broadcastnow-beat-luke-littler]. Twenty amateur players will each get one leg of 501 against [Luke Littler](person:luke-littler), then world No. 1 [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler]. Anyone who wins a leg takes £501,000 [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler].

## What the format asks of the players

Each player gets a single leg of 501, and the show is described as one night and one chance [s:banijay-uk-beat-luke-littler]. The prize goes to any amateur who beats Littler in that leg [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler]. The show aims to bring the sport's electric atmosphere to the streaming service [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler]. Netflix describes it as a live sports show, and the producers present it as one night in which the challengers face the world champion on live television [s:banijay-uk-beat-luke-littler].

Netflix announced the show in late July 2026, and Broadcast reported that Banijay UK is producing the event for Netflix [s:broadcastnow-beat-luke-littler]. Netflix opened a nationwide recruitment search, and Littler appears in a video message that launches it [s:banijay-uk-beat-luke-littler].

## Who runs it and how players qualify

Netflix created the show in partnership with the PDC, the organisation behind professional darts [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler]. Workerbee, a Banijay label, produces it [s:banijay-uk-beat-luke-littler] [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler]. Workerbee is part of Banijay UK, the same group Broadcast names as the producer [s:banijay-uk-beat-luke-littler] [s:broadcastnow-beat-luke-littler].

Players qualify through open regional heats. The heats were scheduled for London and Manchester in September 2026, and the final 20 players are selected from them [s:banijay-uk-beat-luke-littler]. A bullet point in the announcement says the heats run across the country, but the body text names only London and Manchester [s:banijay-uk-beat-luke-littler]. Interested players register at BeatLukeLittler.com [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler].

The same Netflix announcement page also lists other UK sport projects. These include a renewed deal for The Rest Is Football's Premier League coverage, a Jose Mourinho documentary and a UK version of the Untold sport documentary series [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler]. None of them is part of Beat Luke Littler.

## Littler's record behind the challenge

Littler won the PDC World Championship in 2025 and again in 2026, which makes him a back-to-back champion [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler]. He was world No. 1 and had just won the World Matchplay when the event was announced [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler].

Littler said: "Everyone thinks they can beat me. Now they get a chance to prove it. Bring it on." [s:whats-on-netflix-beat-luke-littler]
