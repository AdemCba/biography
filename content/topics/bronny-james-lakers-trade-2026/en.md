Bronny James is searched for again because the Lakers trade rumours have moved from general speculation to specific names. The reports say the Lakers could use him as a trade piece for a top power forward or centre [s:heavy-bronny-trade]. ## What the reports say

Analyst Jovan Buha has said the Lakers could move James or Jaden Hardy to land a power forward or centre. PJ Washington and Derrick Jones Jr. are the names mentioned as targets [s:heavy-bronny-trade]. A separate report says James is on the chopping block in the team's trade plans [s:heavy-chopping-block].

Earlier coverage named other clubs as possible partners. The Clippers and Mavericks were among the teams mentioned in one of the reports [s:yahoo-lakers-bronny-trade]. Those reports are older and rumour-stage, so they should be read as possibilities, not as talks in progress.

## What is confirmed

The Lakers have not announced that they are shopping James, and no trade involving him has been confirmed [s:yardbarker-bronny]. Most of the current coverage is built on analysts' suggestions, and that is the main reason to be careful with headlines that say he is "going" somewhere.
