Fans are searching for the new *Avengers: Doomsday* posters because Marvel has released a set made for premium cinema formats, and Doctor Doom is the focus of three of the four official posters. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-premium-2026] The film opens on December 18, and the release date is printed on the posters. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-formats-2026] [s:tribune-doomsday-posters-2026]

## What the posters show

Four official posters are tied to specific formats: ScreenX, 4DX, RealD 3D and Fandango. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-premium-2026] Tribune reports that Marvel Studios released exclusive 4DX and ScreenX posters that focus on the villain rather than the large superhero group. [s:tribune-doomsday-posters-2026]

The 4DX poster shows Doom in dark armor and a long cape against a stormy sky, with the tagline "Feel it in 4DX." The Infinity Vision label sits below the logo on that poster. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-formats-2026] The RealD 3D version is a tight close-up of his metal mask in glowing green, with the line "Meet Your Doom in RealD 3D." [s:comicbasics-doomsday-formats-2026] The ScreenX design shows hooded figures against green stained glass. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-premium-2026]

The Fandango exclusive is the most direct of the set. Doom sits on a throne in riveted armor, lit by warm light through tall stained-glass windows, with the tagline "All roads lead to Doom" and a prompt to reserve tickets. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-formats-2026]

## Infinity Vision and the IMAX gap

Several of the posters carry Infinity Vision branding. It is a premium large-format certification, and it debuted with the *Avengers: Endgame Encore* re-release on September 25. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-formats-2026] On the Infinity Vision poster, Doom stands inside an arched stained-glass window in a green cloak and hood, under the line "See it in Infinity Vision." [s:comicbasics-doomsday-formats-2026]

Each poster carries the release date, and the RealD 3D version prints it as 12.18.26. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-formats-2026]

IMAX fans will have to wait. *Dune: Part Three* holds an exclusive IMAX run for three weeks from December 18, so *Avengers: Doomsday* cannot use IMAX screens at launch. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-formats-2026] The same article says the film will carry 4DX and ScreenX screenings as well. [s:tribune-doomsday-posters-2026] Check the format of each showtime before buying tickets, especially if you want the largest picture. The IMAX gap runs for the first three weeks only, so the choice of screen matters most in the opening weeks. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-formats-2026]

## What to watch next

The next date to watch is December 18, when the film opens in North America. Until then, the question most people will ask is which premium screen is nearby.

For anyone scanning the posters, the Infinity Vision label is the one to look for, and the Fandango design is the only one that asks readers to reserve tickets. [s:comicbasics-doomsday-formats-2026] Marvel has chosen the villain as the face of this push. Whether the campaign keeps that focus as the release gets closer is the thing to watch, along with any new poster that brings back the wider team for a premium format.
