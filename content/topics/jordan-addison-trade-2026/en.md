Fans are searching for Jordan Addison because a November 1, 2026 article, first published by HEAVY and also carried by Yahoo Sports, asks whether the Minnesota Vikings might trade their wide receiver before the NFL trade deadline [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026] [s:yahoo-addison-trade-floated-2026]. The headline says the trade is "floated". The text shows a columnist's argument, not a report of a deal or of negotiations.

## Where the trade idea comes from

The idea was refreshed by Adam Carlson of Sports Illustrated, and the HEAVY writer, Brevan Bane, builds the piece on Carlson's analysis [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026]. Bane's article cites no actual trade talks involving Addison [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026]. Readers should treat it as a discussion of what a trade would require, not as news that one is under way.

## The contract that makes it a question

Addison is entering the fifth-year option of his rookie deal, where Minnesota "would be on the hook for $18 million" [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026]. If he does not sign an extension before the end of the 2027 season, he becomes an unrestricted free agent [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026].

Carlson also writes that the cost of a top-tier WR2 has skyrocketed, with several earning "around $20 million per year" [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026]. The article calls the situation one "to monitor this offseason" [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026].

## What is opinion

Most of the trade case is opinion. The reader should weigh each claim by who makes it.

- Carlson writes that it "would take a stellar trade package" for the Vikings to deal Addison, but that "a deal could get done" for the right compensation [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026].
- Carlson's view is that "Paying Addison to stay in Minnesota should be the most hopeful outcome" [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026].
- Bane writes that "It's hard to imagine that the Vikings would trade Addison away" while they are "sitting at 6-1 or 7-0" [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026]. That is an opinion about the team's position, not a report of any internal discussion.

None of these lines is a statement from the team. None is a report of an offer, a counteroffer or a named rival club's interest.

## The deadline date

Heavy says the Vikings are "eight days away from the 2026 trade deadline" [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026]. The NFL's 2026-27 calendar gives the date as November 10, and it states that "All trading ends for 2026 at 4:00 p.m., New York time" [s:nfl-2026-27-important-dates]. Counted from November 1, the league date is nine days away, not eight. For any question about timing, the league calendar is the figure to use.

## Background from 2025

Addison's name has come up in Vikings trade coverage before. Sports Illustrated's August 20, 2025 article noted Addison's three-game suspension, which followed a no-contest plea to a "wet reckless" driving misdemeanor in California [s:si-vikings-pelissero-exploring-trade-2025]. The same article, citing Tom Pelissero, quoted: "No trade is imminent. But Minnesota is actively looking" [s:si-vikings-pelissero-exploring-trade-2025].

That 2025 report, by its headline, concerned a trade for a veteran receiver. It was not a report about trading Addison. A report that the team is "looking" at the market is a different claim from a report that a player is on the block.

## What is not confirmed

The reporting reviewed for this piece does not show an agreed trade, formal talks or an offer for Addison. It also contains no statement from the Vikings about his trade status.

## What to watch next

The deadline is November 10, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. New York time [s:nfl-2026-27-important-dates]. Three things would change this story. The first is a report from a named NFL reporter that talks have begun. The second is an on-record statement from the Vikings about Addison's future. The third is any extension news, because the contract timeline is central to the question [s:heavy-addison-trade-floated-2026].

Until one of those appears, the accurate summary is short: a columnist has raised the idea, the contract timeline is why it is being discussed, and no trade has been reported.
