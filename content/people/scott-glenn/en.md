Scott Glenn was born on 26 January in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania [s:tv-academy], and the year is where sources part ways. An April 2016 article called him 75, which points to 1941, while a December 2020 interview gave his age as 81, which points to 1939 [s:insidehook-2016] [s:yahoo-greenland-2020]. In March 2024 he described himself as 85 on a podcast [s:ferriss-2024]. We therefore give no exact age and do not pick a year. What every source agrees on is that he has been working as an actor since the 1960s, and that in 2025 he earned an Emmy nomination, the only one the Television Academy lists for him [s:tv-academy].

## Pittsburgh, scarlet fever and the Marines

Glenn grew up in Pittsburgh [s:yahoo-greenland-2020]. As a child he caught scarlet fever. He has said that doctors kept him from reading because they worried about his eyes, so he "lived with his imagination" for that stretch, and he dreamed of becoming a poet [s:yahoo-greenland-2020]. In another interview he said the illness left him with lasting hearing damage and a period of limping, and that he became an athlete as a result [s:ferriss-2024]. He describes himself as "painfully shy" as a boy and says he began martial arts at ten [s:flashback-files].

He studied English at the College of William and Mary and served in the Marine Corps [s:tvguide-bio]. Remind Magazine puts his service at three years in the early 1960s, while Glenn told Tim Ferriss he joined as a six-month reservist, so the length of his service is described differently [s:remind-2025] [s:ferriss-2024]. He credits the Marines with teaching him "peripheral walking", a way of reading threats in side vision that he later used to play a blind character [s:flashback-files].

## From a newsroom to an acting class

Before acting he was a newspaper reporter in Kenosha, Wisconsin [s:tvguide-bio] [s:yahoo-greenland-2020]. At about 27 he accepted a newspaper job in the Virgin Islands that would not start for six months, and a friend suggested that he fill the gap with acting classes in New York to improve his dialogue writing [s:yahoo-greenland-2020]. In his first class, with about eleven people watching, he felt "a light bulb went off between my eyes" [s:yahoo-greenland-2020]. He has said he studied at Berghof Studios [s:ferriss-2024].

The stage came first. Remind Magazine dates his Broadway debut to 1965, his first steady television role on Burt Reynolds' series Hawk to 1966, and his entry into the Actors Studio to 1968 [s:remind-2025]. His first film role was in The Baby Maker in 1970 [s:soapcentral-2025].

## The 1970s: small parts and a near exit

Glenn had small roles in Nashville (1975) and Apocalypse Now (1979) [s:remind-2025]. On Apocalypse Now he was on set for about seven months and says the time around Marlon Brando shaped his confidence as an actor [s:ferriss-2024]. After The Baby Maker, Nashville and Apocalypse Now he felt his film career had ended, and he moved his family to the mountains of Idaho [s:flashback-files].

## How Urban Cowboy gave him a second start

By 1978 he had essentially quit acting [s:insidehook-2017]. His friend, the writer and director James Bridges, persuaded him to audition for the villain in Urban Cowboy, opposite John Travolta, and Glenn drove 2,000 miles in his truck to the audition [s:insidehook-2017]. He won the part of Wes Hightower, and says the role made his career [s:ferriss-2024]. Remind Magazine says the 1980 film made him one of its stars [s:remind-2025]. After it, he says, he rarely had to audition again [s:flashback-files].

## Career timeline

- **1965 to 1968:** Broadway debut, a steady role on Hawk and entry to the Actors Studio [s:remind-2025].
- **1975 to 1979:** small parts in Nashville and Apocalypse Now [s:remind-2025].
- **1980:** Urban Cowboy [s:remind-2025].
- **1983:** Alan Shepard in The Right Stuff. Glenn is left-handed and Shepard was right-handed, so he tied his left hand behind his back to learn to use his right [s:flashback-files] [s:insidehook-2017].
- **1990 and 1991:** The Hunt for Red October, The Silence of the Lambs and Backdraft [s:remind-2025]. For Backdraft he went on ride-alongs with a Chicago Fire Department squad [s:tvguide-bio].
- **2001:** Training Day [s:remind-2025].
- **2015 and 2017:** Stick in Daredevil, then in The Defenders across six episodes [s:collider-2015] [s:soapcentral-2025].
- **2015:** season 2 of The Leftovers in production in Austin [s:joblo-2015].
- **2017:** announced for Alan Pangborn in Hulu's Castle Rock [s:insidehook-2017] [s:ferriss-2024].
- **2025:** Jim Hollinger in The White Lotus and the lead in Eugene the Marine [s:remind-2025] [s:oldenburg-2025].

Remind Magazine notes that he played CIA Director Ezra Kramer in two Jason Bourne films and that since the 2010s he has worked mostly in cable and streaming drama [s:remind-2025]. The Oldenburg festival describes him as one of Hollywood's most prolific character actors [s:oldenburg-2025].

## Stick, Kevin Garvey Sr. and a late-career run

On television Glenn said he had found the medium unsatisfying until The Leftovers and Daredevil changed his mind [s:joblo-2015]. He called The Leftovers his favourite job ever [s:insidehook-2017]. In Daredevil he played Stick, Matt Murdock's blind mentor, and said it was the first time he had played a blind person [s:collider-2015] [s:joblo-2015]. Marvel, he said, gave him wide creative latitude: "The blueprint was 100% theirs but the filling in was all mine" [s:joblo-2015]. Fans in Melbourne, Australia, called him "Stick" in the street [s:insidehook-2017].

## Awards and net worth

Glenn was nominated for the 2025 Primetime Emmy for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Drama Series for his role as Jim Hollinger in The White Lotus, the nominated episode being "Killer Instincts" [s:tv-academy]. The Television Academy lists no other nomination [s:tv-academy].  The Oldenburg International Film Festival gave him a tribute with screenings of Urban Cowboy, The Challenge, The Silence of the Lambs and Carla's Song in September 2025 [s:oldenburg-2025].

We do not report a net worth. Websites publish many figures, but none we found names an outlet and a date, and no Forbes or Bloomberg estimate turned up.

## Family and public life

Glenn is married to Carol, a potter, and they have two daughters. He also mentions a son-in-law and a grandson [s:flashback-files] [s:ferriss-2024]. He says he met Carol in a Manhattan movie theater [s:ferriss-2024]. They moved to Idaho, where he has lived for roughly four decades and where he climbs, hikes and hunts [s:ferriss-2024].

He writes poetry. Each holiday season he wrote Carol a poem, and for their 50th anniversary she self-published them as *Room Service*. A second collection, *Friction Zone*, grew out of poems he wrote during the pandemic [s:ferriss-2024]. In December 2020 he said the poet in him was "still alive" [s:yahoo-greenland-2020].

## Fitness and the outdoors

Glenn has long been known for physical work. In 2016 he trained with SEAL Team Six, spearfished in open water, rode motorcycles and did martial arts with knives, and had only recently stopped ice-climbing and skydiving [s:insidehook-2016] [s:insidehook-2017]. Remind Magazine reports that he stopped open-water spearfishing after a doctor advised him to following a skin cancer diagnosis [s:remind-2025]. At 85 he described a routine of breathwork, kettlebell swings and push-ups, and gave his best freediving breath hold as 4 minutes 15 seconds [s:ferriss-2024].

## What he is doing now

In The White Lotus season 3 he plays Jim Hollinger, the resort's co-owner, whom Walton Goggins' character confronts in the penultimate episode [s:remind-2025]. He was initially put off: the producers first described an older man with a cane, and he agreed after watching season 1 [s:remind-2025]. He performed the stunt in which he is pushed over in his chair himself [s:remind-2025].

Eugene the Marine, directed by Hank Bedford and shot on 16mm, opened the 32nd Oldenburg International Film Festival in September 2025 as a world premiere [s:oldenburg-2025]. Glenn plays a former marine whose son tries to force him out of the family home while a series of murders begins around him. The cast includes Jim Gaffigan, Annette O'Toole, Shioli Kutsuna and Jeremy Bobb [s:oldenburg-2025].
