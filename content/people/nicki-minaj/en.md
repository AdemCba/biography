## From mixtapes to Young Money

Her first mixtape, Playtime Is Over, came out in April 2007. Sucka Free followed in 2008 and Beam Me Up Scotty in 2009 [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj]. In August 2009 she signed to Lil Wayne’s Young Money label, becoming its first female artist [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj]. She also appeared on the We Are Young Money compilation that year [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj].

2010 was the breakthrough year. Her first single, “Massive Attack”, came out in April, followed by “Your Love” [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj]. Two months later she won Best Hip Hop Female at the BET Awards [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj].

## Pink Friday, Roman Reloaded and The Pinkprint

Her debut album, Pink Friday, came out in November 2010 with eight singles released to promote it. It went triple platinum [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj].

The follow-up, Pink Friday: Roman Reloaded, reached the top of the pop, R&B and rap charts, and “Starships” was its first breakout single [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj]. In 2012 she reached a national audience with a Super Bowl appearance [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj].

The Pinkprint followed on 12 December 2014 with eight singles and went double platinum [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj]. In 2016 she won the BET award for Best Female Hip Hop Artist for the seventh consecutive time [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj].

## Chart records and Queen

In 2017 she took over the record for most Billboard Hot 100 entries by a female artist, a record previously held by Aretha Franklin [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj]. In April 2018 she released “Chun-Li” and “Barbie Tingz” [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj]. On 8 August 2018 she launched her Queen Radio show on Apple Music’s Beats 1 station, and two days later her fourth studio album, Queen, debuted at No. 2 [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj].

Early in 2019 she began The Nicki Wrld Tour in Frankfurt, Germany, with Juice Wrld [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj]. The “Say So” remix with Doja Cat became her first song to reach the top of the Billboard Hot 100 [s:biography-com-nicki-minaj].

## The album she said she would not release

On 24 September 2025 Minaj returned to X and posted “3.27.26” with a CD emoji. Fans read it as a release date for her next album [s:ibtimes-nothing-austin]. The date passed on 27 March 2026 without a release [s:ibtimes-nothing-austin].

On 15 October 2025 she wrote on X: “Ok I’m not going to put out the album anymore... No more music.” [s:ibtimes-nothing-austin] [s:digitalmusicnews-album-cancel]. The post included the line “Hope you’re happy now”, aimed at Jay-Z [s:digitalmusicnews-album-cancel]. Digital Music News noted that the album had been due for release in March [s:digitalmusicnews-album-cancel]. The report was dated 16 October 2025, and its headline said she blamed Jay-Z for the cancellation [s:digitalmusicnews-album-cancel].

Her Austin answer did not say whether that decision still stood [s:ibtimes-nothing-austin].
