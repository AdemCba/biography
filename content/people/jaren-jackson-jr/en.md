## From Plainfield to the fourth pick

Jackson was born on 15 September 1999 in Plainfield, New Jersey [s:nba-jjj-bio]. He went to Michigan State, where he was named Big Ten Freshman and Defensive Player of the Year in 2017-18 [s:nba-jjj-bio]. The Memphis Grizzlies selected him fourth overall in the 2018 NBA Draft, on 21 June 2018 [s:nba-jjj-bio]. Basketball Reference lists a 2018-19 All-Rookie selection among his early honors [s:bbref-jjj]. That rookie season he played 58 games and averaged 13.8 points, 4.7 rebounds and 1.4 blocks [s:bbref-jjj]. Before the NBA, he helped the United States win gold at the 2016 FIBA U17 World Cup [s:nba-jjj-bio].

## Defensive Player of the Year and All-Star selections

The NBA named him the 2022-23 Kia Defensive Player of the Year. He received 56 first-place votes and 391 points, ahead of Milwaukee's Brook Lopez, who had 31 first-place votes and 309 points [s:nba-jjj-dpoy-2023]. The award was announced on 17 April 2023 [s:nba-jjj-dpoy-2023]. That season he led the league with 3.0 blocks per game [s:nba-jjj-dpoy-2023], and he averaged 18.6 points and 6.8 rebounds in 63 games for Memphis [s:bbref-jjj].

Basketball Reference lists two blocks titles and three All-Defensive selections across his career [s:bbref-jjj]. Those selections were first-team honors in 2021-22 and 2022-23, and a second-team honor in 2024-25 [s:bbref-jjj]. He also finished fifth in the 2021-22 Defensive Player of the Year voting and seventh in 2024-25 [s:bbref-jjj].

He was named a reserve for the 2023 All-Star Game, and he was picked again for the 74th All-Star Game in 2025, which was his second career selection [s:actionnews5-jjj-all-star-2025]. That 2024-25 season he averaged 22.2 points and 5.6 rebounds in 74 games [s:bbref-jjj]. The NBA lists him as a two-time All-Star [s:nba-trade-jazz-2026]. The NBA bio also lists him under NBA Rising Stars in 2019 and 2020 [s:nba-jjj-bio]. His listed position is forward-center, and he wears number 20 for the Jazz [s:nba-jjj-bio].
