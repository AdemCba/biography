Ousmane Dembélé is out with a left thigh injury. After Paris Saint-Germain's Champions League match against Barcelona, the club said he had suffered a left thigh injury and would remain in care for the coming weeks [s:psg-medical-communique-2026-10]. The statement gave no return date. He was born in Vernon, in Normandy, on 15 May 1997 [s:psg-player], and he won the 2025 Ballon d'Or [s:uefa-ballon-dor-2026].

## From Rennes to the Ligue 1 stage

Dembélé joined the Rennes training centre at 13, and he was picked for French youth teams from the U17 level [s:foot01-rennes-2016]. He signed his first professional contract on 1 October 2015 [s:foot01-rennes-2016]. His Ligue 1 debut came at Angers on 6 November 2015, when he was brought on in the 86th minute [s:foot01-rennes-2016]. His first league goal came against Bordeaux on 22 November 2015 [s:foot01-rennes-2016].

That season he had 12 goals and five assists, and the French players' union UNFP named him the best young player in Ligue 1 [s:foot01-rennes-2016]. PSG's profile lists the same award, Ligue 1 Young Player of the Year for 2015-16 [s:psg-player].

## Dortmund and a debut season

In May 2016 Borussia Dortmund announced that the 18-year-old had signed a contract running until 30 June 2021 [s:bvb-signing-2016]. The club said both parties had agreed to keep all information confidential, so it published no fee [s:bvb-signing-2016]. German press put the fee at about 15 million euros, but the figure is unconfirmed [s:foot01-rennes-2016].

In December 2017 the German players' association presented him with the award for best debutant of the 2016-17 Bundesliga, and he also received a trophy for being in the league's best XI of that season [s:fcb-debutant-2017].

## Barcelona, 2017 to 2023

On 25 August 2017 Barcelona announced that they had reached an agreement for the transfer of Dembélé for 105 million euros plus add-ons. The club also gave a five-year contract and a buy-out clause of 400 million euros [s:fcb-signing-2017]. The statement did not give the amount of the add-ons [s:fcb-signing-2017].

Barcelona's honours listed on his PSG profile include three Spanish league titles, two Copa del Rey trophies and two Spanish Super Cups [s:psg-player]. In September 2017 he was hurt in the 29th minute of the match against Getafe. Barcelona's medical statement, dated 17 September 2017, said tests confirmed a tendon rupture in his left thigh, in the biceps femoris. Surgery was scheduled for later that week in Finland, and the expected absence was three and a half to four months [s:fcb-medical-2017].

## Joining PSG in 2023

On 12 August 2023 PSG signed Dembélé on a five-year deal. The fee was 50.4 million euros, the figure given in Barcelona's statement [s:national-psg-2023]. Dembélé said: "I'm delighted to be joining Paris Saint-Germain and can't wait to play for my new club" [s:national-psg-2023].

In the 2024-25 season he scored 21 goals and set up eight in 29 Ligue 1 appearances, and scored eight and set up six in the Champions League [s:si-ballon-dor-2025]. The men's Ballon d'Or was presented on 22 September 2025 [s:topendsports-ballon-dor-2025]. He finished ahead of Barcelona's Lamine Yamal, who was second in the voting [s:si-ballon-dor-2025]. PSG's profile lists the 2025 Champions League with PSG, and describes the 2026 title as the club's second consecutive win in the competition [s:psg-player].

## A thigh that has been hurt before

In September 2025 he hurt a hamstring while playing for France in a 2-0 win over Ukraine in World Cup qualifying. He was sidelined for more than a month [s:foxsports-2025-injury]. In October 2025 PSG named him in the squad for the Champions League trip to Bayer Leverkusen [s:foxsports-2025-injury].

## Rested after the international break

On 9 October PSG announced that Dembélé and Nuno Mendes would rest after the international break and miss the Ligue 1 match against Le Mans the next day. Both were to return to training on 10 October [s:roundtable-rest-2026-10]. Luis Enrique, the PSG coach, said the two needed rest to focus on the upcoming Ligue 1 and Champions League matches [s:roundtable-rest-2026-10]. He added: "You have to get used to it, because this will be the norm this season" [s:roundtable-rest-2026-10].

That was a management decision, not an injury. The same report said the decision did not appear to be about favouritism, and it noted the forward's history of recurring injuries [s:roundtable-rest-2026-10].

## The thigh injury against Barcelona

The injury came in the Champions League match against Barcelona. Press reports said he came off early in the first half while chasing a ball [s:gffn-thigh-injury-2026-10]. The report, which cites PSG's medical statement, said he would be out for weeks [s:gffn-thigh-injury-2026-10].

The club's statement did not give a date for his return. It said only that he would remain in care for the coming weeks [s:psg-medical-communique-2026-10]. Al Jazeera, citing AFP, AP and Reuters, reported on 23 October that he would miss the Ballon d'Or ceremony in London on Monday, because PSG's Ligue 1 home match had been moved to the same evening [s:aljazeera-ballon-dor-absence-2026-10].

## Ballon d'Or 2026

UEFA lists Dembélé among the 2026 men's nominees, with the ceremony set for 26 October in London [s:uefa-ballon-dor-2026]. The report also said that several PSG team-mates were nominated with him, and that the Lyon game had been postponed to Monday, after a police request amid violent protests in Paris [s:aljazeera-ballon-dor-absence-2026-10].

## Club and country

His PSG profile gives his full name as Masour Ousmane Dembélé and lists him as squad number 10 [s:psg-player]. It also records him as a France international since September 2016 [s:psg-player], and as World Cup winner with France in 2018 [s:psg-player].
