Jynxzi, whose real name is Nicholas Stewart, is 25 years old as of 4 November 2026, having been born on 26 September 2001 [s:legit-bio-2024]. He is an American streamer who built the most-subscribed channel on Twitch by playing almost nothing but Rainbow Six Siege [s:meinmmo-2023] [s:tubefilter-rcc-2023]. In February 2026 he was back at number one on Twitch's subscription chart with 81,286 subscribers [s:earlygame-2026].

## Age, real name and background

Legit.ng gives his birth date as 26 September 2001 and his birthplace as Washington, United States, without saying whether that means the state or the capital [s:legit-bio-2024]. MeinMMO called him 22 in September 2024, which fits that date [s:meinmmo-breckie-2024]. Know Your Meme confirms the real name, Nicholas Stewart, and says he lives in Florida [s:kym-jynxzi]. Tubefilter uses the same name [s:tubefilter-2024].

Legit.ng lists his height as 5 ft 10 in (178 cm) and says he has two brothers and a sister [s:legit-bio-2024]. Little else about his family is public, and we do not guess. The same profile says he began streaming at 17 [s:legit-bio-2024]. His nationality is American [s:meinmmo-2023].

## Four slow years, then a breakout

Tubefilter says he started streaming consistently in 2019 [s:tubefilter-2024]. According to Dexerto, quoted by Tubefilter, he averaged one viewer per stream that year [s:tubefilter-2024]. A Tubefilter piece from December 2023 says he played Rainbow Six Siege to about five viewers for roughly his first year [s:tubefilter-rcc-2023]. Legit.ng instead says he first streamed Call of Duty: Black Ops 4, so the sources differ on the very first game [s:legit-bio-2024].

On YouTube, Know Your Meme dates his audience building to 19 January 2020 and points to a "Rage Compilation #1" of his Rainbow Six Siege matches as the earliest video [s:kym-jynxzi]. That clip had more than 228,000 views and 5,100 likes after four years [s:kym-jynxzi].

The change came in 2023. Tubefilter reports that his peak monthly concurrent viewership was about 5,000 at the start of 2023 and regularly above 100,000 by early 2024 [s:tubefilter-2024]. MeinMMO wrote in November 2023 that he had about 3 million followers and, according to TwitchTracker, the most active subscribers on the platform, ahead of ibai, xQc, Gaules, HasanAbi and [Kai Cenat](person:kai-cenat) [s:meinmmo-2023]. By December 2023 Tubefilter counted 3.3 million followers and more than 106,000 active subscribers, over 24,000 more than Kai Cenat [s:tubefilter-rcc-2023].

He did this on Xbox, not PC like most top Rainbow Six Siege streamers, and MeinMMO said he noticeably lifted the game's viewer numbers [s:meinmmo-2023]. His style is loud: yelling, swearing and jumping around while he plays [s:meinmmo-2023].

## Management, memes and the 2024 awards

Tubefilter reported in December 2023 that he had signed with the talent agency Right Click Culture, which Sam Alavi founded in May 2020 and which has also worked with MrBeast, Ninja and SypherPK [s:tubefilter-rcc-2023]. In his first 30 days with the agency it arranged more than $1 million in brand deals, including an ambassadorship with the PC maker Starforge Systems [s:tubefilter-rcc-2023]. Tubefilter also mentions a G Fuel sponsorship [s:tubefilter-2024].

Know Your Meme records two clips that travelled beyond Twitch. A hot-wings stream produced the "Light Work, No Reaction" sound, which became a TikTok trend in mid-2023 [s:kym-jynxzi]. A Fortnite stream in which viewers joined his match and hunted him drew more than 13 million TikTok plays on 31 October 2023 [s:kym-jynxzi]. The same page says he started the Jynxzi Podcast in February 2024, with CaseOh as his first guest [s:kym-jynxzi].

On 18 February 2024 he won Gamer of the Year and Best Breakthrough Streamer at the 2024 Streamer Awards, according to Know Your Meme [s:kym-jynxzi]. We could not open the award body's own results page, so we cite that single source for the awards.

## Viewers, subscribers and records

How many people watch him depends on the date. MeinMMO measured an average of 32,900 viewers over three months in November 2023 [s:meinmmo-2023]. Tubefilter reported an average above 30,000 and a record peak of 61,000 in December 2023 [s:tubefilter-rcc-2023]. In 2024 MeinMMO said he often had about 40,000 viewers [s:meinmmo-breckie-2024], and in April 2025 it said more than 25,000 [s:meinmmo-earnings-2025]. MeinMMO also put his Twitch following at 6.3 million in September 2024 and above 7.2 million in April 2025 [s:meinmmo-breckie-2024] [s:meinmmo-earnings-2025].

In 2023 he passed Kai Cenat's subscriber record with more than 100,000 active subscribers [s:tubefilter-2024]. In February 2026 EarlyGame reported that he had regained first place on the subscription chart from the AI VTuber Neuro-sama, with 81,286 subscribers against her 73,617 [s:earlygame-2026]. When he first lost the top spot, he said he knew little about Neuro-sama and joked about being beaten by a robot [s:earlygame-2026].

His personal viewing record is recent. Streams Charts reported that his League of Legends tournament in May 2026 peaked at 417,700 concurrent viewers on his own channel, up from 249,500 at a 2024 celebrity boxing event [s:streamscharts-lol-2026]. The article's other figures contradict each other (the event total is given as both 920,800 and 908,000), so treat them as approximate [s:streamscharts-lol-2026].

## Net worth and earnings

No outlet such as Forbes or Bloomberg has published a net worth figure that we could verify. Two data points exist.

MeinMMO reported in April 2025 that he accidentally showed his Twitch statistics on stream on 23 April, covering 25 March to 23 April. They showed about $452,449 in earnings over roughly 237 hours of streaming [s:meinmmo-earnings-2025]. That figure covers subscriptions and ads and leaves out sponsorships, YouTube and TikTok [s:meinmmo-earnings-2025]. MeinMMO noted that monthly income can swing a lot [s:meinmmo-earnings-2025].

StreamScheme, a creator-statistics site, estimates his net worth at about $8.5 million, within a range of $4 million to $15 million [s:streamscheme-networth]. Its page is undated but uses 2026 material, and it says it cannot see his expenses, taxes or contracts [s:streamscheme-networth]. It is an estimate, not a reported figure.

## Girlfriend and the Breckie Hill split

For years the most searched private question was his relationship with the TikTok creator Breckie Hill. MeinMMO wrote that the pair often appeared together and made videos about the relationship [s:meinmmo-breckie-2024]. HotNewHipHop reported in June 2024 that they had split, reconciled, and then split again [s:hnhh-breakup-2024]. In a short, tearful stream he said "The reason why we broke up was 100% my fault" and "I never, obviously, cheated or anything like that" [s:hnhh-breakup-2024].

MeinMMO, in a September 2024 article, quoted him saying on stream on 10 September that Hill had learned he had looked at other women on social media, and that she was hurt [s:meinmmo-breckie-2024]. It reported that Hill posted on TikTok that she was single [s:meinmmo-breckie-2024]. Legit.ng's profile, last updated in November 2024, describes him as single [s:legit-bio-2024]. The sources we reviewed report no later relationship.

## What he is doing now

His channel is on Twitch at twitch.tv/jynxzi [s:twitch-jynxzi]. Since late 2024 he has also streamed on YouTube Live [s:streamscharts-clash-2025]. In 2025 he played Clash Royale, including a challenge in which he started on 99 wins and the first to 100 won the match [s:streamscharts-clash-2025]. xQc never won a game before Jynxzi reached 100, while StableRonaldo won game 90 [s:streamscharts-clash-2025]. Streams Charts counted more than 1,000,000 hours watched across both platforms [s:streamscharts-clash-2025].

In May 2026 he hosted a League of Legends tournament with 40 creators, which Team xQc won 28-4 over Team Zoil in the grand final [s:streamscharts-lol-2026]. Tubefilter reported on 13 May 2026 that Esports Charts counted a 920,000 peak for the event, then raised the question of whether such numbers were real amid a wider debate about viewbotting [s:tubefilter-viewbot-2026]. The article does not accuse him of using viewbots [s:tubefilter-viewbot-2026].

On 11 August 2026, Ubisoft announced he would host a six-hour Rainbow Six Siege 2v2 tournament for 16 streamers, with Twitch Drops for viewers [s:ubisoft-tournament-2026]. That event confirms he remains tied to the game that made his name. For similar creators, see [IShowSpeed](person:ishowspeed).
