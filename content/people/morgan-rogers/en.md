Morgan Rogers was born on 26 July 2002 in Halesowen [s:wikidata-morgan-rogers]. He is 24 [s:wikidata-morgan-rogers]. Wikidata lists him as a citizen of the United Kingdom and describes him as an attacking midfielder who also plays as a winger [s:wikidata-morgan-rogers].

## From West Brom and Manchester City to Middlesbrough

Wikidata records a spell at West Bromwich Albion in 2019, followed by Manchester City from 2019 to 2023 [s:wikidata-morgan-rogers]. Those years included loans to Lincoln City in 2021, AFC Bournemouth in 2021 and 2022, and Blackpool in 2023 [s:wikidata-morgan-rogers]. Middlesbrough held him from 2023 to 2024 [s:wikidata-morgan-rogers].

Middlesbrough sold him to Aston Villa, but the club kept a sell-on clause. Under that clause, Middlesbrough will receive 20% of the Chelsea fee [s:herald-chelsea-signing].

## Two seasons at Villa

Wikidata dates his Aston Villa spell from 1 February 2024 to 21 July 2026 [s:wikidata-morgan-rogers]. Across all competitions, Fanatik puts his Villa record at 31 goals in 125 appearances [s:fanatik-chelsea-signing]. In the Premier League he scored 21 goals in 85 games [s:herald-chelsea-signing]. The Analyst also records 17 Premier League assists over the same 85 games [s:analyst-rogers-stats]. Last season he added 14 goals and 11 assists in 55 appearances [s:herald-chelsea-signing].

## The Europa League final in Istanbul

Villa beat Freiburg 3-0 in the Europa League final at Besiktas Park in Istanbul on 20 May 2026 [s:premierleague-villa-freiburg]. Fanatik calls it the club's first major European trophy since 1982 [s:fanatik-chelsea-signing]. Youri Tielemans and Emiliano Buendia scored in the first half, Buendia in first-half stoppage time [s:premierleague-villa-freiburg]. Rogers made it three in the 58th minute, poking in Buendia's low cross from the left at the front post [s:premierleague-villa-freiburg].

Afterwards Rogers told TNT Sports, "We will go down in history" [s:premierleague-villa-freiburg]. John McGinn called it the proudest night of his career so far, noting how close the club came to a bad situation seven years ago [s:premierleague-villa-freiburg]. The Herald called him a key figure in that victory [s:herald-chelsea-signing].

## The fee and the move to Stamford Bridge

Chelsea signed Rogers from Villa in July 2026 for a reported £117m, which British media call a British transfer record [s:herald-chelsea-signing]. Fanatik converts that to $157 million [s:fanatik-chelsea-signing]. Wikidata dates his Chelsea career from 21 July 2026 [s:wikidata-morgan-rogers]. Villa had already lost Youri Tielemans to Manchester United, and Unai Emery was losing Rogers too [s:herald-chelsea-signing]. The fee is more than the £116m Manchester City paid Nottingham Forest for Elliot Anderson earlier in July [s:herald-chelsea-signing]. It also makes Rogers Chelsea's most expensive signing, ahead of Enzo Fernandez, who cost £107m from Benfica in 2023 [s:herald-chelsea-signing]. Fanatik notes that it beats the £115m Chelsea paid Brighton for Moises Caicedo in 2023 [s:fanatik-chelsea-signing].

Chelsea said he "has signed a contract at Stamford Bridge until 2033" [s:herald-chelsea-signing]. Chelsea had chased him for two years. Arsenal refused to match Villa's fee, and Chelsea got him instead [s:herald-chelsea-signing]. He was 23 when the deal was done. He passed a medical on the Monday after returning from the World Cup in North America, where he had played a role in England's run to the semi-finals [s:herald-chelsea-signing].

Rogers said he was "so excited". He called Chelsea "the biggest club in London" [s:herald-chelsea-signing]. He and Cole Palmer are close friends from their time together in Manchester City's academy [s:herald-chelsea-signing].
