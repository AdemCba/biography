## One-day cricket and the 2015 World Cup

The tournament that stands out in his limited-overs career is the 2015 World Cup. He took 22 wickets there and was named player of the tournament [s:cricket-australia-profile]. In the final he dismissed Brendan McCullum in the first over [s:cricket-australia-profile].

## T20 cricket and retirement
