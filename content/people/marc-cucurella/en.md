Marc Cucurella left Chelsea for Real Madrid in June 2026, on a contract that runs to 30 June 2032 [s:peninsula-real-madrid-confirm] [s:geosuper-cucurella-2032]. The club did not disclose a fee [s:geosuper-cucurella-2032], so the figures in circulation come from press reports, which are set out below. The left-back was born on 22 July 1998 in Alella, Spain [s:laliga-five-things], and he has been capped by Spain [s:theanalyst-national]. Counting from that date, he was 28 on 26 October 2026 [s:laliga-five-things] [s:fotmob-profile].

## From Alella to the Camp Nou

Cucurella started playing futsal in his native Alella at the age of four [s:laliga-five-things]. His first formal football step came at RCD Espanyol. His father, Oscar, took him to a trial at the club's academy [s:laliga-five-things]. Barcelona scouts Guillermo Amor and Albert Puig were impressed by his display in a match against CF Damm, and he joined the Camp Nou in 2012 [s:laliga-five-things].

He names David Luiz and Carles Puyol as his footballing role models [s:laliga-five-things]. His LaLiga debut came on 25 September 2018. He played the full 90 minutes in SD Eibar's 1-0 away win over Espanyol, the club that had first given him a trial [s:laliga-five-things].

## Chelsea and Spain

Chelsea signed him from Brighton & Hove Albion on a six-year contract [s:chelsea-cucurella-becomes-a-blue]. Chelsea's own summer review says he left for Real Madrid after four successful seasons at Stamford Bridge [s:chelsea-summer-transfers-2026].

For Spain, The Analyst's national-team page lists 35 caps, one goal and four assists [s:theanalyst-national]. That page carries no date, so those totals should be checked against the federation's own records before they are quoted as current.

## The move to Real Madrid

Real Madrid announced the agreement on 15 June 2026 [s:hardtackle-official-cucurella], and The Peninsula carries the same date [s:peninsula-real-madrid-confirm]. The Hard Tackle's headline calls the move official [s:hardtackle-official-cucurella]. The Peninsula reports a deal for the next six seasons, until June 30, 2032, and quotes this line on the deal with Chelsea: “Real Madrid and Chelsea have reached an agreement for the transfer of the player Marc Cucurella” [s:peninsula-real-madrid-confirm].

LaLiga's transfer summary for the 2026-27 season lists his signing alongside Yan Diomandé, Bernardo Silva, Konaté, Dumfries and Carlos Espí. ## Fee figures

No official fee has been published, and the press reports are not identical:

- AFP, reported by the New Straits Times, gave “a fee reported to be €55 million (US$63.8 million)” [s:nst-real-sign-cucurella].
- Fabrizio Romano, cited by The Hard Tackle, reported a base fee of €55 million and €5 million in add-ons [s:hardtackle-official-cucurella].
- GeoSuper said international media reported an initial €55 million, with a further €5 million potentially payable in performance-related add-ons [s:geosuper-cucurella-2032].

The €55 million base figure appears in all three reports. Only some of them include the €5 million in add-ons, and Real Madrid did not disclose the add-ons [s:geosuper-cucurella-2032]. The sources reviewed do not say which performance targets would trigger them, so readers should treat €60 million as a possible total rather than a confirmed one.

## First matches in Madrid

In late August 2026 he played in two LaLiga matches for Real Madrid. On 26 August he came on in the 45th minute to replace Álvaro Carreras in the club's 4-1 win over Real Sociedad [s:laliga-sociedad-match]. Four days later he started the 4-0 win over Málaga [s:laliga-malaga-match].

LaLiga's club statistics page lists him with 10 matches, six starts, four appearances as a substitute and 655 minutes, along with 390 passes [s:laliga-real-madrid-stats]. The page does not say which dates those figures cover, so they are best read as a snapshot.

On 25 October 2026, Real Madrid won 2-1 against Barcelona in El Clásico. LaLiga's match commentary logs four fouls by Cucurella in the first 38 minutes, and two corners he conceded, at 61 and 83 minutes [s:laliga-clasico-2026].
