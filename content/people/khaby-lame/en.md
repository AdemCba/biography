Khaby Lame became the most-followed person on TikTok in June 2022, and he did it with very few words. Lame answered other users' overcomplicated life-hack clips with a silent video that showed a simpler fix [s:guinness-tiktok-most-followed-2022]. Since then he has added brand deals, a UNICEF role, a sports-event ambassadorship in Senegal and a company sale worth $975 million that has drawn scrutiny from securities experts [s:bloomberg-975m-deal] [s:forbes-deal-red-flags].

## From a factory line to TikTok

Lame was born on 9 March 2000 in Senegal [s:unicef-goodwill-profile]. Sources differ on the town. UNICEF's profile names Ziguinchor, while the Ghana News Agency says he was born in Mbacké [s:unicef-goodwill-profile] [s:gna-youth-olympics-ambassador].

He worked at a factory near Turin until the pandemic cost him the job [s:unicef-goodwill-profile]. Guinness World Records reports that he moved back into his parents' home in Turin at the same time [s:guinness-tiktok-most-followed-2022]. He started posting on TikTok after losing that job in March 2020 [s:unicef-goodwill-profile]. His first videos showed him dancing, watching video games and using filters. They drew few views [s:guinness-tiktok-most-followed-2022].

## Silent replies and the 2021 climb

The breakthrough came in November 2020. Another user posted a complicated life-hack video, and Lame replied without speaking, showing a simpler method [s:guinness-tiktok-most-followed-2022].

By April 2021 he had passed Gianluca Vacchi as the most-followed Italian on TikTok [s:guinness-tiktok-most-followed-2022]. By July 2021 he was the second most-followed user on the platform overall, and the top male account after he overtook Zach King [s:guinness-tiktok-most-followed-2022]. Guinness compared his growth rates. He gained followers at an average of three million a month, against one million a month for Charli D'Amelio [s:guinness-tiktok-most-followed-2022].

## Passing Charli D'Amelio

On 23 June 2022 Guinness World Records reported that Lame had overtaken Charli D'Amelio, who had held the top spot since March 2020 [s:guinness-tiktok-most-followed-2022]. The article gives his follower count as 142.8 million at the time of writing, but a different figure, 142.6 million, appears in the page's own description. The exact number on that day is therefore not settled by this one source [s:guinness-tiktok-most-followed-2022].

## Italian citizenship and brand work

In August 2022 Lame became an Italian citizen at a ceremony in Chivasso, Euronews reported on 19 August 2022 [s:euronews-italian-citizenship-2022].

Brand work followed the rise. Guinness lists a partnership with Hugo Boss, a collaboration with Juventus F.C. and a meeting with Lionel Messi among his milestones [s:guinness-tiktok-most-followed-2022]. Hugo Boss's own page calls him a global brand ambassador for BOSS and describes the "BOSS x Khaby" capsule as a collection with a next-gen creator [s:hugo-boss-boss-x-khaby]. The capsule included all-black t-shirts, hoodies and varsity jackets with leather details, and a logo based on his likeness. Hugo Boss says it was released during Milan Men's Fashion Week [s:hugo-boss-boss-x-khaby]. The same page says he co-created a Khaby Lame doll [s:hugo-boss-boss-x-khaby].

TIME placed him on its TIME100 Creators 2025 list in the Titans category, under the name "Khabane Lame" [s:time100-creators-2025].

## UNICEF and the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympics

UNICEF named Lame its newest Goodwill Ambassador after a visit to Senegal [s:un-messengers-peace]. The UN and UNICEF pages date the appointment to 30 and 31 January 2025 [s:un-messengers-peace] [s:unicef-press-release]. During the trip he met children living on the street, toured inclusive schools, joined workshops for out-of-school girls in STEM and supported youth-led climate action [s:un-messengers-peace].

UNICEF says his work will focus on education and skills, empowering girls, protection from violence and abuse, and access to healthcare, nutrition and a healthy environment [s:unicef-press-release]. He said: "It's a true honour to be appointed as a UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador" [s:unicef-press-release]. UNICEF's executive director, Catherine Russell, called him "a passionate and powerful advocate for children and young people all over the world" [s:unicef-press-release].

In March 2026 he was appointed an ambassador for the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games. The Ghana News Agency reported the appointment on 1 April 2026 and said it was made on 26 March in Dakar [s:gna-youth-olympics-ambassador]. The role includes promoting the Games among young people in Africa and abroad, and taking part in events such as the torch relay across Senegal [s:gna-youth-olympics-ambassador]. Lame said: "This commitment reflects my desire to support young African talent" [s:gna-youth-olympics-ambassador].

## The $975 million deal and its scrutiny

In January 2026 Rich Sparkle Holdings agreed to buy Lame's holding company, Step Distinctive Limited, for $975 million. Forbes reports that the buyer is Hong Kong-based and that the deal was all stock [s:forbes-deal-red-flags]. Bloomberg describes the buyer as a Nasdaq-listed financial services firm and describes a three-year package of exclusive worldwide rights to Lame's brand. Bloomberg does not state how the price was paid [s:bloomberg-975m-deal].

Rich Sparkle said Lame would be a "controlling shareholder" and that his likeness would be replicated by an "AI Digital Twin" [s:forbes-deal-red-flags]. Forbes calculated that his stake was worth about $6.6 billion at the peak of the buyer's stock on 15 January 2026 [s:forbes-deal-red-flags]. The stock later fell as low as $41 a share, about 77% below that peak [s:forbes-deal-red-flags]. Forbes also said it was unclear whether Lame had sold any shares, because foreign entities have limited disclosure requirements [s:forbes-deal-red-flags].

Securities attorney Brenda Hamilton said "it's very suspect" and called the stock swings "a red flag" [s:forbes-deal-red-flags]. Short seller Jim Chanos said the situation "looks completely like a Chinese stock promotion" [s:forbes-deal-red-flags]. Lame and Rich Sparkle did not respond to Forbes' requests for comment [s:forbes-deal-red-flags].

## The 2025 ICE detention

On 6 June 2025, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) detained Lame at Harry Reid International Airport in Las Vegas, according to NPR's report of ICE's statement [s:npr-ice-detention]. ICE said he had entered the United States legally on 30 April and then stayed longer than his visa allowed [s:npr-ice-detention]. ICE also said it granted him voluntary departure on 6 June, that he has since left the country, and that such departures are discretionary and do not involve a removal order [s:npr-ice-detention].

NPR reported that requests for comment from Lame and his management company were not immediately returned, and that his TikTok and Instagram accounts had not mentioned the detention [s:npr-ice-detention]. The account in this section comes from ICE, and Lame's side of it is not in the sources used here.
