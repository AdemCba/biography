William Devane is best known to many viewers as James Heller in 24, and as President John F. Kennedy in The Missiles of October [s:plex-william-devane] [s:gulfnews-devane-24]. He has played presidents on Stargate SG-1, and the Secretary of State on The West Wing [s:gulfnews-devane-24].

The sources disagree on his birth year. The Plex and Cinegraph headers give 5 September 1939, but the biography text on both pages gives 5 September 1937 [s:plex-william-devane] [s:cinegraph-william-devane]. None of the pages checked for this biography settles the difference, so this page gives neither a birth year nor an age. Cinegraph lists him as from Albany, New York, USA [s:cinegraph-william-devane].

## From the New York Shakespeare Festival to the screen

He started in theatre with the New York Shakespeare Festival, where Plex says he performed in 15 plays [s:plex-william-devane]. The same page lists him as an actor, director and writer [s:plex-william-devane].

## Film roles from the 1970s to the 2010s

He played a ruthless agent in Marathon Man, released in 1976 [s:gulfnews-devane-24]. He was a lawyer in McCabe & Mrs. Miller (1971) and Major Charles Rane in Rolling Thunder (1977), and he was Arthur Adamson in Family Plot (1976) [s:plex-william-devane]. In the 1979 miniseries From Here to Eternity he was Natalie Wood's love interest [s:gulfnews-devane-24].

In Space Cowboys (2000) he played Eugene Davis, and in Payback (1999) Carter [s:cinegraph-william-devane]. He was Dr Kramer in Hollow Man (2000) and Williams in Interstellar (2014) [s:cinegraph-william-devane]. He was Tom Wetherly in Testament (1983) and Mike Leak in The Bad News Bears in Breaking Training (1977) [s:plex-william-devane].

In 50 to 1, released in 2014, he plays Leonard Doc Blach [s:cinegraph-william-devane]. His character, Dr Leonard Blach, is one of the owners of the thoroughbred Mine That Bird, Gulf News says [s:gulfnews-devane-24].

## Two Emmy nominations

The Television Academy records two Emmy nominations for Devane and no wins [s:tv-academy-bio]. The first, in 1975, was in the category for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Special Program for The Missiles of October, the ABC production in which he played Kennedy [s:tv-academy-bio] [s:plex-william-devane]. The Gulf News piece links that nomination to his star-making turn as Kennedy, and quotes Devane saying the role “certainly typecast me, there's no question” [s:gulfnews-devane-24]. The second, in 1976, was in the category for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited Series for Fear on Trial, where he played John Henry Faulk [s:tv-academy-bio] [s:plex-william-devane].

## Knots Landing and Revenge

The Gulf News piece calls Greg Sumner, his Knots Landing role, a villainous one, and dates his run on the CBS soap from 1983 to 1993 [s:gulfnews-devane-24]. Knots Landing is also on Plex's known-for list [s:plex-william-devane].

In December 2011 Michael Fairman TV reported that he had been cast as Edward Grayson, the father of Conrad Grayson and patriarch of the Hamptons clan in ABC's Revenge [s:michaelfairman-revenge-2011]. TVmaze lists him in the episodes broadcast on 8 February 2012 and 15 February 2012 [s:tvmaze-edward-grayson].

## Presidents and later television roles

Christopher Nolan's 2012 film The Dark Knight Rises also gave him a president to play [s:gulfnews-devane-24].

He was Dr Dix in the Jesse Stone films from 2006 to 2015, Dean Sanderson Sr. in The Grinder in 2015 and Peters in Psych in 2006 [s:plex-william-devane]. Falling from the Sky: Flight 174 and Jesse Stone: Lost in Paradise are on his known-for list [s:plex-william-devane].

## James Heller in 24

Heller was introduced as a tough, no-nonsense Secretary of State, and Devane reprises the role in 24: Live Another Day, Fox's reboot of its Emmy Award-winning 2001-2010 series [s:gulfnews-devane-24]. The same article also calls Heller Secretary of Defence on 24, so its job titles do not agree [s:gulfnews-devane-24]. For the new series, the producers gave Heller early-stage Alzheimer's [s:gulfnews-devane-24]. The Gulf News article was last updated on 15 September 2018 [s:gulfnews-devane-24].
