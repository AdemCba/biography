Matt Rife is 31 years old as of 3 November 2026. He was born on 10 September 1995 in Columbus, Ohio [s:tmdb-rife] [s:martincid-rife]. He is a stand-up comedian and actor who spent years on small stages and a MTV improv show before short crowd-work clips on TikTok turned him into an arena act [s:today-natural-selection] [s:bww-lucid].

## Early life in central Ohio

Rife was born in Columbus and raised in North Lewisburg, a village in central Ohio, with periods in New Albany and Mount Vernon [s:martincid-rife]. The Movie Database also gives Columbus as his birthplace, while its biography text describes him as being from North Lewisburg [s:tmdb-rife]. The two accounts do not conflict so much as describe the city of birth and the town he grew up in.

The family details that circulate online come mostly from low-quality profile sites, so this page leaves them out. What is documented is how he started. When he was 14, a teacher mentioned a talent show at his high school, a friend pushed him to enter, and he did [s:martincid-rife]. A year later he was already working professionally, on Midwest club stages next to comics roughly twice his age [s:martincid-rife].

## Wild 'N Out, MTV and early television

His television work began when he was nineteen [s:martincid-rife]. In 2015 he joined the cast of *Wild 'N Out*, the improv and battle-rap series, and it heads the list of TV credits in Netflix's *Lucid* announcement [s:martincid-rife] [s:bww-lucid]. In 2017 he co-hosted MTV's short-lived reboot of *TRL* and competed on *The Challenge: Champs vs. Stars* [s:martincid-rife].

The next years were a run of small steps. In 2018 he played in the slasher film *Black Pumpkin* [s:martincid-rife]. In 2019 he reached the semi-final showcase of NBC's *Bring the Funny* and was eliminated in week eight [s:martincid-rife]. His acting credits also include guest parts on *Brooklyn Nine-Nine* and *Fresh Off the Boat*, and *That '90s Show* [s:bww-lucid]. None of this brought wide fame. For most of the decade he was a working comic on the club circuit.

In 2021 he put out *Only Fans*, the first of three specials he released himself on YouTube [s:martincid-rife] [s:bww-lucid]. In December 2022 he signed with the talent agency Creative Artists Agency [s:martincid-rife].

## TikTok and the crowd-work boom

The change came through short videos. From the summer of 2022 Rife posted crowd-work clips on TikTok, where he talks to audience members and improvises jokes from what they say [s:today-natural-selection]. By November 2023 he had more than 18 million followers on the app [s:today-natural-selection]. A Netflix announcement for *Lucid*, as reported by BroadwayWorld, says his tour sold more than 600,000 tickets in under 48 hours [s:bww-lucid].

His self-released specials followed the same pattern. *Matthew Steven Rife* came out in 2023, and the crowd-work special *Walking Red Flag* arrived in June 2023 [s:martincid-rife] [s:bww-lucid]. By August 2024 *Walking Red Flag* had more than 13 million views [s:netflix-lucid].

## Netflix: Natural Selection and Lucid

*Matt Rife: Natural Selection* was released on Netflix on 15 November 2023 and was filmed in Washington, D.C. [s:today-natural-selection] [s:netflix-natural-selection]. Netflix describes it as a stand-up special in which he covers topics from crystal-related grievances to clashes with social media critics [s:netflix-natural-selection]. BroadwayWorld's report of Netflix's *Lucid* announcement says it drew more than 10.3 million views in its first two weeks and reached the Top 10 in 42 countries [s:bww-lucid].

The special also set off the biggest controversy of his career so far. Its opening sequence included a joke about a hostess with a black eye at a Baltimore restaurant, and critics on X called it disrespectful [s:today-natural-selection]. Rife later said the joke was about domestic violence [s:today-natural-selection]. On 20 November 2023 he posted an "official apology" on Instagram Stories whose link led to a website selling helmets for people with special needs, which many users criticised as a further misstep [s:today-natural-selection]. In a Variety interview the day before the release he had said the special was aimed at men too, saying "I would argue this special is way more for guys" [s:today-natural-selection].

*Matt Rife: Lucid - A Crowd Work Special* followed on 13 August 2024. It was filmed at The Comedy Zone in Charlotte, North Carolina, and Netflix presents it as its first crowd-work special [s:netflix-lucid]. It was part of a two-special deal with the streamer [s:bww-lucid]. In it Rife tells the audience they are "equally as at fault for how this goes as I am" and pushes back on critics who call his style easy [s:netflix-lucid].

## Tours and ticket numbers

Rife's first big headline run was the ProbleMATTic World Tour, which, per Netflix's *Lucid* announcement, sold more than 600,000 tickets in under 48 hours [s:bww-lucid] [s:martincid-rife]. The same announcement says he sold out four shows at the Dolby Theatre, five nights at the Mohegan Sun Arena and the Hollywood Bowl, and that he was the youngest stand-up comedian to do so [s:bww-lucid]. AOL reported that his 2024 tour grossed $60 million worldwide across 256 shows and 733,000 tickets, but it did not name a source for those numbers, so they should be treated as unconfirmed [s:aol-net-worth].

The next run, the Stay Golden Tour, played arenas and included his first headline show at Madison Square Garden [s:martincid-rife]. The current Stay Golden World Tour is produced by Live Nation [s:oklahoman-tour] [s:red-rocks-stay-golden]. In November 2025 the Oklahoman reported that added dates brought the 2026 leg to more than 60 shows in over ten countries, with some markets getting a 360-degree, in-the-round stage [s:oklahoman-tour]. Dates include Red Rocks Amphitheatre near Denver on 18 October 2026 [s:red-rocks-stay-golden] and a final show at Paycom Center in Oklahoma City on 29 December 2026 [s:oklahoman-tour].

## The Annabelle doll and the Warren museum

In August 2025 Rife announced on TikTok that he and the content creator and ghost hunter Elton Castee had bought the Connecticut home and Occult Museum of the paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren, in Monroe [s:boston-annabelle]. The pair said they planned overnight stays and museum tours, and that they would act as "legal guardians and caretakers" of about 750 items, including the Annabelle doll, for at least five years [s:boston-annabelle].

The wording of the deal was later disputed. Tony Spera, the Warrens' son-in-law, told TODAY.com that he and his wife still own the doll and artifacts and leased them to Rife and Castee for five years, adding that "we have no plans to ever 'sell' the artifacts" [s:aol-net-worth]. A Rife representative had confirmed the arrangement as legitimate on 2 August [s:aol-net-worth].
