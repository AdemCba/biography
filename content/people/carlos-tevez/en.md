Carlos Tevez made his Boca Juniors debut in 2001, at 17, against Talleres de Córdoba [s:mancity-legends-tevez] [s:juventus-tevez-profile]. Juventus's profile, written when he joined the club in 2013, calls him an Argentine frontman [s:juventus-tevez-profile]. The club is now preparing a farewell match for him at La Bombonera, Boca's stadium, on Sunday 20 December [s:canal26-tevez-farewell-date]. The start time, ticket sales and guest list are not final yet [s:canal26-tevez-farewell-date].

## From Ciudadela to Corinthians

Tevez was born Carlos Alberto Martínez on 5 February 1984 in Ciudadela, Buenos Aires [s:mancity-legends-tevez] [s:wikidata-tevez]. He grew up in Fuerte Apache, where he got the nickname "El Apache" [s:mancity-legends-tevez]. He was adopted by his mother's sister and her husband, Segundo Tevez, and later took the Tevez surname [s:mancity-legends-tevez].

His first spell at Boca Juniors ran from August 2001 to June 2005 [s:fotmob-tevez]. He joined Corinthians in January 2005 [s:fotmob-tevez]. Juventus's profile says he captained the side to the Brazilian domestic title and was named Brazilian Player of the Year in 2005 [s:juventus-tevez-profile]. Manchester City's page adds that he was the first non-Brazilian to win that award [s:mancity-legends-tevez].

West Ham United followed, from August 2006 to June 2007 [s:fotmob-tevez]. Juventus's profile notes that he scored a late winner against Manchester United during that season [s:juventus-tevez-profile].

## Two seasons at Manchester United

Manchester United signed him on loan from Media Sports Investments in August 2007 [s:mufcinfo-tevez]. He made his debut on 15 August 2007, in a 1-1 draw at Portsmouth [s:mufcinfo-tevez]. Across two seasons he made 99 appearances and scored 34 goals [s:mufcinfo-tevez] [s:futdados-tevez-stats]. The loan ran until July 2009 [s:mufcinfo-tevez]. The club's player archive lists the Premier League and the European Cup in both seasons, and the League Cup in 2008-09 [s:mufcinfo-tevez].

## Manchester City, Juventus and the return to Boca

Manchester City's legends page gives his City career as 2009 to 2013, with 125 appearances and 74 goals [s:mancity-legends-tevez]. He joined on 14 July 2009 [s:mancity-legends-tevez]. He scored his first goal for the club on 27 August 2009, in a 2-0 win over Crystal Palace [s:mancity-legends-tevez]. He was named Premier League Player of the Month in December 2009 [s:mancity-legends-tevez], and Roberto Mancini made him captain [s:mancity-legends-tevez].

The legends page also records a suspension during the 2011-12 season, after which he resumed training on 14 February 2012 [s:mancity-legends-tevez].

Juventus announced his signing in June 2013, when he moved from Manchester City [s:juventus-tevez-profile]. FotMob dates his Juventus spell from July 2013 to June 2015 [s:fotmob-tevez].

He returned to Boca Juniors in 2015 [s:mancity-legends-tevez]. That second period was not unbroken. FotMob lists Shanghai Shenhua from January 2017 to January 2018, then a return to Boca from January 2018 to June 2021, on a free transfer [s:fotmob-tevez].

## Argentina and the 2004 Olympics

Tevez played for Argentina from January 2004 to December 2015 [s:fotmob-tevez]. FotMob and Futdados both count 76 matches and 13 goals [s:fotmob-tevez] [s:futdados-tevez-stats]. FotMob warns that its counts before 2006 may be inexact [s:fotmob-tevez]. Juventus's 2013 profile gave 64 appearances at the time it was written [s:juventus-tevez-profile].

Argentina won Olympic gold at the 2004 Athens Games [s:mancity-legends-tevez] [s:juventus-tevez-profile]. Juventus's profile says he scored eight goals in six matches there [s:juventus-tevez-profile]. He also played at the 2006 and 2010 World Cups [s:juventus-tevez-profile].

## Titles

Juventus's profile lists a Brazilian domestic title with Corinthians, Olympic gold in 2004, Premier League and Champions League titles in 2007-08, and Premier League, Club World Cup and League Cup titles in 2008-09 [s:juventus-tevez-profile]. Manchester City's page lists the FA Cup in 2010-11, and the Premier League and Community Shield in 2011-12 [s:mancity-legends-tevez]. The Juventus profile calls the 2011-12 Premier League the club's first top-flight title in 44 years [s:juventus-tevez-profile]. This page does not list his Boca Juniors honours, because no source checked here gives them.

## The farewell match at La Bombonera

Canal 26 reports that Tevez announced the date in a post shared between his Instagram account and the official account of the event [s:canal26-tevez-farewell-date]. The match is set for Sunday 20 December at La Bombonera, and the start time is still to be confirmed [s:canal26-tevez-farewell-date]. Ticket prices and sales had not been announced when the report was published, and Canal 26 says the organisers will publish them in the coming days [s:canal26-tevez-farewell-date].

Canal 26 says the guest list is not yet defined, and names Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo among possible guests [s:canal26-tevez-farewell-date].

Infobae published a first list of names on 29 October 2026 [s:infobae-tevez-farewell-2026]. According to that report, Tevez confirmed in an interview with Rio Ferdinand that Lionel Messi and Juan Román Riquelme will attend [s:infobae-tevez-farewell-2026]. Infobae also says former players and internationals have told him they will be there [s:infobae-tevez-farewell-2026]. Cristiano Ronaldo's attendance is still awaited, and Tevez said he would contact him himself [s:infobae-tevez-farewell-2026]. These are press reports, not an official guest list.
