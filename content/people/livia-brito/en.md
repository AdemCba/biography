Livia Brito has played a lead in a new telenovela almost every year since her television debut in 2010. Born on 21 July 1986, she is 40 years old in November 2026 [s:sensacine-biografia] [s:periodico-cubano-bio]. Her official site describes her as a Cuban-Mexican actress, model, entrepreneur and influencer [s:livia-brito-official]. Her recent work includes Mujer de nadie in 2022 and Amanecer in 2025 [s:livia-brito-filmography].

## From Cuba to Mexico

SensaCine and Periódico Cubano both give Havana as her birthplace [s:sensacine-biografia] [s:periodico-cubano-bio]. Busca Biografías instead names Ciego de Ávila, so the sources disagree on this point [s:buscabiografias]. She is the daughter of the actor Rolando Brito and the ballet dancer Gertrudis Pestana [s:sensacine-biografia] [s:periodico-cubano-bio].

At 13 she moved with her family to Mexico [s:sensacine-biografia]. Her father opened a Cuban restaurant there. She worked as a waitress [s:sensacine-biografia]. She later modelled to pay for her business administration studies [s:sensacine-biografia]. In 2009 she represented Mexico in the World Banana Queen contest [s:sensacine-biografia]. Busca Biografías adds that she finished as first finalist and won the Miss Photogenic title [s:buscabiografias].

## Debut at Televisa

In 2010 she trained at Televisa's Centro de Educación Artística (CEA) [s:sensacine-biografia]. The same year she debuted in the telenovela Triunfo del amor, playing Fernanda Sandoval [s:livia-brito-filmography] [s:periodico-cubano-bio]. Her official site lists the production as a Televisa one and gives its run as 2010 to 2011 [s:livia-brito-filmography].

Abismo de pasión followed in 2012 [s:livia-brito-filmography]. She was Paloma González [s:livia-brito-filmography]. Her first starring role came with De que te quiero, te quiero in 2013 and 2014, as Natalia García [s:livia-brito-filmography]. Periódico Cubano lists her as the lead of that production [s:periodico-cubano-bio].

In Muchacha italiana viene a casarse (2014 to 2015) she played Fiorella Bianchi opposite José Ron [s:livia-brito-filmography] [s:periodico-cubano-bio]. The telenovela was shot partly in Italy [s:livia-brito-filmography]. Periódico Cubano reports that she won the ACE award for best actress in 2016 for this role [s:periodico-cubano-bio]. The same article says she was named best theatre revelation by the Asociación de Críticos y Periodistas Teatrales in 2012 [s:periodico-cubano-bio].

## Stage and screen

Brito also worked in theatre. In El cartero (2012 to 2013) she played Beatriz González and shared the stage with Ignacio López Tarso and Helena Rojo [s:livia-brito-filmography]. She later appeared in Los bonobos (2015 to 2016) as Ángeles [s:livia-brito-filmography]. Periódico Cubano also lists the bioseries Por siempre Joan Sebastian (2016) among her credits [s:periodico-cubano-bio].

Her film credits include Volando bajo (2014), where she played Ana Bertha, and La dictadura perfecta (2014), where she played Jazmín [s:livia-brito-filmography]. She played María in Juan Apóstol, el más amado (2017), which was shot in Spain [s:livia-brito-filmography]. In Infelices para siempre (2023) she played Carmen Pelusi [s:livia-brito-filmography].

## Leading roles from 2017

La piloto (2017 to 2018) gave her the part of Yolanda Cadena [s:livia-brito-filmography]. She then played Regina Villaseñor in Médicos, línea de vida (2019 to 2020) and Fernanda Linares in La desalmada in 2021 [s:livia-brito-filmography].

Mujer de nadie came next. It is a Televisa telenovela that retells Amarte es mi pecado [s:publimetro-mujer-de-nadie]. She plays Lucía Arizmendi, opposite Marcus Ornellas as Fernando Ortega, and Giselle González produced it [s:publimetro-mujer-de-nadie]. It premiered on Las Estrellas in Mexico on 13 June 2022 and on Univision in the United States on 28 June 2022 [s:publimetro-mujer-de-nadie]. A Salvadoran newspaper reported in May 2022 that she was recording it in Mexico and was 35 at the time [s:el-salvador-2022].

In Minas de pasión (2023 to 2024) her official filmography credits her with two parts, Emilia Sánchez and Victoria Alcázar [s:livia-brito-filmography].

## Amanecer

Amanecer was produced by Juan Osorio and began on Las Estrellas on 7 July 2025 at 21:30, and it is also available on ViX [s:televisaunivision-amanecer]. Fernando Colunga plays Leonel Carranza and Brito plays Alba Palacios [s:televisaunivision-amanecer]. According to TelevisaUnivision, Leonel targets Alba's family with his anger and forces her to marry him, and over time their resentment turns into love [s:televisaunivision-amanecer]. Her official filmography gives the full name of the character as Alba Palacios Salvatierra [s:livia-brito-filmography].

## Beyond acting

Her official site presents a wider business. It lists a podcast, Esencia Livia, in which she talks about relationships, emotions and mental health, and a media kit for brand campaigns [s:livia-brito-official]. The site states a combined audience of more than 30 million followers, a figure it gives itself [s:livia-brito-official]. Her filmography page lists the podcast, available on YouTube, as a 2026 addition to her work [s:livia-brito-filmography].
