Mats Hummels scored the second goal in Germany's opening 4-0 win over Portugal at the 2014 World Cup, and he also scored the 1-0 winner in the quarter-final against France [s:dfb-hummels-retirement]. Germany went on to win the tournament in Brazil [s:dfb-hummels-retirement]. That summer made him a world champion, but most of his club career ran between two clubs, Borussia Dortmund and Bayern Munich, with a final season in Italy.

## Youth at Bayern and the move to Dortmund

Hummels came up through Bayern Munich's youth teams. The database's table also shows a first senior spell at Bayern Munich from 1 July 2006 to 2 January 2008 [s:dfb-datencenter-hummels]. The German federation's report calls Bayern Munich his home club [s:dfb-hummels-retirement]. The DFB player database lists him with the club's under-17 side from 2003 and its under-19 side from 2005, and with the reserve team, Bayern Munich II, from May 2006 [s:dfb-datencenter-hummels]. His first move away from Bayern came in January 2008, when he joined Borussia Dortmund [s:dfb-datencenter-hummels]. The database records that Dortmund spell as running from 3 January 2008 to 30 June 2016 [s:dfb-datencenter-hummels].

When his retirement was announced in April 2025, the club said one of the greatest players in its history was stepping away, and its managing director, Hans-Joachim Watzke, said Hummels was one of the biggest players Dortmund has ever developed [s:bundesliga-hummels-retirement].

## Back to Bayern, then Dortmund again

Hummels returned to Bayern Munich on 1 July 2016 and stayed until 30 June 2019 [s:dfb-datencenter-hummels]. He then went back to Dortmund for a second spell that the database lists from 1 July 2019 to 30 June 2024 [s:dfb-datencenter-hummels]. The German federation's report on his retirement credits him with five German championships and three DFB-Pokal wins across his club career [s:dfb-hummels-retirement].

Taken together, the database dates show five seasons in his second Dortmund spell, which ended on 30 June 2024 [s:dfb-datencenter-hummels].

## A final season in Rome

After leaving Dortmund, Hummels moved to Italy. AS Roma announced the signing of the former Dortmund defender on 4 September 2024 [s:lanacion-roma-2024]. The transfer details were not disclosed by the club. The one-season length of the deal came from Italian media reports, as relayed by the Reuters story [s:lanacion-roma-2024]. The Roma spell was the last club stage of his career [s:bundesliga-hummels-retirement].

## Germany career and the big tournaments

Hummels made his senior debut for Germany on 13 May 2010, in a 3-0 win over Malta in Aachen under coach Joachim Löw [s:dfb-hummels-retirement]. Before that he had won the European Under-21 Championship with Germany's U21 side in Sweden in 2009 [s:dfb-hummels-retirement].

Germany reached the semi-finals of Euro 2012 and Euro 2016 with him in the squad [s:dfb-hummels-retirement]. The 2018 World Cup in Russia ended for the team in the group stage, and Euro 2020 ended in the round of 16 [s:dfb-hummels-retirement].

His national-team record is 78 caps and five goals [s:dfb-hummels-retirement]. His final appearance came on 21 November 2023 in Vienna, under coach Julian Nagelsmann [s:dfb-hummels-retirement].

## Retirement in 2025

Hummels announced his retirement on 4 April 2025 [s:dfb-hummels-retirement]. The DFB report gives his age as 36 at the time and says he would end his successful career in the summer [s:dfb-hummels-retirement]. Bundesliga.com carried Borussia Dortmund's reaction that one of the greatest players in the club's history was stepping away in the summer [s:bundesliga-hummels-retirement].

In early October 2026 Bentley announced Hummels as a brand ambassador on a two-year collaboration focused on German-speaking markets [s:dupont-bentley-hummels].
