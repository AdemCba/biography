Adrián Lastra kept going through rejection. He has said that of about 50 castings, 47 ended in a no [s:diezminutos-interview]. He now has a Goya nomination, a long run in Spanish musical theatre and the role of Pedro in the Antena 3 series *Velvet* [s:valenciaplaza-cinemajove].

## Vallecas and the first musicals

Lastra was born on 26 February 1984 in Palomeras Bajas, a neighbourhood of Vallecas in Madrid [s:diezminutos-interview] [s:formulatv-profile]. He has two siblings, Rocío and Raúl. His father and sister sing flamenco and play guitar [s:diezminutos-interview]. He says he decided to become a singer after watching the first episodes of the talent show *Operación Triunfo* [s:diezminutos-interview]. He took singing lessons with the opera singer Victoria Manso and acting classes with Patricia Ferro [s:diezminutos-interview].

In 2003 he won a pop-rock contest, which led to casting calls for musicals [s:diezminutos-interview]. Before his stage breakthrough he sang with the group La Década Prodigiosa [s:diezminutos-interview]. In 2004 he appeared in *Broadway Millenium* [s:formulatv-profile]. The next year he joined *Hoy no me puedo levantar*, a musical built on songs by Mecano. It opened at the Teatro Rialto in Madrid and toured until 2009, and he played Mario, Colate and Panchi [s:formulatv-profile]. Lastra later said that the show brought him more recognition than his first television hit [s:cinemajove-interview]. He returned to it at the Teatro Coliseum as Colate, and that role earned him the 2017 Premio Gran Vía for best male cover [s:formulatv-profile].

Other musicals followed: *40, el musical*, in which he played a journalism student for two seasons from 2009, and *Más de cien mentiras*, based on songs by Joaquín Sabina [s:formulatv-profile] [s:valenciaplaza-cinemajove].

## Television: from Lalola to Velvet

His early television work was small: an episode of *Los hombres de Paco* and one of *Yo soy Bea* in 2006 [s:formulatv-profile]. In *Lalola* (Antena 3, 2008 to 2009) he played Juan Manuel, known as Boogie [s:formulatv-profile] [s:cinemajove-interview].

The role that most people know him for is Pedro Infantes in *Velvet*, an Antena 3 series [s:formulatv-profile] [s:valenciaplaza-cinemajove]. FormulaTV dates his run to 2013 to 2016 in one section and 2014 to 2016 in another, so the exact start year is not settled in that source [s:formulatv-profile]. A sequel, *Velvet Colección*, ran on Movistar+ from 2017 to 2019 with Lastra again as Pedro [s:formulatv-profile]. For the original series he received the Unión de Actores award for best supporting actor, which FormulaTV dates to 2016 [s:formulatv-profile] [s:valenciaplaza-cinemajove]. In an interview he credited the series with changing his life [s:cinemajove-interview].

More recent television credits include *Jaguar* on Netflix (from 2021, as Sordo), *Sin límites* on Amazon Prime Video (2022, as Captain Mendoza) and *Camilo Superstar* on Atresplayer (from 2023, as Teddy Bautista) [s:formulatv-profile] [s:ecartelera-profile].

## Film and the Goya nomination

His first film appearance was a brief part in *Fuga de cerebros* in 2009. He described it as a character with two lines [s:formulatv-profile] [s:cinemajove-interview]. The breakthrough came with *Primos* (2011), directed by Daniel Sánchez Arévalo, in which he played José Miguel, a hypochondriac [s:ecartelera-curiosidades]. Lastra called working with Sánchez Arévalo "the biggest gift of my life" [s:cinemajove-interview]. The role brought a nomination for the Goya for best new actor, announced in January 2012 for the ceremony of 19 February [s:sensacine-goya-2012] [s:ecartelera-curiosidades]. One 2015 interview says he won the award, but the other sources, including eCartelera, describe a nomination, and that is how it is recorded here [s:diezminutos-interview] [s:formulatv-profile].

Other films include *Fuga de cerebros 2* (2011), *De chica en chica* (2015), *Toc Toc* (2017, as Otto), *Litus* and *Si yo fuera rico* (both 2019), *Hasta que la boda nos separe* (2020), *La manzana de oro* (2022) and *Matusalén* (2023) [s:ecartelera-profile]. In 2017 the Cinema Jove festival in Valencia gave him its "Un futuro de cine" award, with a retrospective of his work [s:valenciaplaza-cinemajove] [s:cinemajove-interview].

## Theatre, dance shows and height

In 2015 he played King George VI in the stage version of *El discurso del rey*, directed by Magüi Mira, at the Teatro Español in Madrid [s:diezminutos-interview] [s:formulatv-profile].
