## Ten La Liga titles and the 2012 season

Between 2004 and 2021 he won 10 La Liga titles, seven Spanish Cups, seven Spanish Super Cups, four Champions Leagues, three UEFA Super Cups and three FIFA Club World Cups [s:reuters-factbox-2026].

The peak came in 2012. He scored 91 goals in all competitions that calendar year, which the Reuters factbox describes as a record for a calendar year [s:reuters-factbox-2026]. In the 2011-12 season he scored 50 La Liga goals, a single-season record [s:reuters-factbox-2026].

He left Barcelona in August 2021 as the club's all-time top scorer [s:reuters-factbox-2026].
