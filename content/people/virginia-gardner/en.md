Virginia Gardner is 31 years old: she was born on 18 April 1995 in Sacramento, California [s:tmdb] [s:rotten-tomatoes]. The year she turned 31 was a busy one for her. She has a film out in cinemas, *Fall 2: Deadpoint*, in which she returns as the character who died in the first film [s:comingsoon-fall2], and a run of other releases including *Killer Whale* and a film called F\*ck Valentine's Day [s:filmstarts] [s:rotten-tomatoes].

## Early life in Sacramento

Gardner is American and was born in Sacramento [s:tmdb] [s:adorocinema]. Her full name is Virginia Elizabeth Gardner [s:adorocinema] [s:ecartelera]. The sources we could open say little about her family, and we leave private details out. What they do show is a child who was active. W Magazine reports that she began taekwondo around second grade and had a black belt by about fifth or sixth grade [s:w-magazine]. As a teenager she also modelled for brands including Abercrombie & Fitch and Kohl's [s:w-magazine].

That martial arts background mattered later. Before *Runaways* she told W Magazine: "I have a black belt in taekwondo, so I have a little bit of stunt training already." She added that she is "clumsy and uncoordinated" and hired a trainer to work on her footwork for the role [s:w-magazine].

## First television roles

AdoroCinema dates her screen debut to 2011, a small part in the CW series *Hart of Dixie*, followed by an appearance on *Lab Rats* in 2012 and on *Glee* [s:adorocinema]. TMDB lists two *Glee* episodes, in which she played Marissa and Katie Fitzgerald, and eight episodes of *The Goldbergs* as Lexy Bloom [s:tmdb]. Sources disagree on some of these years, so treat the dates as approximate: AdoroCinema places *The Goldbergs* in 2015, while TMDB dates it to 2013 [s:adorocinema] [s:tmdb].

In 2015 she appeared as Sally Landry in an episode of *Law & Order: Special Victims Unit* and as Molly Bartlett in *How to Get Away with Murder* [s:tmdb].

## The break: Project Almanac and Runaways

Her first prominent film role was Christina Raskin in the found-footage science-fiction film *Project Almanac* [s:tmdb]. Rotten Tomatoes dates the film to 2015, while TMDB lists 2013, the year it was made; AdoroCinema shows both 2014 and 2015 [s:rotten-tomatoes] [s:tmdb] [s:adorocinema]. Both TMDB and AdoroCinema name it among the work she is best known for [s:tmdb] [s:adorocinema].

The larger step was Karolina Dean in *Marvel's Runaways*. The Hulu series premiered in November 2017, and W Magazine described her then as a 22-year-old California native [s:w-magazine]. AdoroCinema calls Karolina a lead role across all three seasons [s:adorocinema], and TMDB credits her with 33 episodes [s:tmdb]. Of the character she said: "She's struggling with a lot internally and who she's pretending to be and who she actually is." [s:w-magazine]

ComicBook.com named *Runaways* the winner of its 2017 Golden Issue Award for best ensemble cast, an award the whole cast shared [s:comicbook-golden-issue].

## Halloween and the horror years

In 2018 she played Vicky in David Gordon Green's *Halloween* [s:tmdb] [s:rotten-tomatoes]. eCartelera notes that she made two films with Green: *Halloween* and the earlier *Goat* (2016), in which she played Leah [s:ecartelera]. In the same year she was in *Little Bitches* (Kelly), *Monster Party* (Iris) and *Starfish* (Aubrey) [s:rotten-tomatoes]. In 2020 came *All the Bright Places*, as Amanda [s:rotten-tomatoes]. TMDB lists the film under 2019, so the year depends on the source [s:tmdb]. She also played Bernadette in an episode of *American Horror Stories* and appeared in the miniseries *Gaslit* as Sharon [s:tmdb] [s:ecartelera].

## Fall

*Fall* (2022) is the film most readers now look her up for. Directed by Scott Mann, it follows two friends who climb a 2,000-foot radio tower in the desert and become stranded at the top [s:fangoria-fall-sequels]. Grace Caroline Currey plays Becky and Gardner plays Shiloh Hunter, a character the Yahoo box-office report calls Hunter [s:tmdb] [s:yahoo-fall-box-office].

Lionsgate opened it in 1,548 North American venues, and tracking before the weekend projected only $1 million to $2 million [s:yahoo-fall-box-office]. Reviews were mixed. Variety's Owen Gleiberman called it a "fun, occasionally cheesy, but mostly ingeniously made thriller" [s:yahoo-fall-box-office]. Fangoria later reported that it earned more than $20 million on a $3 million budget [s:fangoria-fall-sequels]. That return led to two sequels, *Fall 2* and *Fall 3*, which Capstone Studios greenlit [s:fangoria-fall-sequels].

## What she is doing now

*Fall 2: Deadpoint* opened in the United States on 2 September 2026 through Lionsgate [s:comingsoon-fall2] [s:comicbasics-fall2]. It is directed by Michael and Peter Spierig, and the screenplay is by Scott Mann and Jonathan Frank [s:comingsoon-fall2] [s:comicbasics-fall2]. Harriet Slater and Arsema Thomas play the two new climbers, and Gardner and Currey are listed in the supporting cast [s:comicbasics-fall2].
