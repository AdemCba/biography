Paz Vega went from waiting in Madrid bars between castings to a Goya in a few years. The film that did it, *Sex and Lucia*, also pulled her out of a successful television sitcom. [s:buscabiografias] [s:hola-bio]

She was born María Paz Campos Trigo on 2 January 1976 in Seville, Spain, and took the surname Vega from her grandmother. [s:buscabiografias] [s:rotten-tomatoes]

## From Seville theatre to Madrid

Vega began a degree in Information Sciences but dropped it for acting. [s:buscabiografias] At 16 she joined the Centro Andaluz de Teatro in Seville. [s:hola-bio] After studying dramatic arts she moved to Madrid, where she worked in bars while going to castings. [s:buscabiografias] [s:hola-bio]

Television came first. Hola lists small parts in the series *Menudo es mi padre*, *Más que amigos* and *Compañeros*. [s:hola-bio] Her first film credits were *Zapping* and *Sobreviviré*. [s:hola-bio] The part that made her known to Spanish viewers was Laura in the sitcom *7 vidas*, where she played the Andalusian cousin of Toni Cantó's character. [s:hola-bio] The series is dated 1999 to 2006 in one filmography. [s:buscabiografias] Vega left it to make *Sex and Lucia*. [s:hola-bio]

## Sex and Lucia and the Goya

Julio Medem's *Lucía y el sexo* (*Sex and Lucia*, 2001) was her breakthrough in film. [s:buscabiografias] It won her the Goya for Best Newcomer Actress. [s:buscabiografias] [s:hola-bio] Sources disagree on the year of the award: one gives 2001 and Hola gives 2002. [s:buscabiografias] [s:hola-bio] Vozpópuli describes it as the only Goya of her career. [s:vozpopuli-goya]

Other Spanish work followed quickly. In 2002 she appeared in Pedro Almodóvar's *Hable con ella* (*Talk to Her*) and in *El otro lado de la cama*, and Hola counts her among the "chicas Almodóvar". [s:buscabiografias] [s:rotten-tomatoes] [s:hola-bio]

## Spanglish and Hollywood

Her English-language debut was *Spanglish* (2004), directed by James L. Brooks. [s:buscabiografias] She played Flor, a Mexican nanny who speaks no English, opposite Adam Sandler and Téa Leoni. [s:inquirer-spanglish] The Philadelphia Inquirer's critic called her "beguiling" and wrote that she "gets by on vivacity". [s:inquirer-spanglish]

More American films followed, among them *10 Items or Less* (2006), *The Spirit* (2008), *Triage* (2009, with Colin Farrell) and *Grace of Monaco* (2013). [s:buscabiografias] She also appeared in the series *The OA*. [s:buscabiografias]

She appears in *Rambo: Last Blood* (2019), directed by Adrian Grunberg and starring Sylvester Stallone. [s:slashfilm-rambo] Her character is a reporter who covers the Mexican drug trade and teams up with Rambo after her younger half-sister is kidnapped. [s:slashfilm-rambo]

## Back to Spanish television and film

Vega kept working on both sides of the Atlantic. In 2019 she played the lead villain Catalina Creel in the remake of the telenovela *Cuna de lobos*, and she then appeared in *La casa de las flores* (2020). [s:buscabiografias] Vozpópuli reported in late 2021 that she had lived for a long period in the United States and had settled again in Spain, while keeping her contacts there. [s:vozpopuli-goya] The same piece mentions her thriller *El lodo*, released in Spanish cinemas on 10 December 2021, and the Netflix series *Jigsaw*, which she was shooting in New York. [s:vozpopuli-goya] The series later aired as *Caleidoscopio* (2023). [s:buscabiografias]

## Directing Rita

In 2024 Vega directed and wrote her first feature, *Rita*. [s:seminci-rita] She also plays the mother. [s:seminci-rita] The story is set in a working-class neighbourhood of Seville in 1984, during a backlash against the divorce law, and follows seven-year-old Rita (Sofía Allepuz) as she notices her mother's unspoken suffering. [s:seminci-rita] The film had its world premiere at the Locarno Film Festival and played in the Official Section, out of competition, at Seminci in Valladolid. [s:seminci-rita] It was also in the international competition of the Mar del Plata festival. [s:mardelplata-rita]

## Trinidad and 2026

The latest film announced with her is *Trinidad*, a western directed by Laura Alvea and José Ortuño with a budget of more than 14 million euros. [s:rtvc-trinidad] Vega plays the mother of the title character (Gabriela Andrada), alongside Karla Sofía Gascón as the villain, the Widow Bronson. [s:rtvc-trinidad] [s:senal-trinidad] Part of the shoot took place in southern Gran Canaria. [s:rtvc-trinidad] Two outlets give 4 December 2026 as the cinema release date.
