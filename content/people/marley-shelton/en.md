Marley Shelton was born on 12 April 1974 in Los Angeles, California [s:tmdb-marley-shelton] [s:wikidata-marley-shelton]. She is an American actress [s:wikidata-marley-shelton]. Her earliest credit on TMDb is a one-episode part in *The Family Man* (1990) [s:tmdb-marley-shelton]. Her early film work includes *The Sandlot* (1993), where she played Wendy [s:tmdb-marley-shelton]. Wikidata lists the University of California, Los Angeles and Eagle Rock High School among her schools [s:wikidata-marley-shelton].

## From The Sandlot to Never Been Kissed

She played Margaret Henderson in *Pleasantville* (1998) and Kristin in *Never Been Kissed* (1999) [s:tmdb-marley-shelton]. Brave Beginnings lists *The Sandlot*, *Never Been Kissed* and *Pleasantville* among her film roles [s:brave-beginnings-psa]. Her 1990s credits also include Tricia Nixon Cox in *Nixon* (1995) [s:tmdb-marley-shelton] and Elysia in *Warriors of Virtue* (1997) [s:tmdb-marley-shelton]. Years later she appeared as herself in *The Sandlot Forever* (2019) [s:tmdb-marley-shelton].

In the 2000s TMDb lists her as Chloe in *Bubble Boy* (2001), Ingrid in *Uptown Girls* (2003) and Victoria Winters in *Dark Shadows* (2005) [s:tmdb-marley-shelton].

## Rodriguez, Sin City and Grindhouse

Her credits include *Sin City* (2005), where she played The Customer [s:tmdb-marley-shelton]. In a 2007 interview she said that Robert Rodriguez "has identified this potential in me, both in Sin City and Planet Terror". She said he "makes you feel like anything and everything is possible", and that he writes, directs, DPs, scores, edits and mixes his own movies [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview].

In *Grindhouse* she played Dakota in *Planet Terror*, one of the two films in the double feature [s:tmdb-marley-shelton] [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview]. She said she based the character on the ice-queen blondes of Hitchcock films, and that she had studied suspense for the part [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview]. Her favorite Hitchcock film is *Psycho* [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview]. She also likes *Dial M for Murder* [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview].

Rodriguez asked her to play one sequence as if it came from a silent movie [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview]. *Planet Terror* was shot first and *Death Proof* second, all at Rodriguez's Troublemaker Studios in Austin [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview]. Quentin Tarantino was on set for *Planet Terror* and helped co-direct it [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview]. Her biggest physical challenge, she said, was running in high heels night after night. Her stunt double, Tracey Dashnaw, performed the scene of Dakota jumping out of a window [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview].

She also compared the two directors' methods. Rodriguez worked digitally and quickly, she said, while Tarantino shot on film and was the more verbal director [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview]. Rodriguez called *Planet Terror* a "Gore-Ball" comedy that blends screwball comedy with zombies [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview]. In the same interview she mentioned *The Fifth Patient* (2007), an independent thriller she expected to screen at CineVegas [s:sliceofscifi-grindhouse-interview] [s:tmdb-marley-shelton].

## Scream: from deputy to sheriff

Judy Hicks first appeared in *Scream 4* (2011) [s:tmdb-marley-shelton]. An E! Online article describes her as a red herring, introduced as a deputy sheriff working with Dewey Riley and as a former high school classmate of Sidney Prescott [s:etonline-scream-judy-hicks].

She returned in *Scream* (2022), where Hicks had been promoted to sheriff. The article says Hicks is Wes's mother, and that Dylan Minnette plays Wes [s:etonline-scream-judy-hicks]. When she learned the character would return, Shelton said she "couldn't believe it" and was "over the moon" [s:etonline-scream-judy-hicks]. The article notes that fans are still divided over the character [s:etonline-scream-judy-hicks].

Shelton also talked about the lemon squares linked to Hicks [s:etonline-scream-judy-hicks]. "I can't believe the popularity of the lemon squares," she said [s:etonline-scream-judy-hicks]. The article says the new film explores where the characters are now, "especially after 10 years" [s:etonline-scream-judy-hicks].

## Television and other film work

She played Dr. Kerry Atkins in *Rampage* (2018) [s:tmdb-marley-shelton]. On television she played Emma Dutton in *1923* and Hannah Gray in *Manhunt* [s:tmdb-marley-shelton]. TMDb also lists her as Lydia Reed in *Matlock* (2025), across two episodes, and as Erika Kennedy in *Tracker* (2024), in one episode [s:tmdb-marley-shelton]. Neither TMDb nor BroadwayWorld lists any stage credits for her [s:tmdb-marley-shelton] [s:broadwayworld-marley-shelton].

A BroadwayWorld photo caption dated 9 January 2018 identifies her as Gail Mazzuchelli in the pilot of *Rise* [s:broadwayworld-marley-shelton]. The same site lists *Rise* as an NBC series from 2018 [s:broadwayworld-marley-shelton], and TMDb gives her ten episodes in the role [s:tmdb-marley-shelton].

## A public service film and a 2026 screening

On 12 October 2018 Brave Beginnings announced that Shelton starred in its latest public service announcement. The spot, part of the Brave Beginnings program, shows real premature babies reaching real milestones [s:brave-beginnings-psa]. Brave Beginnings is a program of the Will Rogers Motion Picture Pioneers Foundation [s:brave-beginnings-psa].

In March 2026 the Patchogue Theatre on Long Island announced a screening of *The Sandlot* with a live Q&A with [Chauncey Leopardi](person:chauncey-leopardi) and Shelton. The event was set for June on Long Island [s:broadwayworld-marley-shelton].

## Family
