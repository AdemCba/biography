Joe Buck has been a fixture in broadcasting since 1989, when his career began while he was an undergraduate at Indiana University [s:espn-press-room-bio] [s:sports-broadcasting-hof-buck]. He is known for calling NFL and baseball games for Fox [s:pro-football-hof-rozelle-2020] [s:mlb-frick-award-2026], and since 2022 he has called Monday Night Football for ESPN [s:espn-press-room-bio]. In September 2026 a report said ESPN had agreed a new six-year deal with him, but the network has not confirmed the terms [s:cox-media-buck-espn-extension-2026].

## Cardinals broadcasts, then Fox

Buck's broadcasting path ran alongside his father's. Jack Buck was a legendary St. Louis Cardinals broadcaster, and he also called professional football and baseball games [s:historystpete-joe-buck] [s:pro-football-hof-rozelle-2020]. Joe Buck was part of the Cardinals' broadcasts from 1991 to 2007 [s:mlb-frick-award-2026].

His national break came in 1994, when he joined Fox Sports as an NFL play-by-play announcer [s:baseball-hall-frick-2026]. He became the lead play-by-play announcer on Fox's national MLB coverage [s:mlb-frick-award-2026], and by 2002 he was the lead NFL voice on Fox [s:sports-broadcasting-hof-buck].

The scale of that work is easiest to see in the numbers. The Pro Football Hall of Fame says he has called six Super Bowls [s:pro-football-hof-rozelle-2020]. The ESPN biography credits him with calling 24 World Series [s:espn-press-room-bio]. The National Baseball Hall of Fame says he has called more World Series and All-Star Games on network television than any play-by-play announcer in history [s:baseball-hall-frick-2026].

His play-by-play work has also brought awards. The Sports Broadcasting Hall of Fame inducted him in 2022 [s:sports-broadcasting-hof-buck]. It credits him with a record-tying eight Sports Emmy Awards for play-by-play [s:sports-broadcasting-hof-buck] [s:espn-press-room-bio]. A 2020 Pro Football Hall of Fame release gave his total Emmy count as seven at that time [s:pro-football-hof-rozelle-2020].

## Monday Night Football and the move to ESPN

The move to ESPN was first reported as a plan. In March 2022, TVLine reported, citing Page Six, that Fox was reportedly releasing him from his contract about a year early and that he was setting up a position at ESPN, where he was expected to join Troy Aikman in calling Monday Night Football [s:tvline-buck-espn-2022]. TVLine said it had reached out to Fox Sports and ESPN for comment. The article reports no response from either network [s:tvline-buck-espn-2022].

The ESPN biography describes the signing. In March 2022 ESPN signed Buck, along with [Troy Aikman](person:troy-aikman), to a multi-year deal, making him the new play-by-play announcer of Monday Night Football [s:espn-press-room-bio]. The 2022 report also put his expected pay at roughly five years and more than $60 million. That figure was Page Six's reporting, as TVLine relayed it [s:tvline-buck-espn-2022].

His ESPN work is not limited to football. The National Baseball Hall of Fame says he returned to the baseball booth in 2025 for ESPN's Opening Day broadcast [s:baseball-hall-frick-2026].

## Two Hall of Fame honors

The Pro Football Hall of Fame named Buck the 2020 winner of its Pete Rozelle Radio-Television Award, which it presents each year “in recognition of longtime exceptional contributions to radio and television in professional football” [s:pro-football-hof-rozelle-2020]. The honor was revealed at halftime of the Bengals-Browns game, played on the NFL's centennial, in September 2020 [s:pro-football-hof-rozelle-2020]. Buck and his father are the first father and son to receive it [s:pro-football-hof-rozelle-2020].

The National Baseball Hall of Fame announced on 10 December 2025 that Buck would receive the 2026 Ford C. Frick Award for broadcasting [s:baseball-hall-frick-2026]. He earned the highest point total in a vote by the Hall's 16-member Frick Award Committee, from a field of ten finalists [s:baseball-hall-frick-2026]. His father, Jack Buck, won the same award in 1987, which makes the two the only father and son to have won it [s:baseball-hall-frick-2026]. The Hall said he would be honored during Hall of Fame Weekend, July 24-27, 2026 [s:baseball-hall-frick-2026].

## The September 2026 contract report

On 16 September 2026, Cox Media Group's national content desk published a report, citing The Athletic, that Buck had agreed to a six-year extension worth about $108 million [s:cox-media-buck-espn-extension-2026]. He had been in the final season of a five-year, $75 million deal, and sources said his pay would rise to about $18 million a year [s:cox-media-buck-espn-extension-2026]. The report said the extension would let him call ESPN's first Super Bowl, in February, and its second, in 2031 [s:cox-media-buck-espn-extension-2026].

ESPN has not confirmed the figures. Its executive vice president of communications, Josh Krulewitz, said the network would not discuss talent contract specifics publicly before the Super Bowl [s:cox-media-buck-espn-extension-2026]. The same report says Troy Aikman remains unsigned, and that Aikman is in the final season of a five-year, $90 million deal [s:cox-media-buck-espn-extension-2026].
