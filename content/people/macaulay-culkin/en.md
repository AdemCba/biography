## Born into a big New York family

Macaulay Carson Culkin was born on 26 August 1980 in New York City, the third of seven children [s:biography-com] [s:foxla-walk-of-fame]. His brother is the actor Kieran Culkin, who won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for A Real Pain; Macaulay said he cried watching the win from the crowd [s:thewrap-kieran]. One of his sisters died in 2008, and his first son was later named after her [s:et-engaged].

He did not grow up in an ordinary childhood. At four he performed in an Off-Broadway play, trained at Balanchine's School of American Ballet, and appeared in many television commercials [s:biography-com]. His stage debut is listed as 1984, in Bach Babies at Symphony Space in Manhattan [s:foxla-walk-of-fame]. His father, Kit, managed his early career [s:biography-com].

## How he got his break

The screen debut came in 1988 in the drama Rocket Gibraltar, in which he acted opposite Burt Lancaster [s:foxla-walk-of-fame] [s:biography-com]. The next year he appeared in John Hughes's comedy Uncle Buck, and that role made him Hollywood's favorite child actor [s:biography-com] [s:foxla-walk-of-fame].

Home Alone (1990) was the highest-grossing film of its year [s:biography-com]. Culkin played an eight-year-old left behind when his family flies to Paris [s:foxla-walk-of-fame]. He was nominated for a Golden Globe for best actor in a motion picture, musical or comedy; the Golden Globes site lists the nomination under 1991 [s:golden-globes]. FOX 11 reports that he lost to Gerard Depardieu [s:foxla-walk-of-fame].

## Years away and the return

After Richie Rich he largely left the screen. Biography.com says he returned in Party Monster (2003), followed by Saved! (2004) and Sex and Breakfast (2007) [s:biography-com]. In 2006 he published an autobiography, Junior [s:yahoo-net-worth].

He was a close friend of Michael Jackson, defended him at the 2005 trial, and is godfather to two of Jackson's children [s:biography-com]. People searching for "Macaulay Culkin Michael Jackson" are usually after that friendship. We report only these two public facts and nothing more about it.

Later screen work included the series Kings (2009), The Jim Gaffigan Show (2015 to 2016), American Horror Story: Double Feature (2021) and The Righteous Gemstones (2022) [s:foxla-walk-of-fame]. He appeared in Changeland, which Biography.com dates to 2018 [s:biography-com]. Outside acting, he fronted the comedy rock band The Pizza Underground, which he launched in 2013 [s:yahoo-net-worth] [s:biography-com]. Sources disagree on the end date: Biography.com says it dissolved in 2016, FOX 11 gives 2013 to 2018 [s:biography-com] [s:foxla-walk-of-fame]. In 2017 he founded Bunny Ears, a satirical pop culture website and podcast, where he is publisher and CEO [s:foxla-walk-of-fame].

## Awards and honours

The Golden Globes list one nomination and no wins: best actor in a motion picture, musical or comedy, for Home Alone, filed under 1991 [s:golden-globes]. He won the 1991 American Comedy Award for funniest actor in a motion picture (leading role) [s:foxla-walk-of-fame]. On 1 December 2023 he received the 2,765th star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame, at 6353 Hollywood Boulevard, a ceremony that honoured about 40 years of acting [s:foxla-walk-of-fame]. Catherine O'Hara, who played his mother in Home Alone and its sequel, and Natasha Lyonne spoke [s:foxla-walk-of-fame].
