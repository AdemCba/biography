Montez Sweat is a defensive end who sat out two games this October under the NFL's performance-enhancing substances policy [s:ajc-suspension]. He is back. The league's penalty cost him $2.3 million in base salary [s:nfl-suspension-return], and the same report says more guaranteed money was voided.

## A suspension and what it cost

The league announced the suspension on a Tuesday in October 2026 [s:ajc-suspension]. NFL.com reports the $2.3 million base salary loss and says that the $12.7 million still guaranteed this season and all future guaranteed money, $21 million in 2027, have been voided [s:nfl-suspension-return].

Sweat has addressed the violation. "It's definitely something I've got to live with for my career," he said [s:nfl-suspension-return].

## From Washington to Chicago

Washington took him in the first round of the 2019 NFL Draft, 26th overall [s:bears-roster]. The Bears traded for him on 31 October 2023 in exchange for a 2024 second-round pick [s:bears-roster]. Four days later he signed a four-year contract extension through 2027 [s:bears-roster].

## The sacks that earned a Pro Bowl

Sweat started 16 games as a rookie in 2019 and recorded 7 sacks [s:bears-roster]. In 2020 he had 9 sacks and an interception that he returned for a touchdown [s:bears-roster]. Washington exercised his fifth-year option in April 2022 [s:bears-roster]. He finished 2023 with 12.5 sacks across Washington and Chicago, the first player in NFL history to lead two teams in sacks in one season [s:bears-roster]. He was named to the Pro Bowl after that season, his first [s:bears-2025-stats]. With the Bears that year he made 9 starts and 6 sacks [s:bears-roster].

This is his eighth NFL season [s:bears-2025-stats]. The team's biography lists 307 tackles, 129 quarterback hits, 73 tackles for loss and 15 forced fumbles across his career [s:bears-roster]. He has started every regular-season game he has played, 109 in all [s:bears-2025-stats].

## Sacks and the 2025 season

In 2025 he started all 17 regular-season games, made 53 tackles and led the Bears with 10.0 sacks, along with 13 tackles for loss [s:bears-2025-stats]. He had 5.5 sacks in 16 starts in 2024 [s:bears-2025-stats]. The team's biography puts his career total at 57.0 sacks [s:bears-roster]. The team's career stats table shows 59, and this page uses the biography figure [s:bears-roster].

Five games into 2026 he had two sacks, NFL.com reported at the time of his return [s:nfl-suspension-return].

## A short absence in July

In July the Bears put him on the non-football illness list. They removed him the next day, after he missed the first training camp practice of the year [s:suntimes-illness]. The Sun-Times wrote that he was never expected to miss serious time [s:suntimes-illness], and that he was expected at Thursday morning's practice [s:suntimes-illness].

## Size and background

The NFL's player page lists him at 6-6 and 270 pounds, with Mississippi State as his college [s:nfl-player-profile]. Wikidata gives his citizenship as the United States [s:wikidata].

## Michigan State, then Mississippi State

He began his college career at Michigan State in 2014, then transferred from Copiah-Lincoln Community College to Mississippi State [s:bears-2025-stats]. Over 26 games at Mississippi State he recorded 101 tackles, 29.5 of them for loss, and 22 sacks [s:bears-roster]. As a senior in 2018 he had 11.5 sacks and was a semifinalist for the Bednarik Award [s:bears-roster]. He earned first-team All-American honors from several outlets and was five times the SEC Defensive Lineman of the Week [s:bears-roster]. He attended Stephenson High School in Georgia [s:bears-2025-stats].
