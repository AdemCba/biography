Kylie Cantrall is a singer and actress who records for Republic Records. Heavy reported that she was scheduled to sing the US national anthem before Game 2 of the 2026 World Series in Milwaukee. It also gave her birth date as 25 June 2005 in Los Angeles [s:heavy-cantrall-anthem].

## An anthem slot in Milwaukee

Heavy reported that Cantrall would perform the national anthem before World Series Game 2 in Milwaukee. It also said Mickey Guyton sang the anthem for Game 1 [s:heavy-cantrall-anthem]. The sources used for this page do not confirm that the performance took place. Heavy described the performance as upcoming. This page therefore says only that she was scheduled to sing.

Heavy described her as “the 21-year-old Disney star turned Republic Records pop singer” [s:heavy-cantrall-anthem].

## Born in Los Angeles

Heavy, citing the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel, gives her full name as Kylie Lorena Cantrall and says she was born on 25 June 2005 in Los Angeles [s:heavy-cantrall-anthem]. It is a single press source. No second source has been checked for it.

## Republic Records and the Valley Girl Problems EP

Her EP Valley Girl Problems was released on 24 July 2026 through Republic Records, according to uDiscover Music [s:udiscovermusic-valley-girl-problems-tour]. The official site carries a Republic Records copyright notice in its footer [s:kylie-cantrall-official].

Her official site lists four releases: the EP Valley Girl Problems, an acoustic version of “Carrie Bradshaw”, the deluxe edition of B.O.Y., and the track “Denim” [s:kylie-cantrall-official]. The release list gives no dates for the earlier titles. This page gives none either.

## The Valley Girl Problems Tour

On 4 August 2026, uDiscover Music reported that Cantrall would headline her first tour, The Valley Girl Problems Tour [s:udiscovermusic-valley-girl-problems-tour]. The first date was 25 September in Minneapolis. The last was 22 October in Los Angeles, which uDiscover described as the tour “wrapping up back home” [s:udiscovermusic-valley-girl-problems-tour]. According to the same article, the artist presale begins on 5 August and general tickets go on sale on 7 August [s:udiscovermusic-valley-girl-problems-tour].

## From Descendants to a pop career

uDiscover says she first appeared as Red in Descendants: The Rise of Red in 2024, a role that led to a place on the Descendants/Zombies: Worlds Collide Tour. She reprised the role in the Disney Channel movie Descendants: Wicked Wonderland, which premiered on 16 July [s:udiscovermusic-valley-girl-problems-tour].

uDiscover Music calls her a rising pop star and says she is pursuing “successful new recording and acting projects” [s:udiscovermusic-valley-girl-problems-tour].

## Where to follow her

Her official site links to her accounts on Instagram, TikTok, Spotify, Facebook and YouTube [s:kylie-cantrall-official]. The site also has a tour section. It lists no dates, so the dates on this page come from uDiscover Music.

## What the sources do not say

None of the sources used for this page gives Cantrall’s nationality, so this page does not state one. Heavy’s 21-year-old description is consistent with the birth date it gives [s:heavy-cantrall-anthem].
