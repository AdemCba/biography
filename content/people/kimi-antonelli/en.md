## Born in Bologna, raised on karts

Andrea Kimi Antonelli was born on 25 August 2006 in Bologna, Italy [s:f1-driver-profile] [s:mercedes-driver-profile]. Mercedes says he is more commonly known by his middle name, Kimi [s:mercedes-driver-profile].

He began karting at seven. He raced for his father's team [s:mercedes-everything]. His first kart title came in 2015, in the Trofeo Easykart and Kart Grand Prix EasyKart 60 category [s:mercedes-everything]. His father, Marco, is a former racing driver who runs his own motorsport team, Antonelli Motorsport [s:mercedes-everything].

## Into the Mercedes Junior Programme

Antonelli joined the Mercedes Junior Programme in April 2019, aged 12 [s:mercedes-driver-profile]. Mercedes says he joined "after claiming numerous titles and wins in the junior karting categories" [s:mercedes-everything].

## Single-seaters and Formula 2

His single-seater debut came in 2021, in the Italian F4 and Formula 4 UAE Championship Trophy round [s:mercedes-everything]. He went on to win the Italian F4 title and the ADAC Formula 4 title [s:mercedes-everything].

In 2024 he raced in Formula 2 with PREMA. He won the sprint race at Silverstone, then the race in Hungary, "becoming the youngest multiple winner in series history" [s:mercedes-everything]. Mercedes' own profile lists those 2024 wins at Silverstone and Budapest [s:mercedes-driver-profile].

## Debut season in 2025

Antonelli made his debut at the 2025 Australian Grand Prix in Melbourne, as the third-youngest driver to start an F1 race and the 13th Mercedes driver to race a Grand Prix [s:mercedes-everything]. He finished fourth there [s:mercedes-everything]. He became the youngest driver in F1 history to lead a lap at the Japanese Grand Prix [s:mercedes-everything].

He finished third in Canada, which made him the third-youngest driver to stand on an F1 podium [s:mercedes-everything]. By the Brazilian Grand Prix he had become the highest-scoring rookie in F1 history [s:mercedes-everything]. He chose number 12 as his race number for 2025 and beyond [s:mercedes-everything]. He finished the season seventh in the drivers' standings with 150 points and three podiums [s:mercedes-driver-profile].

## The 2026 season and the first win
