## Born in Wrocław, started at five

Hubert Hurkacz was born on 11 February 1997 in Wrocław, a city in southwestern Poland, and he holds Polish citizenship [s:wikidata-hurkacz]. Tennis started early. His mother was a junior champion in Poland, and he began playing at five [s:atp-bio]. As a child he also did gymnastics, basketball and football [s:atp-bio]. He turned professional in 2015 [s:ausopen-profile], and his nickname on tour is Hubi [s:atp-bio].

At 196 cm and 81 kg, he plays right-handed [s:ausopen-profile]. His favourite shot is the serve, and hard courts are his favourite surface [s:atp-bio]. He grew up on clay, he says, and describes his game as all-round [s:atp-bio]. Roger Federer and Michael Jordan are his idols, and Melbourne is his favourite city on tour [s:atp-bio].

## Wimbledon 2021 and Federer's last singles match

Hurkacz arrived at Wimbledon in 2021 on a six-match losing streak [s:atp-bio]. He still reached his first Grand Slam semifinal there [s:atp-bio]. On the way he beat Roger Federer 6-3, 7-6(4), 6-0, winning in three sets with the last one 6-0 [s:atp-bio]. The ATP profile describes it as the final singles match of the Swiss player's career, and calls Federer an eight-time champion [s:atp-bio].

## Masters 1000 titles in Miami and Shanghai

The Miami Open came first, in 2021. He upset five seeds on the way to the title, including Jannik Sinner in the final [s:atp-bio]. Two years later he won the Shanghai Masters. He saved a championship point against Andrey Rublev before the title was his [s:atp-bio]. He also won Masters 1000 doubles titles in Paris in 2020 with Auger-Aliassime and in Miami in 2022 with Isner [s:atp-bio]. His best doubles ranking is No. 30 [s:ausopen-profile].

Beyond those wins, he reached Masters 1000 semifinals in Paris in 2021, Miami in 2022 and Cincinnati in 2023, and he lost the Montreal final in 2022 [s:atp-bio].

## Halle, the top 10 and the Nitto ATP Finals

In 2022 he won Halle, beating world No. 1 Daniil Medvedev 6-1, 6-4 in 63 minutes for his fifth ATP singles title [s:atp-bio]. He is 8-4 in ATP singles finals, and his Australian Open profile lists eight singles titles and four doubles titles [s:ausopen-profile]. He finished 2021 and 2023 as a year-end No. 9, both seasons after Nitto ATP Finals appearances [s:atp-bio]. He reached a career-high No. 6 in August 2024 [s:atp-bio].
