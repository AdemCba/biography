Vianney stepped back from the media in June 2025, and during that break he built a wooden cabin in the forest [s:purepeople-cabane-liberte]. He spent about nine months on it, and during that time he turned himself into a carpenter [s:parismatch-be-tournee-2026]. The cabin was finished in April 2026 [s:rtbf-tournee-2026]. In May and August of that year he came back with a tour announcement and a new single [s:parismatch-be-tournee-2026] [s:parismatch-be-single-2026].

## Nine months in the forest

Vianney said he might have to become a bit of a carpenter to learn again how to be a singer [s:purepeople-cabane-liberte]. He later wrote that wanting to build something had ended up rebuilding him, and that a retreat in the forest had not changed him but had helped him [s:purepeople-construire-2026]. Once the cabin was finished, he stayed in it for a full month to write and record [s:purepeople-construire-2026]. The cabin now serves as his recording studio [s:parismatch-be-tournee-2026].

## “D'or et d'étain” and the no-computer rule

Vianney wrote and recorded the single “D'or et d'étain” during that month in the cabin [s:parismatch-be-single-2026]. He used no computer. In his words, he had only his instruments and an old tape recorder [s:purepeople-construire-2026]. He warned fans that the result would not be perfect, but that it would be human [s:purepeople-construire-2026]. The single came out on Friday 28 August 2026, at one minute past midnight [s:parismatch-be-single-2026].

Vianney said he had been chasing a freedom he needed to find again, and that this search led him to the song [s:purepeople-cabane-liberte]. Pure People describes it as a love song about relationships that last through time and hardship, mixing melancholy with hope [s:purepeople-cabane-liberte].

## The 2027 and 2028 tour

Vianney announced the tour on Monday 25 May 2026, in an Instagram video in which he marked each 2027 city on a cardboard map of France [s:rtbf-tournee-2026]. Presale opened on 27 May 2026, and general sale began on Friday 29 May at 10:00 [s:purepeople-tournee-2026] [s:stephanelarue-tournee-2026].

The tour opens in Reims on 24 March 2027 [s:sortiraparis-zenith-paris-2027]. Paris gets three nights at the Zénith de Paris La Villette, on 8, 9 and 10 April 2027 [s:sortiraparis-zenith-paris-2027] [s:purepeople-tournee-2026]. Geneva is listed for 18 November 2027 [s:stephanelarue-tournee-2026], and Brussels for 1 December 2027 at the ING Arena [s:rtbf-tournee-2026].
