Gustaf Skarsgård turns 46 on 12 November 2026, and the autumn is crowded for him. He was born on 12 November 1980 in Stockholm [s:tmdb] [s:rotten-tomatoes], and within a few weeks of each other he has a film chosen as Sweden's Oscar entry, a Netflix release and the lead in the new Wallander films [s:sfi-adult-supervision] [s:martincid-profile] [s:moviezine-steget-efter].

## Early life: the second Skarsgård son

Skarsgård is the second child of the actor Stellan Skarsgård and his first wife, My, a physician [s:martincid-profile]. His full siblings are Alexander, Sam, Bill, Eija and Valter, and he has two half-brothers, Ossian and Kolbjörn, who are his father's sons with Megan Everett [s:tmdb-sv] [s:thelocal-skarsgard-2023]. The Local counts six of Stellan's eight children as My's [s:thelocal-skarsgard-2023]. Rotten Tomatoes says the actor Peter Stormare is his godfather [s:rotten-tomatoes].

Acting came early. The Local says he started at six [s:thelocal-skarsgard-2023], and Martin Cid Magazine says he was eight when he played the nephew of his father's character in his first feature, *Code Name Coq Rouge* [s:martincid-profile]. The Swedish encyclopedia Nationalencyklopedin lists that 1989 film, *Sommaren* (1995) and the television drama *Skuggornas hus* (1996) among his child roles [s:ne-skarsgard]. Rotten Tomatoes adds that he moved to larger parts on Swedish television as a teenager [s:rotten-tomatoes].

## Training and the stage

He studied at the Teaterhögskolan in Stockholm between 1998 and 2003 and then joined the Royal Dramatic Theatre, Dramaten [s:thelocal-skarsgard-2023] [s:martincid-profile]. History's cast page describes the theatre as Sweden's national theatre and says he played the title role in a 2010 production of *Hamlet* [s:history-floki]. Martin Cid names Bassanio as another stage part [s:martincid-profile].

## How he got his break

The first lead came while he was still a student. Rotten Tomatoes credits him as the lead in the Swedish supernatural drama *The Invisible* (2002) [s:rotten-tomatoes], and the same site lists *Evil* (2003), where he played Otto Silverhielm, as one of many Swedish films that followed [s:rotten-tomatoes]. His own recognition came with *Kidz in da Hood* (2006), for which History says he won the Guldbagge, the Swedish film prize, for best actor [s:history-floki] [s:rotten-tomatoes]. History also says the European Film Academy gave him its Shooting Star Award in 2007 [s:history-floki].

The international step was *The Way Back* (2010), Peter Weir's film, which Rotten Tomatoes calls his Hollywood debut [s:rotten-tomatoes]. He still worked mostly in Sweden, according to the same profile [s:rotten-tomatoes]. Two years later he played Bengt Danielsson in *Kon-Tiki* (2012), a film that History says earned Golden Globe and Academy Award nominations [s:rotten-tomatoes] [s:history-floki].

## Floki in Vikings

For most viewers abroad he is Floki. The show ran from 2013 to 2020 on the History channel [s:martincid-profile], and TMDB counts 71 episodes for him [s:tmdb-sv]. History describes the character as a skilled shipbuilder who designs a new kind of longship able to cross open ocean and travel up shallow rivers, and as a devout believer who puts the gods above everyone, Ragnar included [s:history-floki]. The A.V. Club, in July 2020, called him the show's "resident wacko shipbuilder" [s:avclub-cursed-2020].

## From Vikings to Merlin

The step after Floki was Merlin. In *Cursed*, which the A.V. The series ran for one season on Netflix [s:martincid-profile]. Before that he had been Karl Strand in *Westworld* in 2017, ten episodes in all [s:tmdb-sv].

## What he is doing now

The biggest project is *Wallander*. TV4 announced that he takes over the lead from Rolf Lassgård and Krister Henriksson in three new films based on Henning Mankell's novels, with filming in Ystad from summer 2025 [s:tv4-wallander]. The first film, *Steget efter* (One Step Behind), is directed by Molly Hartleb and has its world premiere at the Stockholm International Film Festival in November, before reaching TV4 Play Plus in spring 2027 [s:moviezine-steget-efter]. In an August 2026 interview he describes the character as a man "in the middle of a life crisis", drinking too much, in the middle of a divorce and obsessed with work [s:moviezine-wallander-intervju]. He also mentions his directing debut, *Kannibalen på Fårö*, a meta horror film about Ingmar Bergman made with Peter Birro [s:moviezine-wallander-intervju].

On 2 September 2026 the Swedish Film Institute chose *Adult Supervision* (original title *Arkipelag*) as Sweden's submission for Best International Feature at the 99th Academy Awards [s:sfi-adult-supervision]. It is Alex Schulman's feature debut, with Skarsgård, Fares Fares and Linus Wahlgren as three fathers on a school trip with their teenage daughters [s:sfi-adult-supervision]. It opened in Sweden on 9 October 2026; the Academy shortlist of 15 films is announced on 15 December 2026, nominations on 21 January 2027 and the ceremony is on 14 March 2027 [s:sfi-adult-supervision]. Martin Cid says he stars in Netflix's *The Storm*, released days later, as Filip [s:martincid-profile].
