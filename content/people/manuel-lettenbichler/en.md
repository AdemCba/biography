Manuel Lettenbichler won the Red Bull Erzbergrodeo for the fifth year in a row in June 2026, which equals the event record [s:ktm-erzberg-2026]. The Red Bull Romaniacs followed, and his win there was his seventh, which ties the all-time record [s:ktm-romaniacs-2026]. He is also leading the FIM Hard Enduro World Championship after four of its eight rounds [s:ktm-forza-orza-2026].

## Born in southern Germany, son of a hard-enduro rider

KTM's rider profile gives his date of birth as 30 April 1998 and his nationality as German [s:ktm-hero]. It describes him as "born and bred in the rugged landscapes of southern Germany" [s:ktm-hero]. None of the sources I opened names the town where he was born, so this article does not give one.

The same profile names his father, Andreas Lettenbichler, as a hard-enduro rider and a former KTM Factory Racing rider [s:ktm-hero].

## The early titles

KTM's profile lists three championships before his hard-enduro run. He was the junior-class FIM SuperEnduro World Champion in 2016 and the WESS Enduro World Champion in 2019 [s:ktm-hero]. The profile gives the years only, not the dates of the finals [s:ktm-hero]. KTM's race reports also use the name \"Mani\", which is how the team headlines his results [s:ktm-erzberg-2026].

## Four straight hard-enduro titles

The title that defines his record is the FIM Hard Enduro World Championship. KTM lists him as champion in 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 [s:ktm-hero]. The profile calls him "the only rider in history to claim four consecutive Hard Enduro World Championships" [s:ktm-hero].

The 2026 series has eight rounds. After four of them, KTM's provisional standings show him on 117 points, with Mitch Brightmore of Great Britain on 102 [s:ktm-forza-orza-2026]. Motorcycle.com put his lead at 15 points at the halfway stage of the season [s:motorcycle-forza-orza-2026].

## Romaniacs: six wins, then seven

The Red Bull Romaniacs is a multi-day rally held in Romania. In 2025 Lettenbichler won it for his sixth career victory, riding a KTM 300 EXC [s:ktm-romaniacs-2025]. He placed third in the prologue in Sibiu, won the first offroad day by close to seven minutes, then won the second offroad day too [s:ktm-romaniacs-2025].

He finished the 2025 event in 21:41:45 on provisional results. Billy Bolt was second on a time of 22:14:56, and Mario Roman was third [s:ktm-romaniacs-2025]. Lettenbichler won the final stage on the Saturday, which settled the overall result [s:ktm-romaniacs-2025]. The race report labels him \"(GER)\" in its results and calls him the German, which matches his KTM profile [s:ktm-romaniacs-2025] [s:ktm-hero].

The 2026 edition gave him the seventh title. KTM's provisional time for his win is 22:14:41 [s:ktm-romaniacs-2026]. The report says "the German controlled the race from the opening offroad stage to the finish" [s:ktm-romaniacs-2026]. He said: "To win Red Bull Romaniacs for the seventh time is something I never expected, and it's an unreal feeling" [s:ktm-romaniacs-2026].

## Erzbergrodeo: five in a row

The 2026 Erzbergrodeo was run on Sunday 7 June [s:ktm-erzberg-2026]. Lettenbichler crossed the line first in 3:05:39.953 on provisional results, ahead of Trystan Hart of Canada, who also rode for KTM [s:ktm-erzberg-2026]. The five straight wins equal the event record rather than beat it, and KTM's wording makes that distinction [s:ktm-erzberg-2026]. KTM's report says that once he was in front, he "steadily increased his pace and control over the race" [s:ktm-erzberg-2026].

His reaction was short. "Five wins at Erzberg, I have no words!" he said, and then: "It feels incredible to make it five in a row and equal the record" [s:ktm-erzberg-2026].

## Forza Orza and the rest of 2026

Forza Orza, held in Orsa, Sweden, was round four of eight in the 2026 championship [s:ktm-forza-orza-2026] [s:motorcycle-forza-orza-2026]. KTM's headline says the win was used to strengthen his Hard Enduro championship lead [s:ktm-forza-orza-2026]. Lettenbichler won in 2:20:41. Mitch Brightmore finished second, 3:36 back, and Ashton Brightmore third, 7:16 back [s:motorcycle-forza-orza-2026].

The race was not clean for him. "I felt good throughout the race, but then on the second-to-last lap I had a huge crash in the traffic," he said [s:ktm-forza-orza-2026]. He added: "I think I did exactly what I had to do, and it's great to come away with the win" [s:ktm-forza-orza-2026].
