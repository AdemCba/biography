Celso Freitas began in radio in 1970 and built his television career at TV Globo and then at RecordTV [s:folhape-celso-freitas-sbt-news] [s:sbt-news-contrata-celso-freitas]. He joined SBT News in November 2025, and he now anchors its weekday newscast [s:propmark-celso-freitas-novo-ancora] [s:sbt-news-celso-freitas-expectativa].

## Radio, then the Globo newsroom

Freitas started in radio in 1970 [s:folhape-celso-freitas-sbt-news]. TV Globo hired him in 1976, and he stayed until 2004 [s:folhape-celso-freitas-sbt-news]. Starting in 1983, he co-anchored Jornal Nacional with Cid Moreira. Six years later he became the regular host of Fantástico and Globo Repórter [s:folhape-celso-freitas-sbt-news].

The SBT News profile says he became known for covering major stories: the death of Ayrton Senna, Brazil's 1994 World Cup win, Hurricane Katrina in 2005, the 2006 tsunami in Indonesia and Barack Obama's election in 2008 [s:sbt-news-contrata-celso-freitas].

## Domingo Espetacular and Jornal da Record

Record hired him in 2004 to launch Domingo Espetacular [s:folhape-celso-freitas-sbt-news] [s:poder360-record-demite-celso-freitas]. He took over the anchor desk of Jornal da Record from Boris Casoy in 2006 [s:folhape-celso-freitas-sbt-news]. He also moderated electoral debates, hosted Repórter Record and the Retrospectivas programmes, and led Entrevista Record News for five years [s:poder360-record-demite-celso-freitas]. Folha de Pernambuco adds JR 15 Minutos to the list of shows he hosted at the network [s:folhape-celso-freitas-sbt-news].

## Leaving Record in 2025

On 11 March 2025 Record announced that it had dismissed him after 21 years at the station [s:poder360-record-demite-celso-freitas]. The network's note praised his commitment to journalistic ethics. Freitas said in a video posted on social media that he was moving on and heading toward “novos desafios” (new challenges) [s:poder360-record-demite-celso-freitas]. Sergio Aguiar and Christina Lemos took over the presenting of Jornal da Record after him [s:poder360-record-demite-celso-freitas]. The report we checked gives no reason for the decision.

## Joining SBT News

SBT News announced him as one of its new anchors on 10 November 2025, alongside Leandro Magalhães and Raquel Landim [s:sbt-news-contrata-celso-freitas] [s:folhape-celso-freitas-sbt-news]. The channel was set to launch on 15 December 2025 [s:sbt-news-contrata-celso-freitas]. The same announcement named Camila Mattoso as the newsroom director [s:sbt-news-contrata-celso-freitas].

Quoted in the announcement, he said: “O SBT News nasce com a credibilidade do SBT e seu alcance em múltiplas plataformas” (“SBT News is born with the credibility of SBT and its reach across multiple platforms”) [s:sbt-news-contrata-celso-freitas].

## Jornal do SBT News

In an interview published by SBT News on 11 December 2025, he described the job as the anchor of Jornal do SBT News, which airs Monday to Friday from 6:30 pm to 8:30 pm, sharing the desk with Marina Demori [s:sbt-news-celso-freitas-expectativa]. He called the new job “Uma maravilha!” (“Wonderful!”) [s:sbt-news-celso-freitas-expectativa]. He described the difference from open-channel television: on a news channel, the team can take the main stories and go through them in depth: “Enquanto que, no canal de notícias, a gente tem a oportunidade de pegar os principais assuntos e esmiuçá-los” (on a news channel, the team has the chance to take the main stories and examine them closely) [s:sbt-news-celso-freitas-expectativa].

Asked how he keeps his voice in shape, he called his voice “um presente, uma dádiva divina” (a gift, a divine blessing). He advises against drinking ice-cold water after long periods of speaking, because the thermal shock can reduce vocal power [s:sbt-news-celso-freitas-expectativa].
