Ewa Pajor scored twice in the 2026 Women's Champions League final, a 4-0 win for Barcelona over OL Lyonnes in Oslo, and UEFA named her the official Player of the Match [s:uefa-final-player-of-the-match]. It was her sixth European final. She had lost the first five [s:uefa-pajor-records].

## From Uniejów to Medyk Konin

Pajor was born on 3 December 1996 in Uniejów, Poland [s:fcb-profile-es]. She is a forward who has also played on the left wing [s:fcb-profile-es]. With Medyk Konin she won two Polish league titles and three Polish cups [s:fcb-profile-es]. She broke into the senior side there aged 15 [s:uefa-pajor-records].

She was part of the Poland side that won the UEFA Women's Under-17 Championship in 2013 [s:uefa-pajor-records] [s:fcb-profile-es]. Her senior debut came on 20 August 2013 against Czechia. She won a penalty and scored [s:fcb-profile-es] [s:uefa-pajor-records].

## Nine seasons at Wolfsburg

Pajor moved to VfL Wolfsburg in the summer of 2015 [s:uefa-pajor-records] and spent nine seasons there [s:fcb-signing-2024]. Wolfsburg won five Bundesliga titles in her time there, in 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020 and 2022 [s:fcb-signing-2024]. They also won nine German Cups [s:fcb-signing-2024] [s:uefa-pajor-records].

In the 2017/18 Champions League she scored in Wolfsburg's 5-1 aggregate semi-final win over Chelsea [s:uefa-pajor-records]. While she was there, Wolfsburg lost four Champions League finals, and she was in the side for each one [s:fcb-signing-2024]. In 2022/23 she was the competition's top scorer with nine goals for Wolfsburg [s:uefa-pajor-records]. In her last season there she was the German league's top scorer, with 18 goals in 19 games [s:fcb-signing-2024].

## Moving to Barcelona

Barcelona agreed to sign her in June 2024 on a three-year deal running to 30 June 2027 [s:fcb-signing-2024]. She scored nine times in her first six league games [s:uefa-pajor-records].

## Top scorer in the 2025/26 Champions League

In 2025/26 Pajor was the competition's top scorer with 11 goals. She scored twice in each of Barcelona's quarter-final and semi-final wins [s:uefa-pajor-records]. Her Champions League career total reached 44 goals by the end of that season, seventh on the all-time list, behind Ada Hegerberg at the top [s:uefa-pajor-records].

## The Oslo final

Barcelona beat OL Lyonnes 4-0 at Oslo on 23 May 2026 [s:uefa-final-player-of-the-match]. Pajor scored her tenth and eleventh goals of the campaign, then set up Salma Paralluelo for Barcelona's fourth [s:uefa-final-player-of-the-match]. The UEFA technical observer panel said: "A great impact. The first touch and finish for her first goal were of the highest quality." [s:uefa-final-player-of-the-match] The match was the sixth European final of her career, and the first she won [s:uefa-pajor-records]. The previous final had gone to Arsenal, who beat Barcelona 1-0 in Lisbon in 2025 [s:uefa-pajor-records]. Her two goals in Oslo made her the first player to score more than once in a Champions League final since 2023 [s:uefa-pajor-records].

## Poland's record scorer

In September 2022 Pajor passed Marta Otrębska to become Poland's all-time leading female goalscorer [s:uefa-pajor-records]. She scored in both play-off ties, against Romania and Austria, to reach Women's Euro 2025 [s:uefa-pajor-records]. Poland lost their first two group games at the tournament, to Germany and Sweden [s:uefa-pajor-records]. Then she scored in their 3-2 win over Denmark [s:uefa-pajor-records].
