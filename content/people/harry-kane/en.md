Harry Kane was the bookmakers' favourite going into the Ballon d'Or ceremony on 26 October 2026, held in London [s:sky-ballon-dor-favourite-2026]. The FA's profile of England's nominees says it is the first ceremony held outside Paris [s:fa-ballon-dor-nominees-2026]. No source used for this page gives the result, so the page does not name a winner. What follows is the record that put Kane in the race, his club situation and the points still unconfirmed.

## Bundesliga top scorer for a third year

Kane finished the 2025/26 Bundesliga season on 36 goals, including a hat-trick in a 5-1 win over Cologne on Matchday 34 [s:bundesliga-top-scorer-2026]. The league's report, dated 16 May 2026, says he became the first player to win the top scorer award in each of his first three seasons in the Bundesliga [s:bundesliga-top-scorer-2026]. It notes that Robert Lewandowski is the only other player to win the award three years in a row [s:bundesliga-top-scorer-2026].

The FA's shortlist profile lists 36 Bundesliga goals in 31 league appearances, 100 goals in 104 competitive appearances for Bayern, a European Golden Shoe and a third straight Bundesliga top-scorer award [s:fa-ballon-dor-nominees-2026]. It credits his Bayern form with “extraordinary scoring” [s:fa-ballon-dor-nominees-2026]. Sky Sports said he was the bookmakers' favourite to be crowned, but its article quotes no odds [s:sky-ballon-dor-favourite-2026]. The FA's profile says the accumulated voting points decide the prizes at the ceremony [s:fa-ballon-dor-nominees-2026].

## From Tottenham to Bayern

Bayern Munich signed Kane from Tottenham Hotspur in August 2023 in a club-record deal, on a four-year contract [s:tnt-bayern-signing-2023]. TNT described Bayern as the German champions at the time [s:tnt-bayern-signing-2023]. TNT reported a potential fee of €120 million (£104m) [s:tnt-bayern-signing-2023]. The figure was described as potential, not as a confirmed price. TNT says it would have surpassed the €80 million (£68m) Bayern paid for Lucas Hernández from Atlético Madrid in 2019, their previous club record [s:tnt-bayern-signing-2023].

His Tottenham years had their own records. Tottenham's 2019 article put his England tally at 25 goals in 40 caps while he played for the club [s:tottenham-2019]. Its 2022 feature listed Jimmy Greaves' 266 Spurs goals as the record he was chasing [s:tottenham-2022].

## England's record scorer

Kane made his England debut against Lithuania in March 2015 and scored 79 seconds after coming off the bench [s:tottenham-2022]. The FA's feature from September 2024 counted 99 caps and 66 goals before his 100th cap, which was due against Finland at Wembley [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. In those 99 games he also had 17 assists [s:fa-kane-100-caps].

The goals came under two managers. Under Roy Hodgson he scored five in 16 games, and under Gareth Southgate he scored 61 in 81 [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. He has scored a record 25 goals in 34 games at Wembley for England [s:fa-kane-100-caps].

In qualifying he scored 17 goals in 14 World Cup qualifiers and 23 goals, with eight assists, in 21 EURO qualifiers [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. Against San Marino he scored four goals, the first England player to do so since Ian Wright in November 1993 [s:fa-kane-100-caps].

In March 2023 he scored against Italy to pass Wayne Rooney's 53 and become England's record goalscorer [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. Rooney's total came in 120 caps between 2003 and 2018 [s:tottenham-2022]. Earlier, in a Nations League match against Germany, a late equaliser made Kane only the second England player to reach 50 goals, moving him above Bobby Charlton's 49 [s:tottenham-2022]. He has faced 44 countries for England, most often Italy (six games) and Switzerland (five), and has scored or assisted against all but ten of them [s:fa-kane-100-caps].

## Major tournaments and hat-tricks

The FA counts 15 goals at major tournaments, the most in England's history, from 29 major-tournament appearances: 8 goals in 11 World Cup matches and 7 in 18 EURO finals matches [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. The 2026 FA profile raises his World Cup total to 14 and calls him England's leading men's World Cup scorer, ahead of Gary Lineker [s:fa-ballon-dor-nominees-2026].

He has five England hat-tricks, against Panama, Bulgaria, Montenegro, Albania and San Marino [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. The Bulgaria treble took him to 25 international goals and moved him above Geoff Hurst into England's top 15 all-time scorers [s:tottenham-2019].

At the 2018 World Cup England reached the semi-finals and Kane won the Golden Boot [s:tottenham-2022]. At Euro 2020 he scored four goals in three games to take England to the final [s:tottenham-2022]. He had been named England captain in 2017 [s:tottenham-2022]. The 2026 FA profile also records a record number of caps as England captain, passing Bobby Moore, and 125 senior caps, equalling Peter Shilton [s:fa-ballon-dor-nominees-2026].

## Contract talks at Bayern

Kane's deal expires at the end of the current season, and the extension talks aim to keep him at the club beyond 2027 [s:nst-bayern-extension-talks-2026].

On 29 August 2026 he confirmed that talks had started. “The first meeting started last week,” he said, adding that “there's no panic from either side” [s:nst-bayern-extension-talks-2026]. He also said “I'm sure we'll get to a point where both sides are happy” [s:nst-bayern-extension-talks-2026].

The sources used here confirm talks, not a signed deal. The most recent of them is dated 29 August 2026 and does not report an agreement [s:nst-bayern-extension-talks-2026].
