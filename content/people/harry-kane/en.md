## October 2026

Kane won the 2026 Ballon d'Or at the London Palladium on Monday 26 October, the first Englishman to win it in about 25 years [s:uefa-ballon-dor-ceremony-2026] [s:sky-kane-ballon-dor-win-2026]. He beat Kylian Mbappé and Lamine Yamal, and the ceremony began 45 minutes late because of traffic [s:sky-kane-ballon-dor-win-2026].

Bayern's assistant coach Aaron Danks said Kane was travelling from London to Munich to join training on Tuesday, and spoke of "the immense joy Harry experienced yesterday" [s:fox-kane-munich-return-2026]. Bayern had promised him a special welcome before the German Cup tie against 1. FC Magdeburg on Wednesday 28 October, and Danks said Kane would be available to play [s:fox-kane-munich-return-2026] [s:khelnow-kane-munich-welcome-2026]. The sources checked for this page do not report the result of that match. Bayern also promised to recognise the award when they face Borussia Dortmund on Saturday 31 October [s:fox-kane-munich-return-2026] [s:khelnow-kane-munich-welcome-2026].

## Bundesliga top scorer for a third year

Kane finished the 2025/26 Bundesliga season on 36 goals, including a hat-trick in a 5-1 win over Cologne [s:bundesliga-top-scorer-2026]. The league's report, dated 16 May 2026, says he became the first player to win the top scorer award in each of his first three seasons in the Bundesliga [s:bundesliga-top-scorer-2026]. It notes that Robert Lewandowski is the only other player to win the award three years in a row [s:bundesliga-top-scorer-2026].

The FA's shortlist profile lists 36 Bundesliga goals in 31 league appearances, 100 goals in 104 competitive appearances for Bayern, a European Golden Shoe and a third straight Bundesliga top-scorer award [s:fa-ballon-dor-nominees-2026]. ## From Tottenham to Bayern

Bayern Munich signed Kane from Tottenham Hotspur in August 2023 in a club-record deal, on a four-year contract [s:tnt-bayern-signing-2023]. TNT described Bayern as the German champions at the time [s:tnt-bayern-signing-2023]. TNT reported a potential fee of €120 million (£104m) [s:tnt-bayern-signing-2023]. The figure was described as potential, not as a confirmed price. TNT says it would have surpassed the €80 million (£68m) Bayern paid for Lucas Hernández from Atlético Madrid in 2019, their previous club record [s:tnt-bayern-signing-2023].

His Tottenham years had their own records. Tottenham's 2019 article put his England tally at 25 goals in 40 caps while he played for the club [s:tottenham-2019]. Its 2022 feature cited Jimmy Greaves' 266 goals for Spurs [s:tottenham-2022].

## England's record scorer

Kane made his England debut against Lithuania in March 2015 and scored 79 seconds after coming off the bench [s:tottenham-2022]. The FA's feature from September 2024 counted 99 caps and 66 goals before his 100th cap, which was due against Finland at Wembley [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. In those 99 games he also had 17 assists [s:fa-kane-100-caps].

The goals came under two managers. Under Roy Hodgson he scored five in 16 games, and under Gareth Southgate he scored 61 in 81 [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. He has scored 25 goals in 34 games at Wembley for England [s:fa-kane-100-caps].

In qualifying he scored 17 goals in 14 World Cup qualifiers and 23 goals, with eight assists, in 21 EURO qualifiers [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. Against San Marino he scored four goals, the first England player to do so since Ian Wright in November 1993 [s:fa-kane-100-caps].

In March 2023 he scored against Italy to pass Wayne Rooney's 53 and become England's record goalscorer [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. Rooney's total came in 120 caps between 2003 and 2018 [s:tottenham-2022]. Earlier, in a Nations League match against Germany, a late equaliser made Kane only the second England player to reach 50 goals, moving him above Bobby Charlton's 49 [s:tottenham-2022]. He has faced 44 countries for England, most often Italy (six games) and Switzerland (five), and has scored or assisted against all but ten of them [s:fa-kane-100-caps].

## Major tournaments and hat-tricks

The FA counts 15 goals at major tournaments, from 29 major-tournament appearances: 8 goals in 11 World Cup matches and 7 in 18 EURO finals matches [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. The 2026 FA profile raises his World Cup total to 14 and calls him England's leading men's World Cup scorer, ahead of Gary Lineker [s:fa-ballon-dor-nominees-2026].

He has five England hat-tricks [s:fa-kane-100-caps]. The Bulgaria treble took him to 25 international goals and moved him above Geoff Hurst into England's top 15 all-time scorers [s:tottenham-2019].

At the 2018 World Cup England reached the semi-finals and Kane won the Golden Boot [s:tottenham-2022]. At Euro 2020 he scored four goals in three games to take England to the final [s:tottenham-2022]. He had been named England captain in 2017 [s:tottenham-2022]. The 2026 FA profile also records a record number of caps as England captain, passing Bobby Moore, and 125 senior caps, equalling Peter Shilton [s:fa-ballon-dor-nominees-2026].

## The Bayern contract

Sky Sports reported on 16 October that Kane had agreed a new deal in principle with Bayern Munich until the summer of 2029, and that the club was finalising the paperwork on the extension [s:sky-kane-bayern-deal-2029]. The report added that a few things still needed to be ironed out before confirmation [s:sky-kane-bayern-deal-2029]. No official Bayern announcement of a signed extension was found for this page, so the deal is reported as agreed in principle, not as signed.

The talks came first. Kane confirmed them in a report dated 29 August 2026 [s:nst-bayern-extension-talks-2026]. The same day, Bayern's Jan-Christian Dreesen said the chemistry was "totally right" [s:bundesliga-kane-talks-de-2026]. “The first meeting started last week,” he said, adding that “there's no panic from either side” [s:nst-bayern-extension-talks-2026].
