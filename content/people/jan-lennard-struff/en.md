At 36, Jan-Lennard Struff reached his first Grand Slam quarter-final at Wimbledon in July 2026, the oldest man in the Open Era to do so [s:atp-wimbledon-2026-r4]. It was his 47th attempt at a major [s:tennismajors-wimbledon-qf-2026].

## A first title in Munich

Struff won the BMW Open in Munich on 21 April 2024, beating Taylor Fritz in the final for his first ATP Tour title [s:atp-munich-2024-title]. He was 33 at the time [s:atp-munich-2024-title]. A later ATP report describes that Munich run as a title on home soil [s:atp-brussels-semifinal-2026].

Before Brussels, his three earlier final defeats came at the Munich 250 in 2021, the Madrid Masters in 2023 and the Stuttgart 250 in 2023 [s:lavenir-brussels-final-2026].

## Wimbledon 2026: a comeback against Hurkacz

Hubert Hurkacz won the first two sets of their fourth-round match on 5 July 2026, 3-6 and 6-7(5) [s:atp-wimbledon-2026-r4]. Struff won the next two sets and led 4-2 in the fifth when Hurkacz retired [s:atp-wimbledon-2026-r4]. That gave him a quarter-final against Jannik Sinner [s:tennismajors-wimbledon-qf-2026], and the first major quarter-final of his career [s:atp-wimbledon-2026-r4].

## Brussels: a semi-final win and a lost final

On Saturday 24 October 2026, Struff beat top seed Arthur Fils 6-4, 6-7(7), 7-6(5) in the Brussels semi-final [s:atp-brussels-semifinal-2026]. He came back from a break down twice in the third set [s:atp-brussels-semifinal-2026]. ATP called him the first German man in the tournament's history to reach the final, and the win his third over a top-10 player that year [s:atp-brussels-semifinal-2026]. The same report calls it one of the biggest wins of his season [s:atp-brussels-semifinal-2026], and the final was his first ATP Tour final since the Munich title in 2024 [s:atp-brussels-semifinal-2026].

The final on 25 October went to Tallon Griekspoor, 4-6, 6-3, 6-4 after two hours and two minutes [s:lavenir-brussels-final-2026]. L'Avenir had Struff at No. 58 and Griekspoor at No. 60 before the match [s:lavenir-brussels-final-2026].

## Basel and the ranking

Struff arrived in Basel with his confidence renewed [s:atp-basel-preview-2026] and opened Swiss Indoors with a 6-4, 6-3 win over Yannick Hanfmann on Wednesday 28 October [s:atp-basel-preview-2026]. The ATP preview of that day lists him as World No. 47 and says he had won five of his past six tour-level matches [s:atp-basel-preview-2026]. His next opponent was Novak Djokovic, who leads their head-to-head 8-0 [s:atp-basel-preview-2026].

Reports disagree on his age. The ATP semi-final report calls him 34 [s:atp-brussels-semifinal-2026], while the ATP preview of 28 October gives 36 [s:atp-basel-preview-2026], which matches his birth date [s:wikidata-struff].
