Rafael Jódar started 2026 ranked No. 168 [s:atp-bio-es]. As of 27 October 2026, his ATP profile gives a career-high of No. 11, reached in August [s:atp-bio-es]. El Debate gives his birth year as 2006 and his hometown as Leganés, in the Madrid region [s:eldebate-junior-2024].

## A garage at home, then a club in Madrid

Jódar started playing tennis at four, in the garage of his family home [s:elespanol-garage-2026]. At six he joined the Club de Tenis Chamartín in Madrid. He remembers going to its hard courts with his father, at any hour, just the two of them [s:elespanol-garage-2026]. Football took up part of his childhood. He played it alongside tennis until he was 11, then chose tennis [s:elespanol-garage-2026].

In a June 2026 El Español interview, he said his father was his only coach [s:elespanol-garage-2026].

## The 2024 US Open junior title

In 2024 he won the US Open boys' singles at 17, beating Budkov Kjaer in a final decided by a tiebreak in the last set [s:atp-bio-en] [s:eldebate-junior-2024]. His junior ranking peaked at No. 4 [s:atp-bio-en]. He qualified for the 2025 Next Gen ATP Finals [s:atp-bio-es].

## College, then the pro tour

He played college tennis at the University of Virginia, where he was the 2025 ITA National Rookie of the Year and earned All-American honours [s:atp-bio-en].

He won Challenger titles at Hersonissos, Lincoln and Charlottesville [s:atp-bio-en]. That made him the third Spanish teenager to win three Challenger titles [s:atp-bio-en].

## Marrakech in April 2026

On 5 April 2026 Jódar won the Grand Prix Hassan II in Marrakech, a clay-court event [s:atp-marrakech-final-2026]. He beat Trungelliti 6-3, 6-2 in 69 minutes [s:atp-marrakech-final-2026]. Trungelliti was 36 and had come through qualifying. The ATP report calls the age gap between them the fifth-biggest in the Tour's history since 1990 [s:atp-marrakech-final-2026].

It was his first ATP Tour title [s:atp-bio-es]. He was 19 [s:atp-marrakech-final-2026]. The ATP report says he was the second man born in 2006 or later to win a Tour title, after João Fonseca [s:atp-marrakech-final-2026]. The win lifted him 32 places to a career-high No. 57 [s:atp-marrakech-final-2026]. Afterwards he said he had no words to describe the feeling [s:atp-marrakech-final-2026].

The ATP profile also says he became the sixth Spanish man to win a tour-level trophy before turning 20 [s:atp-bio-es].

## Australia, Roland Garros and Montreal

At the Australian Open he made his Grand Slam main-draw debut as a qualifier and won his first-round match [s:atp-bio-en]. His 2026 results on the ATP profile also include a semi-final in Barcelona and a quarter-final in Madrid [s:atp-bio-en].

At Roland Garros he was the No. 27 seed and reached his first Grand Slam quarter-final [s:atp-bio-en]. In Montreal he reached an ATP Masters 1000 semi-final, becoming the first man born in 2006 to do so [s:atp-bio-en].

## Washington and the final against Fritz

In August 2026 Jódar beat Tabilo 6-7(6), 6-4, 6-4 in the Washington semi-final, a match that lasted two hours and 23 minutes [s:atp-washington-semi-2026]. He had reached No. 19 after his quarter-final, which was his Top 20 debut [s:atp-washington-semi-2026]. The semi-final took him to No. 15, and to a final against third seed Taylor Fritz [s:atp-washington-semi-2026]. ATP called it the biggest final of his career [s:atp-washington-semi-2026].

He is the third Spanish man to reach the Washington final, after Alex Corretja in 2000 and Alejandro Davidovich Fokina in 2025 [s:atp-washington-semi-2026]. David Ferrer, now Spain's Davis Cup captain [s:atp-davis-cup-debut-2026], said on the Cadena SER programme El Larguero that Jódar reminded him very much of Nadal at 17, and that he was having a magnificent year [s:eldebate-ferrer-2026].

## Davis Cup debut in Santiago

Jódar made his Davis Cup debut in the play-off tie away to Chile, in Santiago. He was Spain's No. 1 for the tie [s:atp-davis-cup-debut-2026]. In the ATP preview he said things were moving very fast and that he was enjoying it [s:atp-davis-cup-debut-2026].

On 19 September he beat Cristian Garín in straight sets, after Spain had won the opening singles. That put Spain 2-0 up in the tie [s:puntodebreak-davis-cup-win-2026].
