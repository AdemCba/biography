Lewis Hall is a left-back [s:the-week-squad] for Newcastle United [s:nufc-profile] and has seven senior caps for England, with no goals [s:fa-squad]. He was born on 8 September 2004 in Slough, England [s:fa-squad], which makes him 22 as of 2 November 2026 [s:fa-squad].

## A season-long loan, then a permanent move

In August 2023 Newcastle took him on a season-long loan from Chelsea [s:nufc-profile]. He made his Newcastle debut in an 8-0 win at Sheffield United in September 2023 [s:pl-permanent-move], and made 22 appearances on loan [s:nufc-profile].

Newcastle announced in July 2024 that it had signed Hall permanently from Chelsea [s:pl-permanent-move] [s:nufc-profile].
## An injury-hit 2024-25 season

Hall started every Premier League game from September 2024 until a foot injury ruled him out for the rest of the season [s:pa-injury]. The injury was to a bone in his foot [s:pa-injury]. PA called him one of Newcastle's most dependable players that season [s:pa-injury].

## A new contract and the 2026 World Cup

Hall has reportedly agreed a new long-term contract to stay at Newcastle [s:beinsports-contract]. The same report puts his Newcastle record at 107 appearances, eight assists and three goals [s:beinsports-contract].

England named their 2026 World Cup squad in May 2026, and Hall was not in it [s:the-week-squad]. One commentary piece argued that leaving out left-back Lewis Hall could prove a bigger mistake than omitting any higher-profile player [s:the-week-squad].
