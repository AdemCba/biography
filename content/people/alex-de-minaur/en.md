Alex de Minaur beat the top seed in Vienna on Saturday 31 October 2026. He won the semi-final 6-4, 1-6, 7-6(4) against Alexander Zverev, the top seed [s:atp-vienna-2026]. ABC called Zverev the "soon-to-be world number one" in its report [s:abc-tennis]. Zverev won his first-round match in Vienna on 27 October, and a report dated 28 October said he was one win away from the top ranking [s:tennistourtalk-zverev]. That first-round match was 7-5, 3-6, 7-6(11) and lasted three hours and five minutes [s:tennistourtalk-zverev].

A sidebar on a Tennis Majors page dated 31 October 2026 carried the headline "No Alcaraz vs Zverev final in Vienna! De Minaur stuns the top seed and moves into a Turin place" [s:tennismajors-vienna].

The other semi-final went to Carlos Alcaraz, the second seed and a wild card, who beat Daniil Medvedev 6-4, 7-6(2) [s:atp-vienna-2026]. The ATP results page lists both as semi-final winners and lists no result for the final [s:atp-vienna-2026].

## Sydney, Alicante and a Uruguayan father

De Minaur was born in Sydney, Australia, and plays right-handed [s:rg-profile]. The ATP bio gives his father as Uruguayan and his mother as Spanish, and says he trains in Alicante, Spain [s:atp-bio]. Roland-Garros lists Adolfo Gutierrez and Matt Reid as his coaches [s:rg-profile]. The ATP bio says he was mentored by former world No. 1 Lleyton Hewitt [s:atp-bio].

## Rankings: No. 6 in 2024, No. 5 in 2026

He reached No. 6 in July 2024 [s:rg-profile]. The ATP bio gives his highest ranking as No. 5, reached in July 2026 [s:atp-bio]. He finished 2024 ranked No. 9 and 2025 ranked No. 7 [s:atp-bio].

He has won 11 ATP Tour titles and is a seven-time Grand Slam quarter-finalist [s:atp-bio]. In the 2024-25 season he became the first Australian to qualify for the Nitto ATP Finals in singles since Lleyton Hewitt in 2004 [s:atp-bio].

## Alcaraz and the head-to-head

Alcaraz beat de Minaur 7-5, 6-2, 6-1 in the Australian Open quarter-final on 27 January 2026. That win put Alcaraz's Lexus ATP Head2Head lead at 6-0 [s:atp-ao-2026-qf]. The ATP report says de Minaur had won only two sets in the rivalry since 2022 [s:atp-ao-2026-qf]. That quarter-final sent Alcaraz into his first semi-final at Melbourne Park [s:atp-ao-2026-qf].

## An earlier Vienna semi-final

In October 2025 Jannik Sinner beat de Minaur 6-3, 6-4 in the Vienna semi-final [s:tennismajors-vienna].

## The race to Turin

Before the Vienna event, Tennis Majors listed de Minaur 10th in the PIF ATP Live Race, first outside the cut, with 2,880 points [s:tennismajors-race-2026]. The article said a win would take him to 2,930 points, and within 254 points of Rafael Jodar, who was ninth on 3,184 [s:tennismajors-race-2026]. The same preview said he was due to play Learner Tien in Vienna that Wednesday, and that Tien was 8-9 against top-10 opponents [s:tennismajors-race-2026].

The sources used here do not report a final result, or his race position after the semi-final.
