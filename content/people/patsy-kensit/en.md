Patsy Kensit was born on 4 March 1968 and is an English actress and singer [s:entertainment-ie-profile]. In 2026 she returned to *Emmerdale* as Sadie King, a part she had left two decades earlier [s:rte-emmerdale-return].

## Sadie King in Emmerdale, 2004 to 2006

Kensit first played Sadie in 2004. The character arrived as the wife of Jimmy King, played by Nick Miles [s:rte-emmerdale-return][s:tvchoice-emmerdale-return]. Her storylines included a house explosion [s:rte-emmerdale-return]. Sadie's downfall came when Cain Dingle (Jeff Hordley) double-crossed her, stole the ransom money and left her behind [s:rte-emmerdale-return]. Kensit left the show in 2006 [s:tvchoice-emmerdale-return].

## Holby City, Tina and Bobby and later roles

In *Holby City* she played Faye Morton from 2007 to 2010, and the role came back in 2019 [s:tvchoice-emmerdale-return]. In 2017 she played Betty Dean in *Tina and Bobby* [s:tvchoice-emmerdale-return].

Her later credits include *Agatha Raisin* (2020), *McDonald & Dodds* (2021) and *Death in Paradise* (2023) [s:tvchoice-emmerdale-return]. She also appeared in *Never Mind the Buzzcocks* [s:tvchoice-emmerdale-return].

## EastEnders as Emma Harding

Kensit joined *EastEnders* for what was described as a brief stint. She played Emma Harding, the estranged mother of Lola Pearce, who had left when Lola was three. It was Emma's first appearance on the soap [s:hackney-gazette-eastenders]. Kensit said: "I'm so excited to be visiting Walford for this storyline." [s:hackney-gazette-eastenders]

## The 2026 return

Reports on 14 May 2026 said Kensit would return to the show as Sadie King later this year [s:rte-emmerdale-return][s:tvchoice-emmerdale-return]. Kensit said: "I am really excited to return to Emmerdale. I treasure the years I was here before." [s:rte-emmerdale-return] She also said: "Sadie King is a character that I really loved playing." [s:rte-emmerdale-return] She added: "I am so grateful for the chance to return to the show, it's absolutely amazing." [s:rte-emmerdale-return]

RTE describes Sadie as a villainous, scheming character [s:rte-emmerdale-return]. The show's producer, Laura Shaw, said the return "will be explosive and guaranteed to shock" [s:tvchoice-emmerdale-return].

## What Kensit has said about Sadie

In an interview published on 3 August 2026, Kensit said that accepting the role was "just a hard yes for me". She called Sadie "one of the favourite roles I've ever had" [s:yahoo-emmerdale-interview]. She said Sadie may have acquired new depths, and praised what Laura Shaw and the writers have done with the character [s:yahoo-emmerdale-interview].

The interview also names Kim Tate, played by Claire King, and says Sadie gets a more hostile reception from other characters [s:yahoo-emmerdale-interview].
