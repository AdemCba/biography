## From Munich to the first screen role

Elyas M'Barek was born in Munich on 29 May 1982 [s:filmportal-mbarek] [s:earlygame-mbarek-44]. He grew up as the son of an Austrian mother and a Tunisian father [s:earlygame-mbarek-44] [s:serienjunkies-mbarek-person]. He still holds Austrian citizenship [s:serienjunkies-mbarek-person]. In a 2013 interview with TV Movie he said he lived in Munich and was glad of the opportunities he had in Germany [s:tvmovie-interview-2013].

His screen debut came in 2001 with Mädchen, Mädchen [s:serienjunkies-mbarek-person] [s:filmportal-mbarek]. In 2008 he appeared in Die Welle [s:serienjunkies-mbarek-person].

## Türkisch für Anfänger and the breakthrough

He played Cem Öztürk in the television series Türkisch für Anfänger, [s:serienjunkies-mbarek-person]. Earlygame calls the series, rather than any single film, the real breakthrough of his career [s:earlygame-mbarek-44]. The cinema adaptation is listed for 2011/2012 [s:filmportal-mbarek].

## Fack ju Göhte and the years after

The role most often tied to his name is Zeki Müller, a teacher in Fack ju Göhte, released in 2013 [s:filmportal-mbarek] [s:earlygame-mbarek-44] [s:serienjunkies-mbarek-person]. Earlygame calls that film his definitive pop culture breakthrough [s:earlygame-mbarek-44]. Preparing for the part took a lot of work. In a TV Movie interview published on 7 November 2013 he described the preparation as hard work: for four months he trained with a coach every day and kept to a diet [s:tvmovie-interview-2013]. The same interview has him on the set of Der Medicus, where he said working with Sir Ben was great [s:tvmovie-interview-2013].

Asked about Hollywood in that interview, he said he would much rather make a sequel to one of his earlier comedies with Bora [s:tvmovie-interview-2013].

The franchise continued. Fack Ju Göhte 2 is listed for 2014/2015 and Fack Ju Göhte 3 for 2017 [s:filmportal-mbarek].

His other films in the same years include Der Fall Collini (2018/2019), in which he plays a young lawyer at the centre of a courtroom drama [s:earlygame-mbarek-44] [s:filmportal-mbarek], and Das perfekte Geheimnis (2019) [s:filmportal-mbarek]. Nightlife is listed for 2019/2020 [s:filmportal-mbarek].
