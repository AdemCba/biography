Teri Hatcher is {age} and, from 20 October 2026, she is playing Miranda Priestly in the West End musical *The Devil Wears Prada* at London's Dominion Theatre [s:whatsonstage-announce]. She was born on 8 December 1964, so her next birthday is in December [s:encyclopedia]. The part follows a career that runs from a cheerleading squad in San Francisco to a Golden Globe for *Desperate Housewives*.

## Born in the Bay Area

Encyclopedia.com gives her birthplace as Sunnyvale, California, and says she grew up in the San Francisco Bay Area [s:encyclopedia]. TV Guide lists Palo Alto instead, so the two sources disagree on the town [s:tvguide]. Her parents were Owen, an electrical engineer, and Esther, a computer programmer [s:encyclopedia]. Supanova's guest page says she studied ballet as a child [s:supanova].

At Fremont High School she captained the cheerleading squad [s:encyclopedia]. She then studied math at De Anza College, with plans to become a teacher, and took acting classes at the American Conservatory Theatre in San Francisco [s:encyclopedia] [s:tvguide]. In 1984 she joined the San Francisco 49ers cheerleaders, the Gold Rush [s:encyclopedia] [s:tvguide].

## The first screen jobs

Her screen debut came in 1985 as Amy in an episode of *The Love Boat* [s:tvguide]. She also had a recurring role as Penny Parker on *MacGyver* and guest parts in *Star Trek: The Next Generation*, *Quantum Leap* and *Mad About You* [s:supanova]. Film work in this period included *The Big Picture* (1989) and *Soapdish* (1991) [s:encyclopedia].

In 1993 she appeared in an episode of *Seinfeld* [s:encyclopedia].

## The break: Lois Lane

From 1993 to 1997 Hatcher played Lois Lane in *Lois & Clark: The New Adventures of Superman* [s:encyclopedia]. TV Guide records a Viewers for Quality Television Award nomination for the show in 1994 and a Golden Apple Award for Female Discovery of the Year in 1996 [s:tvguide]. She played Lois opposite Dean Cain [s:pinknews-prada].

## Film, Bond and the stage

She played Paris Carver in the James Bond film *Tomorrow Never Dies* (1997) [s:encyclopedia]. Earlier that decade she was in *Two Days in the Valley* (1996) [s:encyclopedia]. Supanova's guest page also lists *Spy Kids*, *Heaven's Prisoners*, *Coraline* and *Planes* among her films [s:supanova]. The publisher Hachette names *Spy Kids*, *Tomorrow Never Dies*, *Soapdish* and *Two Days in the Valley* in her author biography [s:hachette-burnt-toast].

On stage she toured in *Cabaret* in 1999 as Sally Bowles [s:encyclopedia]. West End Best Friend describes it as her musical theatre debut, in the national tour of Sam Mendes's revival, and adds that she was praised as Morticia in *The Addams Family* [s:westendbestfriend]. Time Out lists *Cabaret* and *The Addams Family* as her two US stage musicals [s:timeout-prada].

## Susan Mayer and the 2005 Golden Globe

From 2004 she played Susan Mayer in ABC's *Desperate Housewives*, which ran to 2012 [s:pinknews-prada]. In January 2005 she won the Golden Globe for best actress in a television series, musical or comedy [s:golden-globes]. She was nominated in the same category again in 2006 [s:golden-globes]. The Television Academy lists one Emmy nomination for her, for outstanding lead actress in a comedy series in 2005, and no win [s:emmys]. Hachette's author biography says she also won the Screen Actors Guild award for best actress in a comedy for the 2005 season [s:hachette-burnt-toast].

CBS News reported that in her acceptance speech she thanked ABC for giving her "a second chance at a career when I couldn't have been a bigger has-been" [s:cbs-burnt-toast]. The show was a hit in its first season, according to the same report [s:cbs-burnt-toast].

In 2006 she published *Burnt Toast: And Other Philosophies of Life*, a self-help book; Hachette's page for the audiobook edition gives the on-sale date as 2 May 2006 and describes the book as a personal, humorous book about everyday moments that shaped her outlook [s:hachette-burnt-toast]. CBS had reported a year earlier that she wanted the book to reach women she did not meet on the street [s:cbs-burnt-toast].

## Supergirl and later television

In March 2017 she appeared on *Supergirl* on The CW as Rhea, a new villain in the episode "Star-Crossed" that aired on 20 March 2017 [s:comicbookmovie-supergirl]. Supanova describes the character as Queen Rhea of Daxam and lists a guest role as Ella Lane in *Smallville* [s:supanova]. TV Guide records a Teen Choice Award nomination in 2017 in the Choice TV: Villain category [s:tvguide].

## Why she is in the news: Miranda Priestly

On 7 September 2026 the producers of *The Devil Wears Prada: The Musical* announced that Hatcher would take over as Miranda Priestly, editor-in-chief of Runway magazine, from Vanessa Williams, her former *Desperate Housewives* co-star [s:whatsonstage-announce] [s:pinknews-prada]. Williams played her final performance on 19 September 2026 [s:whatsonstage-announce]. Debbie Kurup covered the role until 17 October, and Hatcher began on 20 October 2026 [s:whatsonstage-announce].

Her run is scheduled to end on 6 February 2027 [s:whatsonstage-announce] [s:pinknews-prada]. The musical opened in the West End in December 2024 and is booking at the Dominion until 25 September 2027 [s:whatsonstage-announce]. Music is by Elton John and the book by Kate Wetherhead [s:whatsonstage-first-look]. WhatsOnStage published first-look coverage of Hatcher in the role on 29 October 2026 [s:whatsonstage-first-look].

Hatcher said she has played "some pretty formidable women" in her career, but "very few can compare to THE Miranda Priestly" [s:whatsonstage-announce]. Time Out quotes her describing Miranda as "intelligent, commanding, and has a glorious way of putting you in your place", and calls this her biggest stage role to date [s:timeout-prada]. In an interview with Hello! she called the part "a gift for me" [s:pinknews-prada].

Supanova lists the Australian convention dates of late 2026 and 2027 on her guest page, but it does not confirm that she will attend any of them [s:supanova].

## Family, marriages and relationships

She married Marcus Leithold, a fitness trainer, in 1988 and they divorced in 1989 [s:encyclopedia]. She married the actor Jon Tenney in 1994 and they divorced in 2003 [s:encyclopedia]. Her daughter is Emerson Rose [s:encyclopedia] [s:hachette-burnt-toast].

In a 2024 interview with Connecticut Public she said she had been removed from the dating app Hinge because staff thought she was an impersonator, a mix-up that was resolved [s:ctpublic]. She also said she was done with dating apps, content on her own, and had a cat [s:ctpublic].
