## Ransom Canyon

Duhamel's current signature role is Staten Kirkland, a stoic rancher in Netflix's Ransom Canyon. The series follows three ranching families who compete for land in the Texas Hill Country [s:thewrap-renewal]. Minka Kelly plays Quinn O'Grady, who owns the local dance hall. April Blair created the show and runs it, and Duhamel and Dan Angel are executive producers [s:thewrap-renewal].

Season 1 was added to Netflix on 17 April 2025 [s:wion-update]. By its fourth week it had 183.6 million hours viewed and had charted in 77 countries, according to What's on Netflix [s:wion-update]. Netflix confirmed the second season on 24 June 2025, with filming at Netflix's Albuquerque Studios [s:thewrap-renewal].

Season 2 arrived on 23 July 2026 with eight episodes, down from ten [s:wion-s2-date]. It picks up six months after season 1.
