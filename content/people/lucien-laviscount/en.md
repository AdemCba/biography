## A Burnley childhood with an Antiguan father

Laviscount was born in Burnley, Lancashire [s:tmdb]. GQ Middle East describes the town as post-industrial and working class, and says he grew up there as a person of colour in a predominantly white town [s:gq-middle-east-2025]. His father is from Antigua and his mother is English [s:gq-middle-east-2025]. The family spent time in Antigua when he was a child [s:gentlemans-journal-2024]. That answers the question many readers search for about his ethnicity and parents: a British actor of English and Antiguan parentage, raised in Lancashire.

He says his parents taught him that being different is a strength, and he calls it his "strongest strength" [s:gq-middle-east-2025]. His mother is a storyteller who read to him at bedtime, which he links to his interest in performing [s:gq-middle-east-2025] [s:gentlemans-journal-2024]. His parents now live with him in Antigua [s:gentlemans-journal-2024].

## Beckham and the acting school

The break came at about eleven, during a Marks & Spencer modelling campaign. Beckham suggested he think about acting, and according to Laviscount, Beckham had his assistant contact an acting school in Manchester [s:gq-middle-east-2025] [s:gentlemans-journal-2024]. He has since told Beckham "thank you, you changed my life" [s:gentlemans-journal-2024].

It moved fast. Within three months of starting classes he had booked his first television role, playing Sophie Okonedo's son in *Clocking Off* [s:gq-middle-east-2025]. TMDB's biography then lists *Grange Hill* in 2007 and *Coronation Street* in 2009 [s:tmdb]. He left *Coronation Street* part way through his contract to take a place in the BBC school drama *Waterloo Road* at 18 [s:gq-middle-east-2025]. There he played Jonah Kirby, a rebellious pupil, and appeared in all but one of the 20 episodes of series six [s:looper-alfie] [s:tmdb].

TMDB also records that he took part in *Celebrity Big Brother* in 2011, and that in 2012 he released a single, "Dance With You" [s:tmdb].

## Scream Queens and the move to the United States

The next step was across the Atlantic. For *Scream Queens* he sent a self-tape, then flew to Los Angeles and read for the producer Ryan Murphy on the set of *Glee* [s:gq-middle-east-2025]. Murphy first told him he was not right for the part, but then wrote him into the show anyway, according to GQ Middle East [s:gq-middle-east-2025]. He played Earl Grey, a British member of the Dickie Dollar Scholars fraternity, in the Fox series from 2015 [s:looper-alfie] [s:tmdb].

Two more series followed. In *Snatch*, the crime comedy-drama for Crackle, he played the boxer Billy Ayres, managed by Albert Hill (Luke Pasqualino) [s:looper-alfie]. Looper reports the role brought him a UK National Film Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor [s:looper-alfie]. In 2020 he was Alexander Cabot, a music executive, in the *Riverdale* spin-off *Katy Keene* [s:tmdb] [s:time-2021] [s:looper-alfie].

## Alfie in Emily in Paris

Alfie arrived in the second season, released on 22 December 2021 [s:time-2021]. Laviscount was 29 [s:time-2021]. He was in New York on other projects when his manager called about an audition for the creator Darren Star. He sent a tape within an hour, spoke on Zoom with Star and Lily Collins the next day, and was in Paris a few days later [s:time-2021]. Another interview puts the tape in early 2021 and says he was cast within an hour of the Zoom call and had to quarantine for ten days after arriving [s:gentlemans-journal-2024]. The two interviews disagree on how long the contract was meant to last. GQ Middle East says he expected only two episodes, while the Gentleman's Journal says four [s:gq-middle-east-2025] [s:gentlemans-journal-2024]. Either way, the part became a regular one.

Alfie is a British banker living in Paris, cynical about the city and wary of Emily at first, before the two become friends and then more [s:time-2021]. He plays in a football league with Gabriel, played by Lucas Bravo [s:time-2021]. Laviscount told TIME he grew up playing football and supports Arsenal, that he found French hard, and that he took online lessons afterwards [s:time-2021]. He had never been to Paris before he was cast, and said Paris "has my heart" [s:time-2021]. In 2025 he was set to return for season five [s:gq-middle-east-2025].
