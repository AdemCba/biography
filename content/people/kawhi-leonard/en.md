## Riverside, San Diego State and the draft

Leonard was born on 29 June 1991 in Los Angeles, California, the youngest of five children [s:nba-10-things]. He began playing basketball at Canyon Springs High School as a sophomore and finished at Riverside King, where he won California's "Mr. Basketball" award in his senior year [s:nba-10-things].

At San Diego State he led the Aztecs to back-to-back Mountain West tournament championships [s:nba-10-things]. He declared for the 2011 NBA Draft after a Sweet 16 run [s:nba-10-things]. Early in his NBA career he kept driving the Chevrolet Malibu from college. Friends eventually persuaded him to buy a Porsche, which he drove only on game days [s:nba-10-things].

On draft night the San Antonio Spurs traded for him, sending George Hill to the Indiana Pacers [s:nba-10-things]. The league's account says Gregg Popovich called that trade the toughest decision of his Spurs tenure [s:nba-10-things]. Leonard finished fourth in Rookie of the Year voting and made the 2012 All-Rookie First Team [s:nba-10-things].

## Work habits in San Antonio

In San Antonio, Leonard trained alongside Tim Duncan, Manu Ginobili and Tony Parker [s:nba-10-things]. Assistant coach Chip Engelland had him study the shooting forms of Kobe Bryant and Richard Jefferson [s:nba-10-things]. Engelland said Leonard had not been "tricked by any of the NBA glamour or bright lights, big city" [s:nba-10-things].
## Two titles with two teams

In 2014 the Spurs beat Miami 4-1 in the Finals. Leonard averaged 17.8 points on 61.2 percent shooting in that series [s:nba-10-things]. At 22 he became the youngest Finals MVP since Magic Johnson in 1980 [s:nba-10-things].

A re-aggravated ankle injury in Game 1 of the 2017 Western Conference finals caused him to miss the rest of that series. He also missed nine games in 2017-18 [s:nba-10-things]. After he requested a trade, San Antonio sent him to Toronto [s:nba-10-things]. He led the Raptors to their first NBA championship in 2019 [s:nba-10-things]. Leonard became one of three players, with LeBron James and Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, to win Finals MVP with two different teams [s:nba-10-things]. He also won Defensive Player of the Year twice [s:nba-10-things].

## Honours beyond the titles

Team USA's profile lists Leonard as a five-time NBA All-Star, selected in 2016-17 and 2019-21 [s:teamusa-profile]. He was a three-time All-NBA First Team pick (2016, 2017 and 2021) and a two-time All-NBA Second Team pick (2019 and 2020) [s:teamusa-profile]. The profile also places him on the NBA 75th Anniversary Team [s:teamusa-profile].

His high school career was decorated too. The Los Angeles Times named him Player of the Year in his senior season, 2008-09, and CalHiSports.com named him the 2009 Mr. Basketball state player of the year [s:teamusa-profile].
