Pamela Anderson was famous for Baywatch in the 1990s, and three decades later a small independent film put her acting in front of awards voters. The Last Showgirl, directed by Gia Coppola, brought her a Golden Globe nomination in December 2024, and she was 57 when it was announced [s:zoomer-globe]. She said she thought her career was over "a long time ago" [s:zoomer-globe].

## Ladysmith and the first job

Anderson was born on 1 July 1967 in Ladysmith, a small town on Vancouver Island in British Columbia [s:golden-globes]. Biography.com gives her full name as Pamela Denise Anderson and names her parents as Barry, a repairman, and Carol, a waitress. It adds that she has a younger brother, Gerry [s:biography-com]. The Canadian Encyclopedia says she attended high school in Comox, British Columbia [s:canadian-encyclopedia]. Biography.com dates her graduation from Highland Secondary School to 1985 [s:biography-com].

She moved to Vancouver in 1988 [s:canadian-encyclopedia]. A year later a television cameraman spotted her at a BC Lions football game, where she was wearing a Labatt's T-shirt. Labatt's offered her a contract, and an offer to pose for Playboy followed [s:canadian-encyclopedia]. Looper dates the Labatt's photos to 1989, when she was 22, and says they became a brand poster that inspired an advertisement [s:looper-career]. Her first Playboy cover came in October 1989, and she was Playmate of the Month for February 1990 [s:looper-career]. Biography.com counts 14 covers over her career, a record for the magazine [s:biography-com].

## From Home Improvement to Baywatch

Television came quickly. In 1991 she joined the sitcom Home Improvement as Lisa, the Tool Time Girl [s:biography-com] [s:canadian-encyclopedia]. Looper counts 23 episodes in seasons 1, 2 and 6 [s:looper-career].

In 1992 she was cast as the lifeguard C.J. Parker on Baywatch [s:looper-career]. She stayed until 1997, appearing in 110 episodes over six years, according to Looper [s:looper-career]. The Canadian Encyclopedia says the show was sold to 140 countries [s:canadian-encyclopedia]. Its schedule often clashed with Home Improvement, and Looper reports that this led to her leaving the sitcom [s:looper-career]. In 1995 Maclean's put her on its cover under the line "The Most Famous Canadian on the Planet?" [s:canadian-encyclopedia].

Her first lead in a feature film was Barb Wire in 1996, in which she played Barbara Kopetski [s:canadian-encyclopedia] [s:looper-career]. Looper puts the film's budget at $9 million and its takings at $3.79 million [s:looper-career]. The Canadian Encyclopedia calls it less of a box-office hit than anticipated [s:canadian-encyclopedia].

## After Baywatch

From 1998 to 2002 she starred as a private investigator in the series V.I.P. and also worked as its producer [s:canadian-encyclopedia]. Biography.com lists her as an executive producer of the show for four seasons [s:biography-com]. She voiced the lead in the animated series Stripperella in 2003 [s:biography-com], and she starred in the sitcom Stacked in 2005 and 2006 [s:canadian-encyclopedia]. Cameo roles followed in Scooby-Doo (2002), Scary Movie 3 (2003) and Borat (2006), where she appeared as herself [s:canadian-encyclopedia]. In 2006 she was inducted into Canada's Walk of Fame [s:canadian-encyclopedia].

She also wrote books. Biography.com lists the novels Star (2004) and Star Struck (2005) and the memoir Love, Pamela (2023) [s:biography-com]. In 2010 she competed in season 10 of Dancing with the Stars, and in 2017 she appeared in the Baywatch film reboot [s:biography-com]. The Canadian Encyclopedia notes her work with PETA, the animal rights group [s:canadian-encyclopedia].

## Broadway and the 2023 reset

On 12 April 2022 Anderson made her Broadway debut as Roxie Hart in the musical Chicago at the Ambassador Theatre, an eight-week limited engagement that ran through 5 June [s:playbill-chicago]. "Playing Roxie Hart is a dream fulfilled," she said [s:playbill-chicago]. Afterwards she told CBS News: "I felt rooted for, which was a different feeling than I've felt before" [s:cbs-ladysmith].

In January 2023 she published the memoir Love, Pamela through Dey Street Books, released alongside the Netflix documentary Pamela, A Love Story [s:cbs-ladysmith]. Biography.com dates the documentary to 2023 [s:biography-com]. It also reports that her cookbook I Love You: Recipes from the Heart, published in 2024, was nominated for a James Beard Award in the Visuals category [s:biography-com].

## The Last Showgirl

In The Last Showgirl she plays Shelley, a veteran dancer whose Las Vegas show closes after a 30-year run [s:zoomer-globe]. Gia Coppola directed the film, and it premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival [s:swissinfo-zff]. On 17 September 2024 the Zurich Film Festival announced that it would honour Anderson for her career and for the role, with a screening on 4 October [s:swissinfo-zff]. Festival director Christian Jungen said she "embodied the role of Shelley with heart and soul" [s:swissinfo-zff]. The Golden Globes site records that she received the festival's Golden Eye Award [s:golden-globes].

On 9 December 2024 she was nominated for the Golden Globe for Best Performance by a Female Actor in a Motion Picture, Drama, her first Golden Globe nomination [s:zoomer-globe] [s:golden-globes]. She was not among the best actress nominees for the Academy Awards, announced on 23 January 2025 [s:gma-oscars]. Her reaction, in an Elle interview, was that an Oscar nomination was "not something I ever expected", and she added: "Doing the work is the win" [s:gma-oscars].

In April 2024 Paramount confirmed her casting in the reboot of The Naked Gun opposite Liam Neeson, directed by Akiva Schaffer [s:abc-naked-gun]. The Golden Globes page lists the film as a 2025 release [s:golden-globes], and Biography.com says she played Beth Davenport [s:biography-com].

## Relationships

Outlets count her marriages differently. Biography.com says she has been married five times to four men [s:biography-com]. Fox News calls Dan Hayhurst her fifth husband and sixth marriage, because it counts Rick Salomon twice [s:fox-marriages]. NBC calls the Hayhurst marriage her fifth [s:nbc-hayhurst].

- Tommy Lee: married in 1995 and divorced in 1998 [s:biography-com]. Fox News reports that the wedding took place four days after they met [s:fox-marriages].
- Kid Rock: married in July 2006, and she filed for divorce later that year [s:biography-com]. Fox News mentions a 2003 date in one place and 2006 to 2007 in another [s:fox-marriages].
- Rick Salomon: married in Las Vegas in 2007, with an annulment requested in early 2008. They remarried in 2014 and divorced in 2015 [s:nbc-hayhurst].
- Jon Peters: a secret ceremony on 20 January 2020, and she announced the split 12 days later [s:fox-marriages]. Biography.com says she later stated they were never legally married [s:biography-com].
- Dan Hayhurst: married on Christmas Eve 2020 at her home on Vancouver Island [s:nbc-hayhurst]. Her representative confirmed the split to People in January 2022 [s:nbc-hayhurst].

She has two sons with Tommy Lee, Brandon Thomas and Dylan Jagger [s:biography-com]. She has said of motherhood: "I was a mother; that saved me" [s:cbs-ladysmith].

## What she does now

Anderson lives in Ladysmith [s:cbs-ladysmith]. She sold her Malibu home and moved back to Canada during the pandemic, and told Better Homes & Gardens: "I was not in a good space when I moved back to Canada" [s:bhg-ladysmith]. She renovated her grandmother's waterfront house and the land around it for the HGTV Canada series Pamela's Garden of Eden [s:today-hgtv]. She described building the garden as "a metaphor of putting my life back together" [s:bhg-ladysmith]. Asked about photographers, she said: "I feel very protected here" [s:cbs-ladysmith].
