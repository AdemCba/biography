## From Monaco to Paris

Mbappé made his debut for Monaco as a teenager and became the club’s youngest-ever first-team player and youngest goalscorer [s:planetsport-mbappe]. In his second season, 2016-17, he scored 26 goals in 44 matches in all competitions. Monaco reached a Champions League semi-final that season and won their first Ligue 1 title in 17 years [s:planetsport-mbappe]. He also won his first UNFP Ligue 1 Young Player of the Year award [s:planetsport-mbappe].

After a year on loan, Paris Saint-Germain signed him permanently. The article describes the fee as an estimated £200 million, and says the loan was reportedly used to avoid Financial Fair Play complications [s:planetsport-mbappe].

## Seasons in Paris

In 2017-18, his first season at the club, PSG won Ligue 1 and he scored 21 goals in 44 appearances [s:planetsport-mbappe]. In 2018-19 he was the league’s top scorer with 33 goals, won the UNFP Player of the Year award and the inaugural Kopa Trophy, and PSG won the Ligue 1 title again [s:planetsport-mbappe].

In March 2019 a goal against Caen was his 50th for PSG, scored under coach Unai Emery [s:planetsport-mbappe]. He also scored a hat-trick against Metz, making him the youngest player to do so in Ligue 1 since Jérémy Ménez in 2005 [s:planetsport-mbappe]. He also scored four goals in 13 minutes against Lyon, the youngest player to score four in a Ligue 1 match [s:planetsport-mbappe].

In 2019-20 PSG won the Trophée des Champions and went on to another treble [s:planetsport-mbappe]. The Champions League final that season was a 1-0 defeat to Bayern Munich [s:planetsport-mbappe]. In 2020-21 he scored twice against Istanbul Başakşehir in the Champions League, reaching 20 goals in the competition and becoming the youngest player to do so [s:planetsport-mbappe]. By January 2022 he had still not won the Champions League, which the article describes as the main medal missing from his collection [s:planetsport-mbappe].

He later told PSG he would not extend his contract and would leave when it expired. “I wanted to announce to you all that it’s my last year (season) with Paris Saint-Germain,” he said [s:nbc-psg-departure].

## Two World Cup finals

France beat Croatia 4-2 in Moscow in July 2018 [s:fader-final-2018]. Mbappé scored in the final at 19, and he became only the second teenager to score in a World Cup final [s:dailystar-pele-2018]. The first was Pelé, who scored in Brazil’s 1958 final win over Sweden [s:dailystar-pele-2018]. Pelé welcomed him to the club on Twitter, saying it was great to have some company [s:dailystar-pele-2018].

The 2018 final was played in Moscow, and the report is dated 15 July 2018 [s:fader-final-2018]. His international rise started earlier. France won the 2016 UEFA European Under-19 Championship, with Mbappé scoring five goals, and he calls it his first international competition [s:uefa-under19-2016].

The 2022 final was played at Lusail Stadium on 18 December 2022 and ended 3-3 after extra time [s:nbcboston-final-2022]. Mbappé scored a hat-trick: two goals in the 80th and 81st minutes, and a penalty in the 118th minute of extra time [s:nbcboston-final-2022].
