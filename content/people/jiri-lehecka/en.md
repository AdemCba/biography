Jiri Lehecka is a Czech tennis player with two ATP Tour titles, won in Adelaide in January 2024 and Brisbane in January 2025 [s:atp-adelaide-2024-final] [s:atp-brisbane-2025-final]. His career high in the ATP singles rankings is No. 12, reached in May 2026 [s:atp-bio]. That peak followed his first Masters 1000 final, in Miami that March [s:atp-bio] [s:atp-miami-2026-sf].

## Early years in Mladá Boleslav and Prostějov

According to his ATP biography, Lehecka started playing tennis with his mother and grandfather [s:atp-bio]. He is a native of Mladá Boleslav, a town in the Czech Republic, as the Czech news agency ČTK reported in a match report [s:cns-profile]. At 15 he left home to train in Prostějov [s:atp-bio]. The ATP player page also describes him as a former world No. 10 junior [s:atp-wikidata-bio]. His biography also lists a junior honour: the 2019 Wimbledon boys' doubles title, won with his countryman Forejtek [s:atp-bio].

## Adelaide 2024: the first title

Lehecka was seeded seventh at the Adelaide International, a hard-court ATP 250 event, in January 2024 [s:atp-adelaide-2024-final]. In the final he beat Jack Draper 4-6, 6-4, 6-3 in two hours and eight minutes [s:atp-adelaide-2024-final]. It was his maiden ATP Tour title, and it was set to lift him to a new career high of No. 23 [s:atp-adelaide-2024-final].

## Brisbane 2025 and the Opelka final

Brisbane was his second title, and the final ended early. Lehecka led Reilly Opelka 4-1 in the first set when Opelka was forced to stop with a back injury [s:atp-brisbane-2025-final]. The ATP report says Lehecka rose four spots to No. 24 in the PIF ATP Live Rankings after the win [s:atp-brisbane-2025-final].

Opelka said: 'Unfortunately, my back has been giving me issues' [s:atp-brisbane-2025-final].

## Majors and a ranking timeline

At the 2023 Australian Open he beat sixth seed Felix Auger-Aliassime 4-6, 6-3, 7-6(2), 7-6(3) in the round of 16, which took him into the quarter-finals [s:tennismajors-ao-2023]. His ATP biography lists quarter-final finishes at that event and at the US Open in 2025 [s:atp-bio]. In New York, he beat Adrian Mannarino 7-6(4), 6-4, 2-6, 6-2 in three hours and 14 minutes, reaching his first US Open quarter-final [s:tennismajors-usopen-2025].

The ranking moves are easy to follow in the sources. He was set to reach No. 23 after Adelaide [s:atp-adelaide-2024-final], and was No. 24 after Brisbane a year later [s:atp-brisbane-2025-final]. He broke into the top 20 in September 2025 [s:atp-bio]. In Miami in March 2026 he was No. 14 in the PIF ATP Live Rankings [s:atp-miami-2026-sf], and the career high in his biography is No. 12, set in May 2026 [s:atp-bio].

## Miami 2026 and the first Masters 1000 final

Lehecka was the 21st seed at the Miami Open [s:atp-miami-2026-sf]. In the semi-final he beat Arthur Fils 6-2, 6-2 in 75 minutes, which the ATP report calls his maiden Masters 1000 final [s:atp-miami-2026-sf]. The report adds that a title would have taken him to No. 12 [s:atp-miami-2026-sf]. His biography dates the career high to May 2026, soon after that Miami final [s:atp-bio].
