Cam Gigandet spent most of the 2000s being hired to be the problem. Born in Washington state, he played the fighter Ryan McCarthy in *Never Back Down* and then the tracker vampire James in *Twilight*, both in 2008, and the two roles brought him a shared MTV Movie Award in each of those years [s:bww-mtv-2008] [s:movieweb-mtv-2009]. In 2026 he is 44 and still working steadily, mostly in action and thriller films [s:tmdb-gigandet].

## Early life in Washington

Cam Joslin Gigandet was born on 16 August 1982 in Tacoma, Washington [s:tmdb-gigandet] [s:martincid-gigandet]. TV Overmind says he is the first of two children and that his father is a business owner who co-owns a chain of pizza restaurants [s:tvovermind-gigandet]. Martin Cid Magazine describes it slightly differently: his father helped build a pizza franchise, and the family was oriented almost entirely around sports [s:martincid-gigandet]. As a boy he was expected to become an athlete, and he went to Auburn Senior High School [s:tvovermind-gigandet].

In 2001 he moved to California for college [s:tvovermind-gigandet]. Martin Cid Magazine adds that after high school he went to Los Angeles and took an acting class on impulse, which changed his direction [s:martincid-gigandet]. TV Overmind says his parents supported him emotionally and financially after the move [s:tvovermind-gigandet]. We found no interview in which Gigandet himself tells this part of the story, so the details come from profiles.

## How he got his break

His first professional credit was a guest role as Mark Young in an episode of *CSI: Crime Scene Investigation* in 2003 [s:tmdb-gigandet] [s:martincid-gigandet]. The next step was television drama. He played Kevin Volchok, a recurring antagonist in the later seasons of *The O.C.* [s:martincid-gigandet]. TMDB's biography says he won the part in 2006, while its credit list dates the role to 2005, so the exact year is not settled [s:tmdb-gigandet].

Film came in 2008. In *Never Back Down* he played Ryan McCarthy, the antagonist, and trained down to a very low body-fat percentage for the part [s:martincid-gigandet]. The film's lead fight, Sean Faris against Gigandet, won Best Fight at the MTV Movie Awards held in Los Angeles on 1 June 2008 [s:bww-mtv-2008] [s:rt-mtv-2008]. TMDB also lists a "One to Watch" award from the 2008 Young Hollywood Awards [s:tmdb-gigandet].

## Twilight and James

In a December 2008 interview with Movies.ie, then aged 26, Gigandet said he had read *Twilight* while still filming *Never Back Down*: "My girlfriend gave me her copy and kept saying, 'You just have to read it'" [s:movies-ie-interview]. The film came from the same producers, and he heard from the studio about six months later [s:movies-ie-interview]. The interviewer notes he had first been cast as Edward's brother Emmett Cullen but ended up as James. Gigandet's answer was direct: "from the first time I read the book, I knew I wanted to be James" [s:movies-ie-interview].

The shoot was short for him. The film was shot over about ten weeks, but he worked only the first three, so his scenes were packed into a tight window [s:movies-ie-interview]. He called Catherine Hardwicke, the director, tough but energetic [s:movies-ie-interview]. The role was physical: he had never done wire work, and one of his first days was the ballet-studio fight, which he described as being thrown in the deep end. *Never Back Down*, he said, was good preparation, but *Twilight* had to be taken to the next level [s:movies-ie-interview].

The part earned him a second Best Fight award, shared with Robert Pattinson, at the 2009 MTV Movie Awards [s:movieweb-mtv-2009]. He returned only as archive footage in *The Twilight Saga: New Moon* in 2009 [s:tmdb-gigandet]. In the same Movies.ie interview he also mentioned shooting *The Unborn* with Odette Yustman and Gary Oldman, and *Pandorum* in Berlin, his first trip to Europe, with scenes alongside Dennis Quaid [s:movies-ie-interview].

## Relationships

Gigandet married the actress Dominique Geisendorff, described by Entertainment Tonight as his wife of 13 years when she filed for divorce [s:et-divorce-2022]. Entertainment Tonight and In Touch both give the wedding as November 2008 [s:et-divorce-2022] [s:yahoo-intouch-settlement]. A February 2013 CBS News report still called her his fiancée, so the sources do not line up neatly on the wedding date [s:cbs-rekker-2013]. Martin Cid Magazine says the marriage lasted sixteen years, which does not match the 13 years the other two report [s:martincid-gigandet].

She filed in Los Angeles Superior Court in August 2022, citing irreconcilable differences and giving 1 May as the date of separation, and asked for joint legal and physical custody [s:et-divorce-2022]. According to court documents reported by In Touch in July 2024, the court approved a settlement and declared them single on 11 July 2024, with joint legal and physical custody of the children [s:yahoo-intouch-settlement].

The couple have three children: Everleigh, Rekker and Armie [s:et-divorce-2022] [s:yahoo-intouch-settlement]. They were 13, 9 and 6 in August 2022 [s:et-divorce-2022]. CBS News reported Rekker's birth, as the couple's second child, in February 2013, and said the actor's older daughter was then three [s:cbs-rekker-2013].

Gigandet's current relationship is with the actress Hannah James. Us Weekly reports that he revealed it in January 2023 and announced in September 2024 that they were expecting a child [s:yahoo-usweekly-custody]. Their son, Dare Wylder James Gigandet, was shown in a photo James posted on 2 January [s:yahoo-usweekly-custody]. Martin Cid Magazine places the birth in October 2024 and counts him as Gigandet's fourth child [s:martincid-gigandet].

In September 2025 Us Weekly, citing court filings, reported that his ex-wife said they had shared custody 50/50 until he moved to Texas on 2 May. A Los Angeles judge ordered him on 27 August 2025 to pay $4,626 a month in temporary child support, and Gigandet joined that hearing by video. The report did not include a response from him [s:yahoo-usweekly-custody]. Martin Cid Magazine also says he has moved to Texas [s:martincid-gigandet].
