## From a German contest to Sports Illustrated

She began modeling at 18 after winning a contest in Germany [s:biography-klum]. In 1992 she won Model ’92, a German television contest on Thomas Gottschalk’s talk show, according to Abendzeitung München [s:abendzeitung-gntm].

In 1998 she appeared on the cover of the Sports Illustrated swimsuit issue [s:biography-klum]. In 2001 she moved to the big screen in the hairdressing comedy Blow Dry, and later roles included Ella Enchanted (2004) [s:biography-klum].

## Project Runway and the host’s chair

Project Runway premiered in December 2004 and soon became a hit [s:biography-klum]. Abendzeitung reports that Klum was one of eleven producers, and that she worked as host and head judge [s:abendzeitung-gntm]. She hosted the show for 16 seasons [s:biography-klum]. ## Germany’s Next Topmodel and the years after

Not long after Project Runway launched, Klum began hosting Germany’s Next Topmodel [s:biography-klum]. The German version started on ProSieben in 2006, and Klum presented it [s:abendzeitung-gntm]. In 2019 she launched another German show, Queen of Drags, a search for the most talented drag queen [s:abendzeitung-gntm].

In March 2013 it was announced that Klum would join America’s Got Talent as a judge [s:biography-klum]. She left after six seasons [s:biography-klum]. She later returned to the judges’ table for the spin-off America’s Got Talent: The Champions [s:abendzeitung-gntm].
