## Four Album of the Year wins

At the Grammy Awards on February 4, 2024, Taylor Swift won Album of the Year for *Midnights*. It was her fourth win in that category, the first time any artist had reached four [s:etonline-midnights]. The same night she also won Best Pop Vocal Album for *Midnights* [s:etonline-midnights].

## Time's Person of the Year

TIME named her its 2023 Person of the Year. The choice was announced live on TODAY on December 6, 2023 [s:nbc-time-poy]. Editor-in-chief Sam Jacobs wrote that "Taylor Swift found a way to transcend borders and be a source of light," and that "No one else on the planet today can move so many people so well" [s:nbc-time-poy].

## The Eras Tour

Pollstar's estimate, published on December 11, 2023, put the Eras Tour above $1 billion. It was the first tour to pass that mark. Pollstar counted 4.3 million tickets sold at an average price of $238.95 [s:intix-pollstar-2023]. When the tour ended in December 2024, Pollstar estimated its total gross at $2.2 billion, based on data from 149 shows [s:kctv5-pollstar-2024]. Both figures are Pollstar estimates.

## The Life of a Showgirl

The album sold 4.002 million units in its first week, according to Luminate data reported by Billboard. That is the most first-week sales since Luminate began collecting figures in 1991, and it passed Adele's *25*, which debuted with 3.482 million units [s:today-showgirl-2025]. Her official website now promotes *The Life of a Showgirl* [s:taylorswift-official].

## Where the fortune comes from

Forbes lists Swift's net worth at $2 billion, as a real-time figure for October 26, 2026 [s:forbes-profile]. Its breakdown, from an update dated June 3, 2026, gives royalties and touring nearly $800 million, her music catalog an estimated $600 million, and real estate about $110 million [s:forbes-profile]. These are Forbes estimates, not reported earnings.
