He was born on 27 November 1976 [s:rotten-tomatoes] [s:tmdb], which makes him 49 on 4 November 2026. Most viewers know him as the nerdy neighbour Steve Urkel on *Family Matters*. In 2026 he hosts the syndicated game show *Flip Side* and the HGTV renovation contest *Totally '90s House* [s:paramount-flip-side] [s:tvinsider-90s-house].

## Early life and first jobs

His full name is Jaleel Ahmad White, and TMDB describes him as an American actor and screenwriter [s:tmdb]. Rotten Tomatoes, TMDB and TV Guide all give his birthplace as Pasadena, California [s:rotten-tomatoes] [s:tmdb] [s:tvguide-bio]. The Game Show Network says he grew up in Pasadena [s:gsn-flip-side]. Wikipedia names Culver City instead, so a reader will see both. We follow the three sources that agree and flag the conflict here.

He started young. TV Guide says he began in commercials at age three, and that he then appeared on *Charlie and Company* and *The Jeffersons* [s:tvguide-bio]. The same profile lists two Young Artist Award nominations from that period, in 1985 and 1986 [s:tvguide-bio]. He was 12 when he landed Steve Urkel [s:dailybeast-urkel]. Looking back on those years in an interview with The Daily Beast, White said his parents were "always right there" and described a "hover mom" [s:dailybeast-urkel]. He credits that family presence for keeping him grounded, and he says he never stopped working afterwards [s:dailybeast-urkel]. The only other detail we found about his parents comes from TV Guide, which says the glasses he wore as Urkel were borrowed from his father to help him find the character [s:tvguide-bio].

## The break: Steve Urkel

In 1989 White was cast as Steve Urkel on *Family Matters*, in what was meant to be a single guest appearance [s:tvguide-bio]. The part grew. Game Show Network says he starred in the sitcom for nine seasons after being cast in 1989 [s:gsn-flip-side], and Rotten Tomatoes dates the series as 1989 to 1998 [s:rotten-tomatoes]. Rotten Tomatoes also credits the role with three Image Awards [s:rotten-tomatoes].

TV Guide lists the recognition in more detail. He won NAACP Image Awards in 1990, 1991, 1993, 1994 and 1995, and a Young Artist Award for Outstanding Young Comedian in a Television Series in 1991 [s:tvguide-bio]. In 1997 he won an NAACP Image Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series [s:tvguide-bio]. A TV Land Award nomination followed in 2004 [s:tvguide-bio]. The sources differ slightly on how many Image Awards he has (Rotten Tomatoes says three, TV Guide lists six wins across the years), so we give both counts rather than pick one.

## Sonic and the years after Urkel

In the 1990s White also voiced Sonic the Hedgehog in three animated series, according to TV Guide [s:tvguide-bio]. TMDB lists *Adventures of Sonic the Hedgehog*, *Sonic Underground* and *Sonic the Hedgehog* among the titles he is known for [s:tmdb], and Rotten Tomatoes credits him with the Sonic voice in a 2013 title called *Sonic* [s:rotten-tomatoes]. Game Show Network counts two Sonic television series in his résumé, which is another place where sources disagree on the exact number [s:gsn-flip-side].

When *Family Matters* ended, work got harder. Rotten Tomatoes says he finished college and tried a sitcom comeback with *Grown Ups*, which ran from 1999 to 2000 [s:rotten-tomatoes]. TV Guide says he graduated from UCLA in 2001 with a degree in film and television [s:tvguide-bio]. He also voiced a character in the animated film *Quest for Camelot* in 1998 [s:rotten-tomatoes].

He rebuilt with smaller parts. Rotten Tomatoes points to a cameo in *Dreamgirls* in 2006 [s:rotten-tomatoes], and TMDB lists *Mega Shark vs. Crocosaurus* among the titles he is known for [s:tmdb]. In 2010 he wrote, executive produced and starred in the series *Fake It Til You Make It* [s:tvguide-bio]. Game Show Network adds guest roles on *Boston Legal*, *House*, *Castle* and *Psych* [s:gsn-flip-side].

## Game shows and the host's chair

White's second career grew out of television games. He hosted the Syfy series *Total Blackout* [s:rotten-tomatoes], and Game Show Network says he hosted two seasons of it [s:gsn-flip-side]. UPI notes that he also appeared on *Family Feud* and competed on *Dancing with the Stars* [s:upi-flip-side]. According to Game Show Network, he was a recurring celebrity guest on *25 Words or Less* and *Pictionary* and holds the record for most bonus-round points on *Celebrity Family Feud* [s:gsn-flip-side]. Cassius quotes him saying he had always really wanted to be the host of a game show [s:cassius-flip-side].

That chance arrived in January 2024, when CBS Media Ventures announced *The Flip Side* with White as host [s:upi-flip-side] [s:yahoo-flip-side]. He called himself the "ringleader" of the show [s:yahoo-flip-side]. The format comes from Keller/Noll, the producers behind *Chopped* and *America Says*, and CBS Media Ventures and Game Show Enterprises Studios share the cost and the distribution windows [s:nexttv-flip-side]. CBS's Wendy McMahon said the project had been in development for over a year [s:nexttv-flip-side].

The show, now titled *Flip Side*, premiered in national syndication on 9 September 2024 and enters its third season on 14 September 2026 [s:paramount-flip-side]. Two teams guess how two different groups of people feel about the same issue, and the winners play for $10,000 [s:paramount-flip-side]. White is also an executive producer [s:paramount-flip-side]. The distributor describes it as syndication's fastest-growing game show, a claim that comes from the company's own press page [s:paramount-flip-side]. GSN repeats the episodes on weeknights at 5 p.m. ET [s:gsn-flip-side]. In April 2025 he talked about the show on CBS Mornings Plus [s:cbs-news-flip-side].

## Marriage and family

White married Nicoletta Ruhl on 4 May 2024 at the Riviera Country Club in Los Angeles, in front of about 175 guests [s:et-wedding] [s:yahoo-ruhl]. Entertainment Tonight described it as his first marriage and said he was 47 [s:et-wedding]. Ruhl is a sports technology executive who formerly worked in digital media for the Los Angeles Chargers [s:yahoo-ruhl]. White has a daughter from an earlier relationship, and Yahoo says that in a 2019 Fox News interview he called her his biggest motivation [s:yahoo-ruhl]. Game Show Network says he lives in Los Angeles with his wife and daughter [s:gsn-flip-side]. We leave out further details about her.

## What he does now

In 2026 White hosts *Totally '90s House*, an HGTV competition in which stars of 1990s television split into two teams to find and renovate a house from that decade [s:tvinsider-90s-house]. It premiered on Wednesday 26 August and runs six episodes, with $25,000 for the winning team's chosen charity [s:tvinsider-90s-house]. Introducing the show, White said "everybody loves the '90s again" [s:tvinsider-90s-house]. Alongside it, *Flip Side* continues, and he has said that after Urkel he faced rejection but never stopped working [s:dailybeast-urkel].
