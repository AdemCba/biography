# Semrush research, 2026-11-03

One-off snapshot from a Semrush trial. Names to write went to data/seo/evergreen.md.

## What competitors earn traffic with (US)

- famousbirthdays.com: 640K/mo visits land on the home page; person pages win on bare names and
  "<name> age" / "how old is <name>" (Taylor Swift, Ronaldo, Shakira, Tom Holland, Justin Bieber).
  Big earners: reality TV (Love Island cast), fitness and lifestyle creators (Sara Saffari, Stephanie
  Buttermore), streamers (Duke Dennis, Jynxzi), mid-level actors (Scott Glenn, Lauren Graham, Josh Duhamel).
  Much of the rest is minors and adult-content names, which we never cover.
- celebritynetworth.com: almost all traffic is "<name> net worth" plus bare names of older actors
  (Christopher Lloyd 246K, Randy Quaid, Teri Hatcher, Patrick Renna, Kim Fields, Sam Neill).
  Lesson: every page needs a sourced net-worth/earnings answer when reliable figures exist.
